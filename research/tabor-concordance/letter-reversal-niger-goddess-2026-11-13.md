## Evidence and distinctions
1. Acts 13:1 (KJV) says "Simeon that was called Niger." Greek Νίγερ is a name/epithet; the verse does not identify a goddess.
2. Latin *niger* means black/dark. English racial terminology developed through later European languages and spellings; the doubled g is not an alteration to the biblical name.
3. Greek boustrophedon inscriptions demonstrate changing writing direction and sometimes reversed glyphs. This does not prove Latin R derives from Cyrillic Я.
4. Latin R has an ancestry through Phoenician resh, Greek rho, western Greek/Old Italic and Latin. Modern Cyrillic Я developed through historical Cyrillic forms including little yus (ѧ); do not conflate visual resemblance with descent.
5. Isis and Egyptian Black Land imagery, and Roman Dea Africa, are distinct research leads; no primary evidence yet establishes a goddess named Niger as the origin of the English slur.
6. Southern English y'all comes from you all; Russian Я means I; Yahweh is associated with the Hebrew divine name YHWH. Similar sound is not proof of common etymology.
