## Redstone, NASA Marshall, and the Tabor family

**March 1942.** Opal Phillips Tabor began work at Redstone Arsenal. Her obituary describes issuing ration stamps and having a gas mask at her desk because mustard gas was manufactured nearby. The Army chronology records the first production of mustard gas at Huntsville Arsenal in April 1942. Opal later set up the top-floor conference room in Building 4200 at NASA Marshall. Her obituary describes a 62-year working life that included other employers. The Army rocket group transferred to NASA in 1960, and von Braun and his staff occupied Building 4200 in 1963. [Opal's obituary](https://obits.al.com/us/obituaries/huntsville/name/opal-tabor-obituary?id=13587085) · [Army chronology](https://history.redstone.army.mil/ihist-1942.html) · [NASA Building 4200 history](https://www.nasa.gov/centers-and-facilities/marshall/marshall-space-flight-centers-building-4200/) · [NASA biography of von Braun](https://www.nasa.gov/people/wernher-von-braun/)

**Granddad Pop.** According to the family account, John Ferrell Tabor Sr. met Opal at Redstone and went on to teach and coach in Madison County. The Huntsville History Collection documents his work with Cecil Fain on the girls' softball program. The Society for American Baseball Research identifies Ferrell as Jim “Rawhide” Tabor's brother. In the family account, Pop used the Bible to point out where people were being smart or stupid. Grandma Opal's instruction was “play dumb until you know what you are dealing with.” [Huntsville History Collection](https://huntsvillehistorycollection.org/hhc/browse-person.php?a=person&f=&id=88) · [Jim Tabor biography](https://sabr.org/bioproj/person/jim-tabor/)

## Monroe's patents and Army training

- **1970–1975.** US3884218A describes varying the amplitude of familiar sound using a signal modeled on a sleep EEG. [Patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/US3884218A/en)
- **1982–1984.** A July 1983 Army memo reports CENTER LANE personnel attending Monroe Institute Discovery training in March 1982 and Gateway training in July 1982. Lt. Col. Wayne M. McDonnell wrote his Army Gateway assessment in June 1983. A separate March 1984 memo addresses continuation of advanced Monroe Institute training. The Institute still offers Gateway Voyage. [CENTER LANE memo](https://www.neoarv.com/stargate/cia-rdp96-00788r001700210007-5/read) · [Army Gateway assessment](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-D101-PURL-gpo152943/pdf/GOVPUB-D101-PURL-gpo152943.pdf) · [Continuation memo](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP96-00788R001700210002-0.pdf) · [Gateway Voyage](https://www.monroeinstitute.org/products/gateway-voyage)
- **1990–1994.** US5213562A describes an EEG-derived pattern on stereo carrier waves. Claim 4 proposes using one person's EEG to induce the associated state in another person. US5356368A describes combining EEG recordings and includes an apparatus claim responsive to ambient noise. A 2023 systematic review found mixed results across EEG studies of binaural-beat entrainment. The patent grants establish the claims. Reliable transfer of a specified state calls for independent evidence. [US5213562A](https://patents.google.com/patent/US5213562A/en) · [US5356368A](https://patents.google.com/patent/US5356368A/en) · [Systematic review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37205669/)

## KJV, cherubim, and gargoyles

> lufifer aka satan is elohim aka theos the God of this world 2 corinthains 4:4 - the other satan / lucifer - so there are 2 satans 2 lucifers and 2 devils

In 2 Corinthians 4:4, the KJV says the god of this world has blinded the minds of unbelievers. The Greek noun *theos* occurs in both “the god of this world” and “the image of God.” Forms of *noēma* are rendered “devices” in 2 Corinthians 2:11, where Paul names Satan, and “minds” in 4:4 and 11:3. Comparing the verse with the patents brings the question of intent and influence into view. The KJV passage is a theological source and the patents describe proposed audio methods. [2 Corinthians 4:3–6 (KJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2%20Corinthians%204%3A3-6&version=KJV) · [Strong's G3540](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g3540/kjv/tr/) · [Greek text analysis](https://biblehub.com/text/2_corinthians/4-4.htm)

Genesis 3:24 places cherubim and a flaming sword east of Eden. Ezekiel 28 addresses the king of Tyrus as the covering cherub and describes his fall. Isaiah 14 uses “Lucifer” in a passage addressed to the king of Babylon. The association comes from reading across those passages. Renaissance painters also used putti, a motif from classical art. In architecture a gargoyle carries runoff away from the wall. Historic England also documents a tradition of attributing protective symbolism to gargoyles and grotesques. Rouen's dragon legend is recorded centuries after Saint Romanus lived.
