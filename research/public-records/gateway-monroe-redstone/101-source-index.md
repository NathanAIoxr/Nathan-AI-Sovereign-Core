## How to read the index

The 101 entries are a claim-by-claim documentary index. Archive IDs identify distinct release records. A packet may include pages also released elsewhere. Mirror copies of the same identifiable item are linked as access copies and counted once. The search covered public holdings and related published sources. Surviving public releases contain redactions and omissions. The index records what each source establishes, where it reports a proposal or author conclusion, and where results differ.
