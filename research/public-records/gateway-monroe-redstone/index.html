Redstone, NASA Marshall, and the Tabor family

March 1942. Opal Phillips Tabor began work at Redstone Arsenal. Her obituary describes issuing ration stamps and having a gas mask at her desk because mustard gas was manufactured nearby. The Army chronology records the first production of mustard gas at Huntsville Arsenal in April 1942. Opal later set up the top-floor conference room in Building 4200 at NASA Marshall. Her obituary describes a 62-year working life that included other employers. The Army rocket group transferred to NASA in 1960, and von Braun and his staff occupied Building 4200 in 1963. Opal's obituary · Army chronology · NASA Building 4200 history · NASA biography of von Braun

Granddad Pop. According to the family account, John Ferrell Tabor Sr. met Opal at Redstone and went on to teach and coach in Madison County. The Huntsville History Collection documents his work with Cecil Fain on the girls' softball program. The Society for American Baseball Research identifies Ferrell as Jim “Rawhide” Tabor's brother. In the family account, Pop used the Bible to point out where people were being smart or stupid. Grandma Opal's instruction was “play dumb until you know what you are dealing with.” Huntsville History Collection · Jim Tabor biography

What the record establishes

The Army used Monroe training. The July 19, 1983 CENTER LANE memo identifies Discovery training in March 1982 and Gateway training in July 1982. McDonnell’s June 9, 1983 assessment is a separate Army document. The August briefing and March 1984 records document continued involvement. The workbook and Hemi-Sync bulletin were authored by the Monroe Institute and preserved in the government collection. Source 005 · Source 001 · Source 024 · Source 008 · Source 002 · Source 003

The present-day Institute program has its own history. The Institute now offers Gateway Voyage. The DIA’s SUN STREAK procedures and the CIA’s 1995 commissioned evaluation concern the government’s remote-viewing program. California uses GATE for Gifted and Talented Education. The dated records identify the institutions responsible for each program. VICE reported obtaining a copy of the missing page 25 from the Institute archives in 2021. The sources reviewed leave the reason for the omission in the circulating government scan unresolved. Source 030 · Source 031 · Source 018 · Source 028 · Source 033 · Source 032

MKULTRA has its own released record. The records include the 1963 inspector general report, the 1977 Senate hearing, and the Subproject 119 document set. A 1975 inspector general memo documents the destruction-of-records issue. Subproject 119 includes a proposal to review the literature and scientific work on human bioelectric signals. That scope is the claim traceable to the file. Source 050 · Source 055 · Source 049 · Source 052

The patent and EEG evidence answer different questions. Claim 4 of US5213562A expressly describes trying to induce in one person a state associated with another person’s EEG. US5356368A includes a separate ambient-noise-responsive apparatus claim. The 2023 EEG review reports mixed results across studies of binaural-beat entrainment. The cited studies measure specific endpoints, such as EEG power, phase, coherence, mood, or sleep, and those measures carry different meanings. The Malech and Carter–Russell patents have their own inventors and claims. Source 059 · Source 060 · Source 068 · Source 061 · Source 062
