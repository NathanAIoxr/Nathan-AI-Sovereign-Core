The GODTABOR story universe

In an invented council beyond any single throne, characters argue about what power should mean. The light is not a ruler; it is the question every ruler must answer: who has the freedom to choose, who bears the consequences, and who protects people from harm?

Original fiction may borrow mythic language while keeping its own characters, rules, and continuity separate from religion, history and evidence.
