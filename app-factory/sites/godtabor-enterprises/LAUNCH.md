## 1. LITERAL — proposed real-world operating structure

GODTABOR ENTERPRISES is a proposed umbrella identity for Nathan Tabor's independent existing software prototypes, research, creative works and proposed future ventures. This umbrella is an **index and brand proposal**, not an automatic transfer of copyrights, ownership or legal control to any entity.

- **Shared (only with approval):** public discovery, indexing metadata, provenance links, accessibility standards, build/release gates and project relationships.
- **Independent by default:** source repositories or folders, original creative titles, datasets, customer records, user permissions, payment accounts, business formation, privacy rules, budget, and release schedules.
- **Public catalog:** read-only, source-based, non-sensitive metadata in [catalog.json](./catalog.json), with separate project IDs, classes, statuses, links and descriptions.
- **Private operations:** require a separately designed authenticated service and per-project permission checks. The public JSON is *not* a database service, synchronizer or automatic AI worker launcher.
- **One hub does not imply one legal entity.** Formal subsidiaries, DBAs, licensing arrangements and equity ownership are decisions requiring documents and authorized human action.

### Essential legal and commercial gates

1. Choose actual registration jurisdiction and structure. If North Carolina is selected, use the [North Carolina Secretary of State business registration guide](https://www.sosnc.gov/manual/launching_a_business/register_your_business).
2. Perform an interactive government business-name search, plus federal/state trademark and broader marketplace clearance, including similar spellings and sounds. A web search alone is **not** clearance.
3. Confirm creator ownership/licensing rights for product names, characters, artwork, music, underlying source and potentially overlapping brands.
4. Submit real legal filings through the jurisdiction's official services. Select registered agent and governance with full owner authorization. No filing is claimed here.
5. Obtain tax ID, finance, bookkeeping, payment, contracts, consumer policy, privacy notice, and insurance as applicable. None is marked complete in this record.
6. Verify domains, hosting, production data isolation, authentication and account recovery before publishing customer functions.
7. Require explicit human approval for financial charges, public announcements, irreversible releases, private data access and third-party actions.

## 2. FIGURATIVE — narrative and meaning

The enterprise can use the conceptual triad:

- **Literal:** what can actually be built, registered, measured, delivered and independently verified.
- **Figurative:** what a word, mirror, symbol, choice or metaphor can mean.
- **Mythological:** what an original fictional world can imagine without claiming evidence for its fictional events.

A metaphor about being a god expresses authorship, freedom or responsibility in a creative context; a software app cannot literally make a user omnipotent or enslave deities. The creator's original titles and formulations should be preserved separately from descriptive or explanatory copy.

## 4. Architecture for separate databases and one directory

Proposed non-destructive relationship model:

```text
GODTABOR PUBLIC DIRECTORY (catalog.json)
    |
    +--- project_id → independent project/source
    +--- external verified URL / approved local route
    +--- stage + evidence + owner-approved relationship
    |
    +--- FUTURE PRIVATE ROOT INDEX (not deployed)
              |
              +--- per-project permissions
              +--- approved cross-project asset references
              +--- non-public integration connectors
              +--- verified per-project records and backups
```

Each project remains separately addressable. No direct access is assumed to private databases, documents, linked accounts or software agents. A master catalog may point to records without copying them or merging unrelated data.

### Catalog schema

The [catalog.json](./catalog.json) file defines `schema`, brand, creator, launch state, relationship model, explicit `status_terms` and an array of `projects`. Every project has a stable `id`, creator-visible `name`, `realm`, `kind`, `status`, `description` and an optional verified public `href`.
