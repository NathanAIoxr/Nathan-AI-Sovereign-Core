## Story grammar

1. **Originality:** Draw on tone/genres without copying plots, scripts, distinctive scenes or characters from *The Godfather* or *Django Unchained*.
2. **Creative latitude:** Adult dark comedy, myth, controversial questions about religion and government, and non-instructional fictional conflict are in scope.
3. **Protection of innocence:** Any themes of exploitation are handled without sexualizing children or glamorizing real harm.
4. **Reality boundary:** Separate real-world people, documents and alleged acts from wholly fictional characters and invented events; avoid unsupported claims about named people.
5. **Exact author text:** Preserve spelling, capitalization, profanity, rhetorical devices and origin labels when archiving the creator's own writing.
6. **Provenance:** Each record should be labeled `AUTHOR_VERBATIM`, `EDITORIAL_PROPOSAL`, `FICTION`, `VERIFIED_SOURCE`, `UNVERIFIED_CLAIM`, or `PRODUCT_STATUS`. These are not interchangeable.
7. **One center, independent workspaces:** Screen, book, game, app, mythology and source archives can stay separate while the GODTABOR universe index connects them.
8. **No unearned deployment claims:** Publishing the site does not create external accounts, payments, apps, films or supernatural mechanisms.
