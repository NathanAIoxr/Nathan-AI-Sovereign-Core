    The Council of Gods — a decision lab

    Propose a change, identify who benefits and who might be harmed, and decide whether it deserves approval, amendment, or a veto. The Council is a creative governance simulation, not a court or real governing body.

    Review questions

    1. Does it protect innocence and consent?
    2. Are claims backed by evidence?
    3. Can people contest unfair decisions?
    4. Who has power, and who checks it?
    5. Could it cause harm or enable retaliation?

    Vetoes apply to proposals in this local simulation. No real-world orders are issued.

    If someone is in immediate danger

    Contact local emergency services (911 in the United States). Prioritize the person’s immediate safety.

    Report suspected exploitation

    In the U.S., contact the NCMEC CyberTipline for suspected online child sexual exploitation. For offline abuse, contact local child-protection authorities or law enforcement.

    Support survivors

    RAINN provides sexual-assault support and help finding services. Do not pressure victims to share their stories publicly.

    Evidence and due process

    Record non-sensitive incident details privately for authorized investigators. Do not upload child abuse material, confront an alleged offender, dox anyone, or treat an unverified accusation as proof.
