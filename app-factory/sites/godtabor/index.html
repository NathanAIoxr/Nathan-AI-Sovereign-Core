In the speculative mythology, the Council of Gods summons claimants to power. Tabor Light is a fictional symbol of revealed truth. The story world distinguishes a being's claim to divinity from evidence of its acts.

The creative boundary

GODTABOR can confront fictional religious and political institutions, include adult satire and question authority. Fiction does not turn accusations against real individuals into established facts. Any real-world reporting should be sourced and separated from fictional characters.

The public launch represents a website and fictional franchise blueprint—not a completed feature film, series, published book, commercial app, active game, registered trademark, production deal or fundraising operation.
