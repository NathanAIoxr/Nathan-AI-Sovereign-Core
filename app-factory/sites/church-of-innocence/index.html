THE RECLAIMED FOUNDING PURPOSE

Care comes before reputation.

The Church of Innocence exists to protect innocence, stand with the vulnerable, confront exploitation and deception, and turn faith into compassionate action. Its working mission calls for comfort, safety, acceptance, practical help and honest accountability.

Author declarations: “INNOCENCE IS NOT A COMMODITY.” · “YES IS YES — NO IS NO.” · “RISE AND SERVE.”

The mission is documented in writings and concept materials. A public site does not establish a registered religious organization, an active rescue team, legal authority, donation processing or an emergency service.

FIVE OPERATING PILLARS

What action means

01

PROTECT

Take threats to children and vulnerable people seriously, with safety and lawful assistance first.

02

RESTORE

Support recovery through compassion, appropriate care and practical resources.

03

TEACH

Teach boundaries, consent, honest faith, respectful conduct and ethical responsibility.

04

ACCOUNT

Reject concealment and retaliation. Refer suspected crimes to the proper authorities, preserve due process and protect those who report harm.

05

SERVE

Connect people with legitimate food, shelter, essential supplies, trusted services and follow-through.

Action cycle: NOTICE → PROTECT → PROVIDE → PREVENT → VERIFY → REPEAT.
