1 · Import your original creations

Import a ChatGPT export ZIP, a prepared Factory asset pack, or multiple individual files from your iPhone Files app. Reimport whenever you work on new material; the Vault skips identical files. This is the local prototype of each creator’s ongoing media library.

In ChatGPT: Settings → Data Controls → Export Data. Confirm the request. OpenAI sends a download link when the export is ready. Save the ZIP in your iPhone Files app, return here and import it. You can also import the smaller Factory asset pack directly. The ChatGPT export may not include every generated image; you can separately import images from your Files or ChatGPT Library.

Official ChatGPT export instructions

Private storage: Your imported files are saved in this browser's IndexedDB on this device. They are not sent to GitHub, ChatGPT, or an outside server by this importer. If Safari clears website data, they may disappear—keep your source ZIP and separate backups. Large imports require adequate iPhone storage, memory, and preferably Wi-Fi for obtaining the export. ZIP importing needs browser support for raw Deflate decompression; the app will explain if that isn't available.
