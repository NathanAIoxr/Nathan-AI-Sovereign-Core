How to play

Tap an adjacent square to move, or choose a command before tapping. Release clears an adjacent iron gate. A ward makes an adjacent hazard safe. Protect innocence by getting every Spark to the sanctuary before your turns run out. Clearing a gate alone earns zero. Walking straight onto a hazard ends the attempt.

Play the rescue, then use Show Unseen to inspect your route and the evidence of what you protected.

1+1=2 · BUILD records the rescue after every Spark reaches sanctuary. 1-1=0 · CLEAR resets this attempt. Use arrow keys on the board, R for Release, W for Ward, or M for Move.
