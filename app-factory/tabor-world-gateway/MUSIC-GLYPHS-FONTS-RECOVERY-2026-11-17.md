## Music: two separate original structures

**54-pulse phrase sequence**: 10, 8, 6, 5, 4, 10, 9, 2; total 54. Five black-note pitch classes F#, G#, A#, C#, D#. Phrase pairs F#/C#, G#/D#, A#/F#, C#/G#, D#/A#, F#/C#, G#/D#, A#/F#.

**19-position original piano study**: -9 through -1 (South / Inferno), 0 (silent equator), +1 through +9 (North / Babel). 18 sounding 4/4 bars, one silent bar, 96 BPM. Each sounding bar uses the user-defined STOMP, STOMP, CLAP, REST pattern. The source is `the_19_secret_chords_piano_key_map.txt`. It explicitly calls the work an original ancient-inspired composition, **not** a recovered melody from King David. No non-public-domain songs are played.

**Research branches**: Proverbs-like spoken cadence, heartbeat imagery, 13- and 17-year cicada cycles, rhythm, speech, phonetics and game touch input. Symbolic relationships are not evidence of historical musical equivalence.

## Tablet of Tabor and script separation

Original source: `Pasted markdown(20261009-213738).md`. Contains Babel Glyph / Chaos Script, Sovereign Protocols, glyph sequences, and Antikythera-inspired creative mechanical and cipher analogies. Its Egyptian signs are historical Unicode characters but the modern symbolic interpretations are not verified translations of ancient inscriptions.

Historical source: Unicode Standard chapter 11, Egyptian Hieroglyphs U+13000–U+1342F, with separate format controls and Extended-A block. See https://www.unicode.org/versions/Unicode18.0.0/core-spec/chapter-11/.

Other independent ciphers include Ankh-Æ Binder, BabelDissolver, Christmas Cipher Matrix / Zorro Font 52-glyph weeks, and 101 Master Matrix Reversals. They are indexed as crossovers, not silently absorbed into the Tablet.

## Typography Vault — critical source discrepancy

The detailed software specification `Pasted markdown(3).md` lists **33 core fonts** plus **222 bonus** and describes **169 mythological designs within the 222**. That implies **255 unique planned positions**, if the 169 is nested.

The promotional poster `Historical Typography Revival Vault.png` advertises **33 + 222 + 169 = 424**. These cannot both be the unique-item total without clarifying whether the 169 are duplicated or an extra pack. The live app preserves this contradiction as an open inventory reconciliation task.

**33 named core specifications recovered**: Barker 1611, Sinaiticus Uncial, Voynich Cipher, Gutenberg 42, Qumran Ink, Lindisfarne Insular, Babylon Wedge, Graf Orlok, Lang Metropolis, Caligari Intertitle, Vintage Ghoul, Bouncy Cel, Rosetta Glyphs, Maya Bark, Norse Stone, Harappa Proto, Aztec Sun, Weber Atlantis, Barbou Monotype, Pynson Drawer, Berthold Venetia, Vogue Intervogue, Privateer Log, Matlack Scribe, Apothecary Cure, Transit Stamp, Parisian Button, Supply Crate, Lexia Read, Draftsman Line, Trace Arrow, CRT Glitch, Signature Engine.

No complete, legally cleared, installable TTF/OTF package was verified in this pass. The preview cards deliberately use system fallback fonts; they are **not** original generated font binaries. Rights and source verification remain necessary.
