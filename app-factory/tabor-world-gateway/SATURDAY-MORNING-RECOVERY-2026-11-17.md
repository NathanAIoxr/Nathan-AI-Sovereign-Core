2. **The Door and the Wilderness**: 5 The school that taught sideways; 6 The useful almost; 7 The river that would not sit still; 8 Feet on both banks.
3. **The Crown and the Kitchen Table**: 9 The three crowns; 10 The photograph with the missing woman; 11 The banquet with no head; 12 The crown’s actual job.
4. **The Question and the Quiet**: 13 A town full of applause; 14 The room beside the room; 15 The song with an empty space; 16 The quiet that stayed.
5. **The Watchman and the Window**: 17 The cloud with a zipper; 18 The people behind the weather; 19 Director Voss offers peace; 20 The broadcast that belonged to everyone.
6. **The Table and the Small Republic**: 21 A very large guest; 22 The repair company; 23 A cup of help and a plate of love; 24 The table with two heights.
7. **The Letter and the Open Door**: 25 What Ellen actually said; 26 The message market; 27 The machine that remembered a hand; 28 The last door has company.
8. **The Twinkling and the Beginning**: 29 The world on the other side; 30 Seven permissions and one person; 31 The choice without a guarantee; 32 The first unedited morning.

It also contains an epilogue **A place at the table**, five reader questions, shared activities and an adaptation note. This is not a claim that the earlier Bible-based eight-book project has eight complete independent novels.
