How glitter is made

Reflective film is prepared, coated with a reflective layer such as aluminum, colored or optically treated, and precision-cut into particles. Other kinds of shimmer use mineral pigments or edible ingredients. These materials are not interchangeable.

Explore reflection, particle shape, light direction, static electricity, microplastics, and alternatives in the digital simulator.
