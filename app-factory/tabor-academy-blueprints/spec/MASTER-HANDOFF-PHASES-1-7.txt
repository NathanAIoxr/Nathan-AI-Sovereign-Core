### 3. The 8-Layer Universal Blueprint Model
1. **Surface Labels:** Different names humans give the items *(e.g., Rabbit Hind Leg vs. Cat Hind Leg)*.
2. **Core Parts & Structure:** Shared physical/structural pieces *(e.g., Femur, tibia, fibula, ankle/hock, foot bones)*.
3. **Functionality & Operations:** What the system actually does in motion *(e.g., Hinged Z-lever propulsion)*.
4. **Characteristics, Attributes, & Traits:** Shared behaviors, tension, load-bearing, and properties.
5. **Scale & Medium:** How size changes physics (Square-Cube Law) and what medium it operates in (air, water, dirt, vacuum, wire, code).
6. **Fuel, Input, & Exhaust:** What powers it -> how it converts -> what exits as output/waste.
7. **Direction, Flow, & Reversibility:** Forward vs. reverse operation *(Motor <-> Generator; Fridge <-> Heat Pump)* OR Lifecycle/State phase *(Ice <-> Water <-> Steam = H₂O)*.
8. **The Modification or Exemption + Observer's Blind Spot:** The exact single tweak (joint lock, grooved hinge, proportion stretch, material swap) that adapts the blueprint, plus why separate industries gave the same force different names *(e.g., friction, resistance, drag, viscosity)*.
