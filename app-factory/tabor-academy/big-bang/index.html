Say it plainly

You promised to call someone and forgot. They ask why you didn’t call. Which response tells the truth and makes room for repair?

“You’re making too much of it.”

That judges their reaction. The promise and the missed call still need an answer.

“I forgot. I said I’d call and I didn’t. Can we find a time to talk?”

This names what happened, takes responsibility, and offers repair. The other person gets to decide whether to talk.

“Let’s just forget it happened.”

That asks them to move on while leaving the missed promise unaddressed.
