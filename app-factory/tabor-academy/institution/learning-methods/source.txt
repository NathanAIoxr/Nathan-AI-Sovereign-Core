What Else This Unlocks: The 5-Channel Stimulation Loop

To turn any of these 101 activities into a complete Tabor Academy lesson, run it through five channels at once:
1. Sensory Hook (Stimulate): Touch, smell, hear, or see the physical object first before reading a single word about it.
2. Pattern Decode (Analyze): Strip away labels and ask: What is it made of? How does it work? What happens if I reverse it?
3. Physical Application (Build/Test): Use your hands to build, repair, cook, map, or test it in real life.
4. Moral & Root Check (Discern): Ask: Does this protect life and truth, or is it a con?
5. Teach Back (Mastery): Explain or demonstrate what you just did to someone else in plain, simple words.
