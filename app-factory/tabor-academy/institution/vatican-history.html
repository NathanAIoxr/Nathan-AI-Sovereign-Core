1666 · Read the law and check whom it applies to

The English Cestui Que Vie Act. Section I addresses particular property disputes where an estate or lease depended on another person’s life and that person was absent for seven years without proof of life. It directs a verdict on that basis in the relevant action. Section IV provides for recovery and profits with interest if the person presumed dead proves to be alive. The rule is limited to the property proceedings it describes. The source identifies an English statute under Charles II and supplies no declaration of universal death or maritime jurisdiction.
