# STL provenance and two distinct source sets

**Public new demo files:** `models/matrix_1.stl` through `matrix_5.stl` are newly generated **plain** approximately 70 × 45 × 5 mm educational plates with complementary tongue/recess shapes. Their authoring process checked each triangle edge occurred exactly twice, supporting closed-manifold mesh topology; this alone cannot prove fit, machine compatibility, slicer acceptance or strength. The 2-D plan includes 10 mm long tongues, 10.35 mm recesses, 15 mm tongues and 15.4 mm openings, nominally 0.35 mm depth and 0.2 mm per-side width clearance. Actual printed tolerances vary.

**Editable model:** `five_into_one.scad` includes raised labels and may generate a different mesh from the simplified, plain public STL sample; it is **not byte-identical** to those STLs.

**Previously supplied source set:** The five original STL models from `Nathan_Tabor_Five_Into_ONE_3D_Print_Shop_v1.zip` and `Nathan_Tabor_Five_Tiles_STL_Ready_v1.zip` are preserved, byte-for-byte, in the private 2026-11-05 recovery bundle with hashes. Public file names are similar, but the new demo STLs are **not substitutes for, or claimed originals of, those files**.

**Print scope:** No toolpaths or physical print were generated/executed in GitHub. All models are nonfunctional classroom-scale connector pieces. Inspect and test a small sample on an appropriately safeguarded machine before printing the rest.
