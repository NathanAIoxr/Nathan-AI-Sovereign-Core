## Working features (November 5, 2026)

- **66 indexed records:** 19 external terminology, 15 Nathan AI blueprints, 15 humanity principles, and 17 mythology/world-building nodes.
- **DATA ATAD:** Unicode-aware forward/reverse/round-trip inspection. Symmetric strings are not necessarily factual. Reversal cannot prove deception.
- **MIDA:** Distinct claim, source, interpretation and human review fields. Binary 1 = reviewed with source, 0 = withhold from use. Zero does not mean false, and one is not a scientific proof.
- **ASR:** Whitespace-only compaction preview, with original and result, bytes, ratio, and lossy warning. Not cryptographic or physical spectral reduction.
- **JAS:** Non-retaliatory boundaries: decline coercion, pause, document, revoke and seek help. No force or retaliation.
- **Humanity:** Practical checks for empathy, sympathy, compassion, honest uncertainty, and privacy, without pretending software feels emotions.
- **Sovereignty:** In-page permission grant/revocation demo; localStorage opt-in; user export/import JSON and wipe; no remote integrations.
- **Offline:** No external scripts, server APIs, remote assets, or network connections by design.

## Limits and legal reality

Sovereign-user design prioritizes revocable permissions, data portability, local execution, and informed choice; it does not put a business outside applicable U.S. or international jurisdiction. An open-weight model is not automatically permissionless or free of license restrictions.

Browser localStorage is not encrypted. Never enter passwords, keys, seed phrases, medical records, donor details or children's personal information in this demo. User-entered source references do not verify claims. No authentication, payment, diagnostics, external service authorization or actual medical service is present.
