The five-stage method

SPINE (carrier) → ARMS (observed gesture) → COUNT (measured marks) → ROOT (attested sound or meaning) → CONTEXT (source and proposed Taborite interpretation).

Ogham provides the teaching pattern, not a universal automatic translator. Unknown signs are preserved; reversible educational ciphers are not presented as secure cryptography.
