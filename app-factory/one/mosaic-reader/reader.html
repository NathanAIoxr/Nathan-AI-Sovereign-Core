Transcription vs. interpretation

Transcription copies marks. Transliteration represents a script with another writing system. Translation requires linguistic evidence. Interpretation proposes a meaning in context.

Ogham master pattern · four aicmí × five positions, plus supplementary signs. Read the actual Unicode symbols, their geometric coordinates, and the proposed conceptual layer separately. The names/values of some later forfeda differ between manuscripts.

The four primary groups are ordered by their traditional 1–5 stroke positions. A supplementary ᚚ Peith also exists in Unicode; it is included separately so it is not lost.

Reading direction is typically upward along the stone edge in monumental Ogham; manuscripts may use left-to-right layouts. The line can be the stone’s corner. Historical readings require the artifact’s orientation and letter forms. Tree names and the metaphysical meanings supplied in the brief are interpretive associations, not a universally demonstrated original code.

The Taborite Model · Reading rules

Physical placement: document whether the mosaic is on a floor, wall, column, ceiling, vault, or another surface.
Visible object: count and describe tesserae, shapes, words, figures, and geometric repetition without adding a hidden meaning.
Documented meaning: cite ancient inscriptions, excavations, restoration notes, archival records, and scholarship.
Proposed Taborite meaning: record below/subconscious, middle/conscious, or above/supra-conscious as a named interpretive reading.
Disagreement: keep contradictory or uncertain readings attached to the same object rather than silently replacing the source.

Foundational dedication

The Mosaic Reader by Nathan Tabor is a working implementation of the Taborite Model by Nathan Tabor, honoring the Taborites and earlier and later generations referenced in the author's project notes.

The app does not claim direct genealogical descent, universal translation, automatically recognized iconography, or a historically proven secret code. These require independent evidence.
