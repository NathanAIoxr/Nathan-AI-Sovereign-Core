## Historical and editorial notes

- **Ogham order:** F1 BLFSN; F2 HDTCQ; F3 MGNGSTR; F4 AOUEI. “NG” and “ST” are approximate common instructional transliterations; real values and variants require specialist sources.
- **Forfeda:** five selected characters appear in the teaching chart; Unicode also encodes Peith. Forfeda values vary among sources.
- **Modern name conversion:** ᚛ᚈᚐᚁᚑᚏ᚜ is an instructional transliteration of the modern letters TABOR, not a documented ancient Irish spelling.
- **Universal scope:** The software preserves arbitrary Unicode and allows expert-supplied sources; it does not promise automatic decipherment of unknown ancient scripts or recognition of every spoken language.
- **Ancestral dedication:** The series honors Taborites and the author's ancestral interests. It does not claim documentary proof of personal descent without genealogical sources.
