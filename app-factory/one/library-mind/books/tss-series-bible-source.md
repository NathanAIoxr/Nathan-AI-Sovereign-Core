# THE TABOR SECRET SOCIETY
## A 13-Book Historical Mystery & Adventure Series
### By Nathan Tabor

> **OUR WORD IS OUR BOND**

**Series mandate:** The story ranges, in its fictional frame, from before the Silk Road through the Silk Road, ancient religious history, the Bohemian Hussite Wars, medieval publishing, transatlantic migrations, American wars and antislavery organizing, World War II resistance, and Madison County, Alabama. An underlying moral question drives all thirteen novels: whether innocence is protected as a human good or treated as a commodity to be possessed, sold, manipulated, or exploited.

**Author's exact thematic line:**

> “The real war that has always been fought is is innocence gonna be treated as a commodity which always has been”

**Fictional central premise:** Across history, people who challenge abuse and deception preserve fragments of an unfinished record called **The Witness Ledger**. The modern protagonists call their circle **THE TABOR SECRET SOCIETY**. The book's mystery is whether the scattered marks signal a genuine descent, independent adoption of a common name, or deliberate falsification. The fictional society's mission is to expose and end **FALSE LIGHT**—the appearance of virtue used to disguise exploitation—through evidence, protection, and accountability. This fictional premise does not establish a real, unbroken secret society or a pre-Silk-Road Tabor bloodline.

## The thirteen novels

### BOOK I — BEFORE THE FIRST ROAD
**Era:** Imagined pre-Silk-Road antiquity and present-day archival prologue.

**The mystery:** A family manuscript begins before recorded genealogies. An unnamed guardian preserves a promise: no child's innocence is merchandise. In the modern frame, the reader immediately learns the supposed antiquity of the manuscript cannot be independently established. Its true date becomes the first mystery.

**Story mechanism:** An invented seal and a recurring three-part question: Who acted? Who benefited? Who was harmed?

**Evidence status:** FICTION for the pre-Silk-Road family network and ancient cipher; historical background only for ancient trade and recordkeeping where separately sourced.

**Cliffhanger:** A mark on the supposed ancient leaf matches a much later printer's device.

### BOOK II — THE CARAVAN'S MIRROR
**Era:** Silk Road societies, told through a later manuscript and archaeological investigation.

**The mystery:** Robāt, ribāṭ, and rebāt lead a fictional investigator toward fortified caravan accommodations. A scribe appears to have translated a place term into a purported family name; the reader must determine whether the translation is authentic or a trap.

**Story mechanism:** T-B-R / T-V-R / R-B-T phonetic and reversal candidate grid, with separate linguistic classifications.

**Evidence status:** DOCUMENTED for historical caravanserais and the ribāṭ/robāt word family; UNVERIFIED for descent from any Silk Road person; FICTION for the hidden message.

**Cliffhanger:** A second copy spells the same alleged ancestor differently—and places that person centuries later.

### BOOK III — ITABYRION
**Era:** First-century Galilee, within a modern archival investigation.

**The mystery:** A description of fortifications associated with Mount Tabor becomes confused with a chronicle of secret tunnels elsewhere. The protagonist must distinguish eyewitness narrative from copied legend.

**Story mechanism:** Greek, Hebrew, and Latin place-name comparisons; a false map.

**Evidence status:** DOCUMENTED for Josephus's discussion of the mountain and Roman conflict; FICTION for the ancestral custodian and surviving hidden key.

**Cliffhanger:** A margin note refers to a light no witness can explain.

### BOOK IV — THE UNCREATED LIGHT
**Era:** Fourteenth-century Byzantium.

**The mystery:** Religious controversy surrounding the Light of Tabor draws the characters into a debate about illumination, authority, and who gets to define truth. An invented copyist hides a warning in variant manuscript readings.

**Story mechanism:** Textual comparison rather than assuming a divine phenomenon can be measured with an invented device.

**Evidence status:** DOCUMENTED for Gregory Palamas and theological controversy; FICTION for the Tabor-family copyist or encoded technical system.

**Cliffhanger:** A later manuscript is found inside a Bohemian monastery binding.

### BOOK V — THE REPUBLIC OF TÁBOR
**Era:** 1419–1424, Hussite Bohemia.

**The mystery:** The real founding of Tábor and Jan Žižka's campaigns become the stage for a fictional ledger recording the treatment of civilians as armies and religious authorities battle for control.

**Story mechanism:** War wagons and community administration, accurately separated from unproven secret printing expeditions or universal tunnel passageways.

**Evidence status:** DOCUMENTED for Tábor's founding, Hussite conflicts, and real leaders; FICTION for an inherited modern-family code or single historic secret society.

**Cliffhanger:** The ledger's custodian discovers that an ally is deleting names.

### BOOK VI — THE LAST WAGON
**Era:** 1434–1452, following Lipany.

**The mystery:** As radical Hussite power fractures, the record survives through two people who disagree about what duty to the truth requires. One protects the living; the other protects the documents.

**Story mechanism:** Multiple conflicting copies of the same letter, and a missing witness signature.

**Evidence status:** DOCUMENTED for the defeat at Lipany, Jan Roháč's later resistance, and the ending of Tábor's political independence; FICTION for the central pair and concealed documents.

**Cliffhanger:** The most dangerous page is not destroyed—it has been printed.

### BOOK VII — THE PRINTER'S CIPHER
**Era:** Sixteenth and seventeenth-century Central Europe.

**The mystery:** Amid persecution and exile, a fictional printer tries to preserve testimonies without revealing endangered witnesses. Readers separate real clandestine publications from the invented word-and-page cipher.

**Story mechanism:** Variant title pages, book signatures, intentional fictional letter grids.

**Evidence status:** DOCUMENTED for the Unity of the Brethren's printing and the Kralice Bible; FICTION for the series' exact cipher and lineage transmission.

**Cliffhanger:** A printed fragment appears centuries later across the Atlantic.

### BOOK VIII — THE NAME ACROSS THE WATER
**Era:** Colonial British America.

**The mystery:** TABER, TABOR, TABOUR and other spellings surface in different church, land, and probate records. A fictional genealogist must resist the convenient assumption that every bearer belongs to one family.

**Story mechanism:** Dated wills, deeds, witnessed signatures, and a page where a name has been deliberately altered.

**Evidence status:** DOCUMENTED only where original individual records can be located; UNVERIFIED for any bridge from the Bohemian group to American Tabor/Taber families; FICTION for the altered page.

**Cliffhanger:** Two men with the same name served opposing political causes.

### BOOK IX — THE CROWN AND THE OATH
**Era:** American Revolutionary War and early republic.

**The mystery:** Patriot service, loyalism, imprisonment, and conflicting testimonies prevent the simplistic story that everyone with a Tabor-like name supported one cause. A fictional descendant must reconstruct who signed what—and why.

**Story mechanism:** Pension declarations and independent corroborating documents; a fictional oath disguised as ordinary family correspondence.

**Evidence status:** DOCUMENTED for individually verified military and pension records; UNVERIFIED for any universal rebel bloodline; FICTION for the concealed oath.

**Cliffhanger:** A family Bible points not westward toward a treasure, but southward toward Alabama.

### BOOK X — THE UNDERGROUND DOOR
**Era:** Mid-nineteenth century, Tabor, Iowa.

**The mystery:** A town named Tabor gives shelter and assistance to freedom seekers. A fictional witness records their names so descendants may someday know the truth, while realizing the records could endanger the very people they protect.

**Story mechanism:** The difference between a secret route and an actual subterranean tunnel; household books and coded names in the novel.

**Evidence status:** DOCUMENTED for Tabor, Iowa, the Todd House, the Underground Railroad, and John Brown's association; FICTION for a direct tie to the author's ancestors.

**Cliffhanger:** The notebook bears the emblem of another, independently founded Tabor-named organization.

### BOOK XI — THE ORDER OF TWELVE
**Era:** 1846–early twentieth century, across the United States.

**The mystery:** An investigator discovers the documented history of Moses Dickson, the Knights of Liberty, and the later Knights and Daughters of Tabor. The order's mutual aid, ceremonies, and specialized vocabulary offer a far stronger moral inheritance than the fictional treasure map initially suggested.

**Story mechanism:** The real 1891 Taborian manual, historical symbols, and a new cipher visibly designated as invented.

**Evidence status:** DOCUMENTED for the 1872 order, mutual-aid role, ritual literature, and 1891 manual; UNVERIFIED for institutional or ancestral succession from medieval Taborites; FICTION for a merged secret society.

**Cliffhanger:** A wartime survivor recognizes an illustration from the ritual book.

### BOOK XII — THE THREE KINGS AND THE GRAVESTONES
**Era:** 1939–1945 Bohemia; parallel twentieth-century Madison County, Alabama.

**The mystery:** Actual Czech anti-Nazi resistance communications contrast with fictional pages about family secrecy. Meanwhile, a modern descendant inventories Madison County graves and discovers that a tempting genealogical bridge has been fabricated.

**Story mechanism:** Distinguish documented historical radio traffic from the newly developed harmonica / energy–harmony–frequency cipher; memorials and genealogy as distinct evidence types.

**Evidence status:** DOCUMENTED for Balabán, Mašín, Morávek and anti-Nazi resistance; FAMILY TESTIMONY / REQUIRES INSPECTION for the described ancestral gravestones; FICTION for the connection between the two stories.

**Cliffhanger:** One grave inscription shows that the book's alleged master genealogy has conflated two people.

### BOOK XIII — OUR WORD IS OUR BOND
**Era:** Present day, drawing on every prior volume.

**The mystery:** The protagonist finally learns that someone spent centuries' worth of fictional records inventing a single bloodline to make the story seem inevitable. The true achievement is to recover individuals' actions without erasing their differences. The society's ultimate purpose is protecting people and exposing exploitation—not building an untouchable legend.

**Story mechanism:** THE TABOR TRINITY, named precisely THE TABORIC LIGHT, THE LIGHT OF TABOR, and THE TABOR LIGHT CIPHER; BUILD (1+1=2), CLEAR (1−1=0), and ALWAYS PROTECT THE ONE as narrative and digital verification principles.

**Evidence status:** VERIFIED where individual records support specific facts; FICTION for the master plot, secret meetings, and continuity of the Tabor Secret Society itself.

**Final revelation:** The prize is not a list of who owned the world. It is a ledger of who protected the innocent—and who failed to act when they had the power.

## The series' truth-label system

Use explicit reader-visible labels for historical elements in every chapter and companion-app evidence card:

| Label | Meaning | Treatment in the novels |
|---|---|---|
| **VERIFIED HISTORY** | A specific event, institution, quotation, place or person is supported by identifiable historical evidence. | Date, original-language name, and source are supplied in an endnote. |
| **DOCUMENTED NAME / LINK UNVERIFIED** | A word, surname, symbol or place exists, but no common ancestry, continuous organization, or code transmission is proved. | Do not convert similarity into descent or complicity. |
| **FAMILY ACCOUNT — NOT YET INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED** | A grave, migration, service record or relationship is supplied through family history but lacks independently checked records. | Preserve as attributed testimony and seek corroborating documents. |
| **DISPUTED** | Sources conflict or specialists disagree. | Present the competing evidence and its limits. |
| **FICTIONAL CONSTRUCTION** | An invented member, meeting, cipher, manuscript, causal connection, family link or ancient history. | Freely dramatize, but do not present as archival fact. |

**Publication rule:** No finite historical investigation can be “verified by the entire history of humanity.” Verification instead means precise claims supported by specific records with traceable provenance. A documented shared name is not proof of kinship.

## Books, app, and research architecture

Each volume stands alone as a novel but contributes one authenticated record, one unverified connection, one fictional cipher puzzle, and one moral choice to the thirteen-book umbrella. Keep a single evidence index across all titles: **source ID → original text/photograph → date → person/place → spelling variant → relationship claim → status → chapter references**. The companion ONE book app can expose both a chronological plot route and a separately filterable historical-source route.
