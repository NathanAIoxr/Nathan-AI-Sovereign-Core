# Language collection reconciliation

The 7,918 language profiles in languages.json are the imported registry snapshot, not the total scope of Nathan Tabor's language project.

The owner's stated catalog scope is 66,666 destinations, including historical, extinct, constructed, regional and custom language systems. A roster with 66,666 individually identified language records has not yet been located in this reconciliation. Do not manufacture missing records or describe all destinations as translation-ready.

## Recovered source material

- [Universal Mammal Language master manual](sources/universal-mammal-source.txt): complete retrieved source text, including 26 vocal symbols, the Clicking & Ticking Sub-System, rhythmic spacing, body-expression channels and screenplay scores.
- [Language history and database architecture](sources/language-history-source.txt): complete retrieved source text covering living languages, historical and lost languages, constructed languages and ancestral variants.

The text is preserved as supplied. Its biological and historical assertions are source claims, not validation results. The mammal manual is an authored language/performance framework; universal animal comprehension has not been demonstrated by these files. Blank glyph positions in the source remain blank rather than being silently invented.
