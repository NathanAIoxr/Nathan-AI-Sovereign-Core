# Historical Sources and Interpretation Boundaries

This document supports source-critical development of the app, game, and books.

## Primary Ogham

- Four primary aicmí with five signs per family: 20 core letters.
- Standard family sequences: Beithe B L F S N; hÚatha H D T C Q; Muine M G NG Z R; Ailme A O U E I.
- Ogham inscriptions often follow stone edges, with the direction and continuation assessed per artifact.
- Traditional letter names and their botanical or poetic associations do not constitute automatic word translations.
- The five later forfeda have variant names, shapes and values; the Unicode supplementary character Peith is distinct.
- Attested readings are separate from interpretive or neo-pagan tree attributions.

## Universal encoding

A modern codec maps each Unicode scalar value to five base-20 digits represented by the twenty primary Ogham signs. It is reversible because 20^5 is greater than 0x10FFFF, the largest Unicode scalar value. Code points in the surrogate range are rejected during decoding. It does not recover an unrecorded speech sound, translate words, or provide confidentiality.

## Taborite Model by Nathan Tabor

This author-created interpretive mapping distinguishes:
- Below: floor, origins, foundations, subconscious
- Middle: eye level, walls, human interaction, consciousness
- Above: overhead, domes, sky/cosmos, supra-consciousness

The categories are an interpretive framework. The location of a real mosaic or inscription does not by itself prove its original intended symbolic meaning. The car-lot inflatable tube figure is a contemporary visual analogy for stems and strokes, not an ancient Ogham character.
