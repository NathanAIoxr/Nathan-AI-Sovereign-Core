# THE TABORITE UNIVERSAL LANGUAGE MATRIX — 13-VOLUME BOOK SERIES

**Author:** Nathan Tabor

The 13 linked manuscripts in this directory are **first-edition short books**, each with three completed prose chapters. They are not represented as full-length commercial novels. Their purpose is to constitute a readable, reviewable series foundation, ready for subsequent expansion without replacing the original material.

01. **The Spine and the Stone** — An opening journey from the visible mark to the preserved record; [read manuscript](01-the-spine-and-the-stone.md)
02. **Twenty Signs, Four Families** — The 4 × 5 geometry of the primary Ogham alphabet; [read manuscript](02-twenty-signs-four-families.md)
03. **Feda, Forest, and Figurative Roots** — Letter names, tree associations, and the line between history and metaphor; [read manuscript](03-feda-forest-and-figurative-roots.md)
04. **The Five After the Twenty** — Forfeda, variant values, and the danger of forcing later material into the original grid; [read manuscript](04-the-five-after-the-twenty.md)
05. **From Stone to Voice** — Articulation, transcription, and the sound of a written mark; [read manuscript](05-from-stone-to-voice.md)
06. **The Stretching-Stick Laboratory** — Turning static geometry into a playable visual lesson; [read manuscript](06-the-stretching-stick-laboratory.md)
07. **Ogham, Runes, and Other Roads** — A comparison of similarities that does not invent a common origin; [read manuscript](07-ogham-runes-and-other-roads.md)
08. **The Scripts of the World** — A cataloguing method for alphabets, abjads, abugidas, syllabaries, and logographic systems; [read manuscript](08-the-scripts-of-the-world.md)
09. **The Reversible Universal Cipher** — Encoding Unicode as coordinate sequences without confusing encryption and translation; [read manuscript](09-the-reversible-universal-cipher.md)
10. **The Three Architectural Planes** — Below, Middle, and Above as an explicitly interpretive reading of position; [read manuscript](10-the-three-architectural-planes.md)
11. **The Language Game of ONE** — Learning design, accessibility, and a library of progressively harder exercises; [read manuscript](11-the-language-game-of-one.md)
12. **The Ledger of Evidence** — Research methods for avoiding false translations and preserving disputes; [read manuscript](12-the-ledger-of-evidence.md)
13. **The Last Key: The ONE Library** — A blueprint for an additive universal archive and its future editions; [read manuscript](13-the-last-key-the-one-library.md)

## Editorial invariants

- 1+1=2 build; 1−1=0 clear.
- Preserve all original Ogham glyphs and source transcriptions.
- Distinguish historical transliteration, modern reversible coding, attested translation, and the author's interpretive model.
- Keep disputed forfeda readings labeled as disputed.
- The Taborite architectural layers are author-defined interpretation, not historical evidence by themselves.
- 13 volumes may be expanded additively; do not overwrite first editions.
