## Scope

The connected ONE archive contains **60 searchable, source-separated summary records** across six independent collections:

1. **History of humanity:** migrations, civilizations, Egypt, Babylon, ancient languages, historical periods, cycle comparisons and library research.
2. **Tabor history & rights:** Mount Tabor, medieval Taborites, early tabour/name research, Hussites, Knights and Daughters of Tabor, the International Order of Twelve, Underground Railroad history, Czech wartime resistance and surname/genealogy questions.
3. **Micro & macro:** micro- and macroscopic scales, 360° Equator/Mirror, original Biological Equator, 400-point architecture, five matrices, independent six-body chess/game design, 0–44 tripolar registry, 13 Polar Axes, 101 inversion family and exact original Tabor Trinity naming.
4. **Above · Middle · Below:** astronomy vs KJV cosmological texts; terrestrial and subterranean geology; above/middle/below language and mirror scans; five Bible matrices; TABOR GPS.
5. **Aliens · UAP · Watchers:** Project Sign, Grudge, Blue Book, NASA scientific UAP research, AARO, Redstone/spaceflight, Genesis 6/Enoch textual study, Hollow Earth lore, and the explicitly fictional Firmament Transit Protocol.
6. **Original story worlds:** THE HUNTSVILLE CONVERGENCE: PROJECT PALADIN, THE REDSTONE CHILD, THE ACT CHRONICLES, THE TAC CHRONICLES, THE CRIMSON WAVE, THE ARCHIVE BELOW, TABORSLVANIA, THE ARRIVAL, PROJECT SILOAM, Redstone–Vatican fiction, O.G.D.E.N. and related simulation design.

Record types are **documented subject**, **research lead**, **author-defined framework** and **creative fiction**. The archive never treats a shared name as an ancestral connection, a sacred text as proof of an alien encounter, or a novel premise as evidence of global secret underground transport. Even documented-topic labels require citation before detailed historical claims.
