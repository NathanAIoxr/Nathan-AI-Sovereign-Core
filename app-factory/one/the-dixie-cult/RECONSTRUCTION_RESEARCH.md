# THE NIGGINA RECONSTRUCTION

**Companion research and system design — editorial expansion, not author-supplied prose.**

## Scope
Trace the information process backward from modern LLMs through digital computing, printing, alphabets, early writing, Paleolithic signs, portable notched objects, oral and landscape traditions, gesture, biological learning, and the physical environment. Rebuild forward with traceable sources.

## 0 — Physical environment
Observe physical events and preserve measurement context. Reality is not an inscription, word, or model.

## 1 — Biological perception
Vision, hearing, smell, touch, animal-track recognition, emotion, and learned prediction. Perception can be wrong and requires evidence checks.

## 2 — Movement, gesture, voice
Repeated rhythms, movements, prosody, and communicative conventions can transmit information without a written alphabet.

## 3 — Oral and landscape memory
Songs, stories, landmarks, and repeated routes support social transmission of ecological, navigational, and cultural knowledge. Australian First Nations Songlines have their own living cultural meanings beyond computational analogy.

## 4 — Notched bones and physical objects
Lebombo and Ishango notches preserve physical patterns; mathematical, lunar and calendrical interpretations vary in confidence. First record geometry, dating, provenance, then hypotheses.

## 5 — Cave signs
The 32 recurring European Ice Age geometric sign types proposed by Genevieve von Petzinger form a research classification, not a decoded 32-character alphabet. Some animal-linked mark sequences have contested calendrical interpretations.

## 6 — Hobo markings, distinct later branch
Reported traveler signs described food, work, dangers, and shelter. Published symbol charts are not proof that every mark had a standardized meaning. Preserve dated source, locality, variant reading, and contrary evidence.

## 7 — Early accounts and scripts
Physical tokens and recordkeeping preceded cuneiform. Sumerian cuneiform served administrative, literary, legal, ritual, and other purposes. Avoid claiming that one institution invented or controlled all human memory.

## 8 — Sumerian lexical archive
Document the scholarly forms níĝ-gi-na (truth) and níĝ-lul (falsehood/deception) separately from the author's NIGGINA / NIGLUL creative spellings and declarations. Do not claim the author's modern application is an ancient translation.

## 9 — Modern computing
Distinguish token units, tokenizer IDs, learned embeddings, positional representation, attention, learned parameters, and model inference. Cave symbols and hobo signs are physical marks, not literally learned neural-network weights.

## 10 — Clear and rebuild
CLEAR means reject unsupported certainty from approved derivative conclusions, not destroy source records. BUILD means produce inspectable claims with provenance, alternatives, confidence, and reversible edits.

## Comparative record schema
- record_id
- system, dating, place and original artifact or utterance
- observed geometry or sound
- source references and reliability
- proposed meanings with independent evidence
- competing readings
- relationship type (resembles, translated_as, derived_from, used_in, speculative)
- approved interpretation and revision history
