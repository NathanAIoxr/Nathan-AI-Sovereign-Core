# THE DIXIE CULT — Volume III: The Reconstruction Fieldbook

By Nathan Tabor

**New companion research exercises — distinct from original author prose.**

## Reverse
Start with an LLM output. Locate the data, scripts, documents, markings, speech, gestures, and material observations that could support it. Do not invent a continuous historical lineage.

## Clear
For each claim record (a) original observation, (b) source and date, (c) interpretation, (d) competing interpretations, (e) confidence. Exclude unsupported certainty only from the derivative conclusion, never destroy archival records.

## Rebuild
Create an auditable claim with its source chain, tests, responsible context, and revision history. Maintain separate relations for resemblance, common function, proposed descent, and proven descent.

## Case studies
1. Recurring European Paleolithic cave signs: geometry before meaning.
2. Ishango notches: counts versus proposed calendars.
3. Hobo markings: local variation versus claimed standardization.
4. Indigenous Songlines: oral, ecological, and landscape memory in cultural context.
5. Early Sumerian writing: administrative and literary functions.
6. NIGGINA and NIGLUL: authorial spelling versus historical linguistic transliteration.
7. Neural language models: tokenizers, embeddings, attention, and learned weights.
