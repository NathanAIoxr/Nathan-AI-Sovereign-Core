One place for your original files, your bath systems, your recovery chronology and ongoing notes. The source is never silently rewritten. New information joins the record with its origin intact.

1 · Unlock your private health space

Checking…

Private by design: The application code is public, but your files are not. This module encrypts every saved original and its filename on this device using AES-256-GCM and your passphrase. Your passphrase is not saved. Nothing is uploaded to GitHub, TABOR Factory or a medical provider. This is a browser prototype, not certified medical-record storage; use a personal, trusted device and keep your own backups.

Checking secure local storage…

2 · Bring in the recovered archives

Select your recovered 36-file ZIP archive, other ZIPs, selected original files, or a whole extracted folder. Import runs on this device only; repeated originals are detected by cryptographic hash.

Supported compressed ZIP types: ordinary stored or deflate. On unsupported browsers, extract the ZIP yourself and choose the folder. Files are added only after you select them. The archived originals stay byte-for-byte intact.

3 · Recovery timeline

Record your own dates, experiences, treatments, changes and milestones. Label personal recollections as recollections; clinical proof is a separate source.

4 · The Bath Lab

Preserve each authored formula, version, source and prototype separately. Formula entries are historical notes, not medical treatment instructions or claims of safety.

5 · Your indexed original sources

Search metadata locally. Decrypt a source on demand to view or download it. Exports are not encrypted; keep exported ZIPs somewhere secure.
