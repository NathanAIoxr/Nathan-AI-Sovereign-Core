Grammar specification v0.1

Core clause: SUBJECT + NUMBER/QUANTITY + EVENT/ACTION + (LOCATION/OBJECT). Example: ANIMAL TWO BIRTH WATER = “Two herd animals give birth at the water” as an illustrative, context-based translation.

Boundaries: single spaces separate lexicon words. Speech: a short pause separates words; original Ti and Da forms preserve the author's pronunciation. Word classes: nominal roots, quantity roots, action roots, and location roots; entries can be reused according to context.

Qualifiers: tense, negation, possession, plural, question marking, and agreement are not yet formally specified. Existing words such as BEFORE, NOW, AFTER, NO, QUESTION are available lexically but are not asserted to form a complete grammatical system.

Language status: user-designed constructed language, not an attested Paleolithic language. Historical Ogham and Morse stages are comparisons, not evidence of direct descent. Ogham Unicode encoding here is letter-to-letter transcription, not restoration of ancient pronunciation.

Local/offline standalone app. No external assets, server, account, or tracking.

Full grammar and numeric system

Canonical clause: SUBJECT + (COUNT) + (TIME) + (POLARITY) + ACTION + (OBJECT) + (AT PLACE). Typed grammar markers: AT OF PAST FUTURE NEG QUESTION AND OR IF BECAUSE TOPIC EMPHASIS.

Exact original example: ^ · • • · Y · ~ → 0111 0010 1110 0110 → Ti-da-da titi ya ti-da-ti → “The herd (2) arrives/gives birth at the river.”

Questions: QUESTION PERSON SEE STAR. Future: FAMILY FUTURE BUILD SHELTER. Negation: PERSON NEG TAKE WATER. These are proposed constructions; the twelve original lexical meanings are unchanged.

Three distinct numeric namespaces: 4-bit original core word codes, 5-bit Tabor letter codes, and 9-bit extension word IDs. Grammar operators have 10-bit typed identifiers. Numbers use binary for machine storage, base-13 for optional positional notation, and tally marks for the human-readable quantities 1–13.

Base-13: decimal 10=A, 11=B, 12=C, 13=10, 26=20, 169=100. Consult GRAMMAR.md for the fully specified operators, examples, and disambiguation rules.
