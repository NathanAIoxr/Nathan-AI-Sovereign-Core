## Original 20-character alphabet

The complete 20-identifier scheme is in `alphabet.json`; it retains the author's `Z` line, `C / K` line, `NG` line and individually supplied 5-bit assignments. Unicode Ogham glyphs are added for comparison, not substituted for authored stem-sketches.

## Research distinctions

- The proposed `point/vector -> symbol -> language -> digital signal` framework is a conceptual synthesis. It is not evidence that all writing systems descend from a single decoded Ice Age alphabet.
- The precise ages/functions of specific Ice Age marks remain open research questions.
- A 0/1 *binary representation* is an abstract mathematical representation of states. Short versus long Morse pulses are both signal-on events, not literally 0 and 1.
- Both `Tabor` surname comparisons and African click consonant phonetics are valid comparative research topics; similar articulations alone do not establish ancestry.
- Humans' use of fingers, gesture, marks and sound to represent quantity supports a useful physical comparison without proving one canonical ancestral language.
