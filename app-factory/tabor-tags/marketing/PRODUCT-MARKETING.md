# TABOR TAGS — Product Marketing Copy
**By Nathan Tabor · 2026-11-12**

## Real equipment. Real movement. Virtual play. Real improvement.

Clip a Tabor Tag onto compatible sports equipment. Connect to a phone or tablet. See motion-driven play and training on your screen, with television presentation as an integration goal. Add body-worn Tabor Tags to investigate how movement unfolds from lower body through the torso, arms, and equipment.

**TAG IT. TRACK IT. PLAY IT. PERFECT IT.**

### Golf
- Use your own driver, irons, wedges, or putter.
- Prototype 3D driving range and animated ball tracer.
- Estimated carry, total distance, clubhead speed, ball speed, side spin and shot shape.
- Prototype putting mode.

### Baseball
- Proposed compatible bat and motion-tag training.
- Planned batting and pitching kinetic-sequence coaching.

### Bowling
- Proposed equipment/wearable tracking.
- Planned analysis of torso tilt, swing plane, release timing, and ball motion.

### Basketball
- Proposed ball/equipment and body-tag integration subject to safe hardware packaging.
- Planned shot-release angle and follow-through analysis.

### Athlete biomechanics
- Multi-tag setup: pelvis, torso, lead wrist, lead knee, equipment.
- Code supplied for temporal motion-order analysis and coaching feedback.
- Score and coaching messages are **prototype estimates**, not validated performance diagnostics.

### Software foundation supplied
- `TaborGolfRangeCartridge.swift`: SwiftUI + SceneKit golf simulation prototype.
- `TaborKinematicBodyEngine.swift`: Swift/CoreBluetooth-related multi-node motion engine and coaching UI.
- `TactileCoreSDK` / `TactileBLEPropManager`: companion integration identified in the original material; not included in this submission.

**Status:** Original code and architecture supplied; full hardware, sensor accuracy, and production functionality require engineering validation. This marketing sheet is a product vision grounded in supplied code, not a claim of independently verified hardware performance.
