THE IMPORTANT QUESTIONS

Answers without guesswork.

Can I pay through this site today?

No. The active flow creates a giving plan, not a charge. A payment merchant, verified webhooks, receipts and monthly cancellation tools are not connected.

Can I claim a tax deduction?

The legal recipient and federal tax-exempt status are not verified here. Do not assume the contribution is tax deductible.

Does a $30 gift guarantee a particular toy or meal?

No. The tier levels are illustrative budgets. Procurement costs and logistics will be verified with real suppliers before guarantees are made.

Can I submit my child's personal information?

No. This development website does not collect private family or child data. Secure referral partnerships will require separate review before launch.

What happens to the original Nathan AI sites?

They remain unchanged. This platform is additive and the existing Cash App draft has not been merged or represented as payment verification.
