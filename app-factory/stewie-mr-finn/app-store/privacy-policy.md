# Stewart & Mr. Finn Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026
Publisher: Nathan Tabor

Stewart & Mr. Finn operates offline. The app does not collect or transmit personal information to its publisher or third parties. It includes no third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, accounts, chat, or in-app purchases.

Game progress, selected characters, stickers, and comfort preferences are stored locally on your device. Removing the app may erase this data. Apple device backups are controlled by your device settings and Apple's policies.

The app does not request access to location, contacts, camera, or microphone. Its bundled web games run locally.
