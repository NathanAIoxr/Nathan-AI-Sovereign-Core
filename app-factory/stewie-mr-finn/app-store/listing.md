## Description
Join Stewart and Mr. Finn, two black-and-white cat companions, for adventures about curiosity, friendship, and kindness.

Explore the original 48-board campaign across six worlds. Visit North Paw and Mrs Clause Cat’s Workshop in a 16-chapter family adventure with 48 additional challenges. Try matching games, word mirrors, number puzzles, storytime, the Biscuit Factory, and a creative sticker playroom.

Choose your cat, collect rewards, and return to saved adventures. Families can play together and talk about listening, animal boundaries, and keeping promises.

Play offline. No ads, in-app purchases, accounts, or tracking. Progress is saved on your device.

By Nathan Tabor, a Human Mammal.

## What's new
North Paw and Mrs Clause Cat’s Workshop adventures, chapter save/resume improvements, clearer privacy information, and improved motion preferences.

## App Review notes
No login or special setup is required. From Home select Grand Adventure Map for the original campaign, Polar Family for the 16-chapter expansion, and Grown-Up Corner for privacy and comfort settings. Middle C and Mirror Bridge use locally bundled web content. No remote service is required. No purchases, ads, accounts, user content sharing, or tracking are included.
