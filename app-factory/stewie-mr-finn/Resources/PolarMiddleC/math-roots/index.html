Two-dimensional mathematics

The crossing of two equations

x² + y² − 5 = 0
x − y − 1 = 0

Newton's method in two dimensions updates (x,y) through a 2 × 2 Jacobian. A valid result satisfies both equations.

This model can generalize to additional dimensions, but each dimension requires equations and defensible solution methods.

Three different meanings of “root”

Mathematical — a zero satisfying f(x)=0; Linguistic — a historically documented word origin; Symbolic — a proposed creative connection. One does not prove the others.
