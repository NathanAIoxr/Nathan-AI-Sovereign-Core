The original game story

Two Cats. Two Poles. One Bridge.

Santa Dog calls “HO HO HO!” from North Paw. Ozzie answers “OH OH OH!” from South Paw. In the middle, Stewart and Mr. Finn take turns spinning their mirrored paw gears, testing whether each pairing is genuinely in balance. Every solved bridge earns a Friendship Star.

Mathematical rule: reverse(reversed word) = original word. Mechanical rule: two torques cancel only when equal in magnitude and opposite in direction on the same axis. Story rule: differences can complement each other without erasing either animal's identity.
