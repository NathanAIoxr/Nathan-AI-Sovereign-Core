# GODSALTER — The Franchise
**Founder / Author:** Nathan Tabor  
**Initial publication:** 2026-11-12  
**Exact title:** GODSALTER  
**Release classification:** Public concept and self-contained interactive website prototype  
**Statuses:** Brand proposed; source published; company unformed/unverified; no franchise sales; no licensed operators.

## The three dimensions
1. **LITERALLY — Enterprise:** a proposed family of digital products, original IP, physical experiences, creator licensing and local chapters.
2. **FIGURATIVELY — Alteration:** a symbolic framework for questioning power, separating truth from claims, respecting free will and replacing harmful systems with accountable practices.
3. **MYTHOLOGICALLY — Universe:** a clearly fictional pantheon and worlds governed by consequences, mirrors, memory and choice.

The name is preserved as **GODSALTER**. Proposed wordplay: "GOD'S ALTAR," "GODS ALTER," and "GOD SALTER" (the latter evokes salt, preservation, discernment and transformation). These interpretations are new creative proposals, not verified historical etymologies or founder quotations.

## The real-world franchise architecture (proposed)
- **Core IP studio:** maintains the name, canon, source-attribution ledger, visuals, games, characters and licensing standards.
- **Digital studio:** web experience first, then apps/games/interactive world-builder, subject to testing and accessibility.
- **Learning studio:** evidence literacy, creative writing, consent, source criticism and collaborative building.
- **Experiences:** events, exhibitions, workshops and pop-ups run by accountable hosts.
- **Partner/licensee program:** only after legal review, operating standards, approved use of brand and any required filings/disclosures.
- **Merchandise/publishing:** original text, apparel, prints, novels, tabletop material, collectibles and ethical collaborations.

### Franchise boundaries
No franchise is offered for sale by this repository. The term "franchise" is being used as a creative/strategic brand architecture, not an assertion that any license exists. A commercial rollout requires business registration as appropriate, ownership/IP review, trademark clearance, tax and insurance planning, consumer protection and franchise counsel review (including U.S. federal and applicable state franchise rules), written disclosures and operational readiness. Do not take money for franchise rights or claim legal approval before those steps.

## Rules of the worlds
- Respect each person's sovereignty, free will and consent.
- Protect children and vulnerable people. Do not glorify abuse or cover-ups.
- Record facts, user-authored originals, interpretations and fiction separately.
- Retain exact source wording where presented as a quote; label assistant-generated ideas as proposals.
- Correct mistakes with evidence. A character or institution has no immunity by title.
- No compulsory worship, fraudulent supernatural promises or real-world coercion.
- Fictional conflict resolves through accountability and constructive choice, not instructions for violence.
