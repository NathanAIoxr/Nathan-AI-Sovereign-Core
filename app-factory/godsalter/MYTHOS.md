# GODSALTER — Original mythological universe
**Status: creative proposal / fiction, not history or evidence.**  
**Credit:** Concept commissioned for Nathan Tabor's GODSALTER franchise.

## The opening
Before the first crown, the first church, and the first throne, there was an empty place where any traveler could lay down a claim. It was called the Altar. It did not demand offerings. It asked: *What have you done, and what will you change?*

The gods came expecting worship. The Mirror returned consequences. Mortals came expecting judgment. The Mirror returned choices. Each learned that a name is not a verdict.

## The three gates
1. **The Material Gate:** craft, trade, invention and tangible consequence. A promise is not a product until it works.
2. **The Mirror Gate:** memory, language, reversal and testimony. Evidence is distinguished from story.
3. **The Myth Gate:** archetypes, impossible landscapes, cosmic travelers and speculative legend. Wonder is free; consent remains binding.

## Fictional artifacts
- **The Mirror of Consequence:** reflects cause and effect, not worship or public image.
- **The Ledger of Names:** records each speaker's exact words before interpretations are added.
- **The Two Keys (0 and 1):** symbolic characters representing clearing and choosing; they are not a claim of supernatural mathematics.
- **The Threshold of Salt:** preserves what is worth keeping and separates what must be corrected.

## Creative guardrail
Figures from global religions, living beliefs and historical institutions may be discussed or adapted in future works with clear attribution and a distinction between tradition, satire, criticism and invented myth. No living person is fictionalized as a convicted offender without evidence. No supernatural character has authority over real participants.

This file is a proposed universe bible, not a claim that the founder previously dictated these details.
