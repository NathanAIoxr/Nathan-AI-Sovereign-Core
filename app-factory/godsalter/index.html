02 / THE ALTERATION

Alter the system. Not another person's will.

GODSALTER is a symbolic place for examining inherited authority, testing assertions, correcting errors, preserving original source words and choosing evidence over coercion.

0 — CLEAR

Identify what is false, harmful or unsupported; revise it responsibly.

1 — CHOOSE

Exercise independent thought, meaningful consent and personal veto.

2 — BUILD

Make an accountable replacement and document how it works.

Truth and myth are distinct records. Allegations are not evidence, and imagination is not proof.

03 / THE MYTHOLOGY • ORIGINAL FICTION

The Altar That Changes the Gods.

In this original fictional universe, the Altar is not a throne. It is a mirror: kings, gods, monsters and ordinary travelers must see the consequences of their choices. A title grants no exemption from accountability.

The Mirror

Shows a being what their actions have caused, not what their followers say.

The Keepers

Independent guardians of records, memory and consent. They hold no compulsory power.

The Crossing

A gateway where mythological worlds and mortal imagination meet without erasing their differences.

The mythic cast, cosmology and supernatural events are creative fiction, not claims about the real world.
