- Full 52-card standard blackjack deck and Fisher–Yates shuffle.
- Ace = 1 or 11; face card = 10; natural 21 detection; player/dealer blackjack comparison.
- Hit / Stand / Double Down (one final card, 2× *practice points*, not a wager).
- Dealer draws to 17 and stands on soft 17; dealer bust; equal score pushes; loss on player bust.
- Browser-local score persistence, session reset, JSON journey export, text-only accessible card labels.

- **Casino Floor Directory: 21 slots**, preserving the 13 supplied mechanics/rules as author text, Hazard (#14) with its received mechanics and explicitly missing Rule, and numbers 15–21 explicitly unprovided. Separate casino games are reference records, not falsely claimed to be playable.
