Equator flip: geographic versus personal frame

The local meaning of poleward changes at the equator: northward in the Northern Hemisphere and southward in the Southern Hemisphere. The directions of the geographic poles do not swap. A person's left/right/forward/backward rotate with their heading anywhere on Earth.

At the equator, geographic poles lie in opposite directions along the horizon, not directly above and below an observer. Near the geographic poles, local geographic heading becomes poorly defined. For safety-critical navigation, use validated sensors, maps and suitable certified systems.
