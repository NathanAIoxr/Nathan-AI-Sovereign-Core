## Documented correction
The supposed bat-wing-shaped "Royal Pingha" numbered 13 in an earlier AI-generated collection flyer was **not** taken from either of these museums. It was fabricated artwork and must never be confused with the historical objects. A photograph of the authentic Cleveland object shows an asymmetrical profile, not a symmetrical Batman-style silhouette. This source register does not authenticate the other 103 named positions.

## Claim review

- **H** does not mean that every precise variant label has been confirmed. It means the parent type/tradition has historical evidence.
- **C** designates editorial analogies and named variation.
- **P** means the claimed traditional name, period or usage is unverified.
- **R** identifies related but different artifact classes (for example, hairpins vs. hatpins and tupus).
- **O** identifies a contemporary Nathan Tabor collector design, not an archaeological type.
- The worldwide appearance of similar physical forms does not establish that an ancient "universal box" was recognized by every culture.
- Antarctica is a contemporary scientific-comparison chapter, not a pre-contact human society.

Original author collection names and independent historical verification stay separate.
