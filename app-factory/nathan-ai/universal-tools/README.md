# Universal 13 — Geography & Geometry of Humanity

**Nathan AI → TABOR ROOT → TABOR 1–2–3 FACTORY → ONE**

## What is implemented

- **Eight** public collector families and **104 catalog positions** (13 per box).
- Exact user-supplied *names* preserved as distinct data records, rather than replaced with generic weapons.
- Responsive, keyboard-accessible 12+1 display-case layouts with an enlarged center cell.
- Search by item name, location, geometry and current editorial evidence classification.
- Browser-playable **World Atlas Challenge** with geometry, regional grouping and evidence modes.
- Browser-playable abstract **Star & Diamond** three-in-a-row strategy board.
- Shared 13th centerpiece for Sets 5–7: **Fire-Drill Bow & Inertial Flywheel**. Prior centerpiece concept names are retained as `previousCenterpiece`.
- Existing games and owner-local source archives are not replaced or uploaded.

## Status labels

`H`: Documented parent artifact/tradition; individual attribution still requires inspection.  
`C`: Comparative form of a documented object family.  
`P`: Provisional historical claim or terminology.  
`R`: Related analog, not the same artifact type.  
`O`: Original collector design and not a known ancient archaeological category.

The catalog is **educational editorial material**, not a claim that every name is historically verified or that people on seven continents independently invented every form. Antarctica is included only as a **modern scientific analogy**. The prototype does not include operational weapon construction, target guidance, sharpening plans or launcher build parameters. Display reproductions are conceptual, rounded, stationary foam/card objects.

## Boundaries and provenance

The earlier owner-authored descriptions are **not** republished as verified historical statements; the catalog preserves names and separates editorial classification. Historical claims should be matched to museum accession numbers and citations in a future provenance pass. No unreleased source-pack content is distributed.

The previous assembly documents `Tabor_13_Unit_Universal_Collector_Atlas.pdf` and `Tabor_Universal_13_Complete_Blueprint_Atlas.pdf` are conversation assets, **not automatically shipped or linked** by this public app.
