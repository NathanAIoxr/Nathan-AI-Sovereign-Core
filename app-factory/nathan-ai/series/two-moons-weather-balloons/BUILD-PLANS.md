## Documented astronomical reference

- Earth's Moon has mean radius **1,737.4 km** and mean diameter **3,474.8 km** (approximately **2,159 miles**).
- A circle at that radius has circumference about **10,916 km** (2πr).
- The natural Moon is a rocky satellite approximately 4.5 billion years old.
- Earth has **one** established natural Moon. Quasi-satellites and small temporarily associated objects require independent orbital identification; an elliptical *path* is not an ellipsoidal *body*.
- No reviewed records substantiate manufacture of the Moon or a second Moon at Redstone Arsenal.

Source: [NASA Moon Facts](https://science.nasa.gov/moon/facts/) · [NASA Moon Formation](https://science.nasa.gov/moon/formation/).
