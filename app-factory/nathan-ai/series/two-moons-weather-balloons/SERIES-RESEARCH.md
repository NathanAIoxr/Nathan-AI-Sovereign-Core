## The weather-balloon connection: documented engineering

| Object / program | How it works | What is established | Primary source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Weather balloon with radiosonde** | Gas-filled balloon carries a meteorological instrument, typically reaches about 100,000–115,000 feet, expands and bursts; the radiosonde transmits pressure, temperature, humidity and wind-related data | Routine atmospheric measurement, generally two launches per day at many stations; **not an orbiting spacecraft** | [National Weather Service radiosonde factsheet](https://www.weather.gov/upperair/factsheet) |
| **Project MOGUL high-altitude balloon trains** | Classified balloon-borne sensors and radar-reflecting equipment used in the late 1940s to investigate Soviet nuclear activity | A U.S. government program; the Air Force identified a likely MOGUL balloon train as the source of 1947 Roswell debris, a conclusion summarized in a 1995 GAO record review. This shows that *some balloons were used for classified missions*, not that every balloon or unusual sky object is classified | [U.S. Government Accountability Office, GAO/NSIAD-95-187](https://www.gao.gov/assets/nsiad-95-187.pdf) |
| **NASA Echo 1A** | 100-foot aluminized Mylar balloon launched into **Earth orbit** on August 12, 1960; reflected radio signals between terrestrial stations | A large **artificial balloon satellite** was genuinely built and deployed; it was a passive communications reflector and is not an ordinary atmospheric weather balloon | [NASA: 50 Years of Communications in Space](https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/50-years-of-communications-space/) and [NASA: Echo, NASA's First Communications Satellite](https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/echo-nasas-first-communications-satellite/) |
| **Project Horizon** | U.S. Army Ballistic Missile Agency 1959 study proposed a military outpost on the Moon and supporting logistics | A real ABMA/Redstone-era study with documented concepts, budgets, and rocket requirements; **the outpost was not constructed** | [U.S. Army Project Horizon history](https://www.army.mil/article/189129/) |
| **Camp Century / Project Iceworm** | U.S. Army Corps of Engineers built a facility under Greenland's ice; a larger missile network was separately considered | Camp Century was real, with engineering plans and a 1965 government construction report. The larger Iceworm missile network was not constructed as envisaged | [U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Technical Report 174](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/GOVPUB-D103-PURL-gpo86535) |
| **Earth's Moon** | Natural satellite with geologically measured crust, mantle, and core, and samples returned by Apollo | Lunar rocks and multiple independent data streams support a natural geologic history. The claim that the Moon was manufactured at Redstone is **not supported by the documents reviewed** | [NASA: Moon Formation](https://science.nasa.gov/moon/formation/) and [NASA: Moon Facts](https://science.nasa.gov/moon/facts/) |

**The critical line:** A balloon can be an atmospheric weather instrument, a classified payload carrier, or an orbital inflatable satellite. That factual similarity of shape or material does **not** establish identical function, ownership, location, mission, or an artificially constructed Moon. Orbiting at roughly thousands of kilometers from Earth and flying within the upper atmosphere are physically different regimes.
