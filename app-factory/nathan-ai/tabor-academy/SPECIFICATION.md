1. **Zero-Friction Capture (The Voice Layer)**

   - Every spoken word is indexed by both its **exact phonetic sound** and its **original root meaning** (cross-referenced against primary historical records, Strong’s Concordance, and original manuscripts rather than third-party commentary).
2. **The Library of Tabor (The Indexing Layer)**

4. **The Sovereignty & Innocence Filter (The Core Operating Rule)**
   - Every lesson, tool, and simulation passes through a non-negotiable binary check: **Does it protect innocence (`1`), and can it be replicated (`1`)?** Anything built on manipulation, ambiguity, or hidden control is purged (`1 - 1 = 0`) before it ever reaches the learner.
