# THE GATHERING CIRCLE™ — by Nathan Tabor
**Date:** November 12, 2026  
**Category:** Identity-building, world-building, community-building, user-generated-content sandbox and real-world growth platform.  
**Status:** Original creator-directed concept + local browser prototype; production multiplayer, backend, moderation and verified real-world progress are **not implemented**.

## Creator's vision
People are trying to remember who they are. With the help of others who can join, they build the world they want to see **while also building themselves in the real world**.

**HERE → THERE → EVERYWHERE → BACK HERE**
- HERE: Know yourself; explore identity, history, creativity, interests, strengths.
- THERE: Explore and learn; games, stories, language, skills, history.
- EVERYWHERE: Build with others; communities, shared worlds, original games and projects.
- BACK HERE: Return to real life; apply skills, take constructive action and reflect on results.

**Build the world. Build yourself. In the game and in real life.**

## Core modules
1. **Discover who you are:** Optional personal reflection and profile.
2. **Build worlds:** User-defined worlds, story spaces, and community projects.
3. **Join communities:** Find collaborators, opt into groups, and contribute.
4. **Learn skills:** Practice new capabilities digitally and offline.
5. **Create games:** Author original mini-games and participatory challenges.
6. **Play together:** Aspirational multiplayer and co-creation.
7. **Real-life missions:** Translate digital plans into voluntary offline activities.
8. **Grow yourself:** Track self-defined goals and progress, without imposing identities.

## Historical throughline (inspiration, not identical functions)
Ancient storytelling, astragali and divination; street circles and games such as marbles or Mancala; board games of strategy and storytelling; digital user-generated sandboxes; then a new feedback loop connecting a community's digital creations with its real-world actions.

## Prototype
Open [index.html](./index.html). A static, accessible, responsive browser experience with editable personal reflection, user-created world ideas, group ideas, learning goals, game ideas, and real-life missions. Content is persisted **only in the same browser's localStorage** and can be exported as JSON or cleared. Inputs are displayed as text, not injected HTML. No secrets, accounts, public user records, or other infrastructure required.

## Production implementation roadmap (not claims of completion)
- Accounts with consent-first optional profiles, privacy controls, data deletion, and export.
- Actual shared multiplayer worlds, safe collaboration, versioned world editing and access permissions.
- User-generated content creator tools, world schema, moderation, abuse reporting and protections for minors.
- Skill learning and real-life mission tracking with user-controlled evidence, not fabricated verification.
- App and game plugin APIs, creator licensing, accessibility, localization, and mobile support.
- Security review, hosting, backend tests, analytics with privacy safeguards and release pipeline.
