How this map works

Capture the auditory form and subjective gut/harmony rating separately from standard orthography, pronunciation notation, etymology, meaning and translations. The International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) is optional and should be checked by language specialists. Accent, speaker and context may produce different legitimate mappings. Rhythm is a notation field, not an instrument measuring bodily frequencies.

Use Unicode for scripts and explicit direction tags. Writing systems and languages do not have a 1:1 relationship. Nothing here automatically translates words, reads your microphone, or proves a physical frequency associated with a meaning.
