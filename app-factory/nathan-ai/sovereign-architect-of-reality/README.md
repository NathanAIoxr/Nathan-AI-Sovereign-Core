# Sovereign Architect of Reality

An independent Nathan AI conceptual architecture linking anchored arithmetic to accountable computing, research and simulations. This is a named project and philosophical framework, not a scientific claim of controlling physical reality.

## Four anchors
- `1+1=2` — BUILD: verify additive inputs and outputs.
- `1-1=0` — CLEAR: verify exact subtraction and zero-state conditions. Do not erase real losses, debt, records, or history.
- `1×1=1` — INTEGRATE: preserve identities and project boundaries when connecting modules.
- `1÷1=1` — VERIFY: independent recomputation, source evidence, and audit.

## Four-stage loop
BUILD → CLEAR → INTEGRATE → VERIFY. All operations retain input, transformation, observed result, timestamp, provenance, and decision. No transformation guarantees a profitable mining outcome.
