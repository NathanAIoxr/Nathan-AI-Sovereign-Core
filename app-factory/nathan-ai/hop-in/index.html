Ask Nathan AI — offline navigation guide

This guide routes you to existing tools; it does not generate model answers. For source-backed conversation, open Nathan AI Assistant.

Time & Space

Research dates, scales and possible perspectives. No literal time travel, remote live tracking, satellite access or microscopic imaging is claimed.

Explore time-aware maps: OpenHistoricalMap · Wikidata. External services are not silently queried.

Group Assembly

Compare research questions about historical figures, religious traditions and recorded texts. These are research lenses, not simulated quotations or authentic voices.

Hold Ctrl/Command to select more than one.

Truth & Source Lens

Separate what a record says, what someone thinks it means, and what still needs proof. A disputed interpretation is not automatically a lie.

Primary material

Original documents, dated manuscripts, direct records and their provenance.

Translation & variants

Interpretations

Create the Mind of Your God

Family & Worlds
