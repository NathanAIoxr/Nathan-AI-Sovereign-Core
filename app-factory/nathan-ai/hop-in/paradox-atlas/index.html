03 · PERSISTENT LENS

Truth. Sources. Agency.

The interface keeps facts, disputed interpretations and creator fiction distinct. A paradox is not automatically an error; an anomaly is not automatically evidence of wrongdoing.

Evidence and classification
  1. State the exact claim, premises and scope.
  2. Find the earliest reliable source.
  3. Classify formal contradiction, apparent paradox, empirical anomaly, cognitive illusion, historical question or creative material.
  4. Compare alternatives and record uncertainty.
  5. Preserve conflicting evidence, without silently deleting it.
Safety and privacy

Clear only removes this screen's selection and search. It does not destroy servers or any other user data. No raw microphone stream is sent by this app itself. Browser dictation may use an external recognition provider. The project’s protection principle is not a claim of proven hardware-level security.

Creator canon remains separate from historical or scientific records.
