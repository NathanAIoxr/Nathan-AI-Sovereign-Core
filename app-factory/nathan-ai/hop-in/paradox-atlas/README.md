# NATHAN AI · Paradox / Enigma / Anomaly / Contradiction Atlas

**Project principle:** INNOCENCE IS THE PRECIOUS GIFT · 1+1=2 BUILD · 1−1=0 CLEAR.

## What this additive change actually delivers

- Three-zone adaptive **browser interface** (mobile input/optional browser dictation; desktop micro/macro comparison; persistent evidence lens).
- A searchable catalog of **298 distinct named reference leads**, grouped across 14 broad fields. They include *different kinds* of topics: mathematical/logical paradoxes, puzzles, empirical anomalies, cognitive illusions, historical enigmas, dilemmas, computing concurrency anomalies, fallacies and textual research questions.
- The **118 canon identities** from the existing independent `app-factory/games/paradox-118/roster.js` loaded without rewriting the game or original names. These are clearly labeled **fictional creator characters**.
- **416 records total**, not 416 academically verified paradoxes. It is a catalog of research starting points and canon references.
- Local search, category and provenance filtering, scrollable result cards, select, perspectives, research-prompt copying, a limited JSON export, optional browser dictation and manual cross-app navigation to the existing Nathan AI chatbox.
- The chatbox now routes `/paradoxes` and related queries into the atlas.

## Provenance and quality boundaries

1. **Reference leads (298):** curated name-level index assembled for this release. They do not each have individual citations, independent verification, nor guaranteed established nomenclature. Several are related formulations, overlapping terms or interpretive topics, not paradoxes in the strict sense.
2. **Creator originals (118):** source is existing named character roster; exact capitalization and variants preserved. Do **not** claim these are independent academic paradoxes.
3. **Historical and theological filters:** frame questions for investigation. They do not delete originals, certify any religion as true/false, speak as actual historical people, or prove anyone lied.
4. **Research link:** external encyclopedia search gives discovery leads, **not primary-source proof**. Use original primary texts, peer-reviewed papers, reference handbooks, independent witnesses and dates before upgrading a claim to verified.
5. A real anomaly does not mean fraud, danger or a supernatural explanation. Not all contradictions are logically inconsistent after premises/context are clarified.

Useful reputable starting overviews: Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy Russell's Paradox https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/russell-paradox/ ; Sorites Paradox https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/sorites-paradox/ .

## Operating modes and limitations

No AI provider, global scanning, continuous voice mapping, voice cloning, browser-level security filter, literal time travel, automatic real-time anomaly detection, remote execution or hardware kill switch is installed by this atlas. Browsing information is in memory unless the user selects export. Clear resets the visible selection and filters only. Browser voice recognition requires permission and may invoke an external provider. No secrets, personal data, or microphone audio is saved by the atlas.

Run repository root with `python3 -m http.server 8765` then open `http://localhost:8765/app-factory/nathan-ai/hop-in/paradox-atlas/`. External model responses remain gated through the separately configured source-grounded Nathan AI Assistant.
