Crew log

An early-’90s paint crew pulls up to a church with SHIT HAPPENS on the bumper. In this fictional arcade retelling, the sticker is the one thing that can turn a whole screen of sludge into gold. What a testimony.

01

The Paint Crew Scaffolds

Scrape, prime, and paint every marked bay. Press Brush three times at each bay.

02

The Inside-Out Test

Keep moving to hold back the rats. Grab the bumper sticker to fire a TIHS wave. A KJV relic charges a spare flip.

03

THE STAR U R

Paint every bay and reach the star at the top. Press TIHS / STAR beside it to clear the stage.

KJV relics

The crew can find verses from 1 John 1:7, Ephesians 5:11, and Matthew 5:16. Uncover all three in a single run.
