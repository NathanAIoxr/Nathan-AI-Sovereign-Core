## Site behavior

- Responsive, text-first visual design; no invented faces, portraits or unrelated people/pets.
- Website directory: 30 independently named projects, each marked with its real public browser module status or a clearly unconnected/planned status. Client-side title/summary search and category filtering.
- Christmas Toy Drive page: original owner and 24/7/365 identity, mission copy and a **browser-local gift ideas planner** with eight example items. No youth profiles, signup form, donation checkout, recipient intake or shared database. Gift selections can be copied or printed; localStorage provides optional convenience only.
- The owner’s snake-language quotation is preserved verbatim and attributed to Nathan Tabor in the Nathan AI directory.
- Source/ownership boundaries remain explicit. A prototype does not prove full external domain integration or nonprofit registration.
