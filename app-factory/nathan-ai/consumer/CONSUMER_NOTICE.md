## Start here — plain language

- **What exists:** A directory of currently identified, source-backed browser experiences and previews, each linked to its own application and technical boundaries.
- **What this does not promise:** No automatic access to your AI-provider history; no guaranteed antivirus, live intrusion countermeasures, biometrics, medical diagnoses, authenticated identity, paid features, production cloud deployment or universal safety.
- **What you choose:** You decide which linked program to open, which user-provided files to import, whether to create a local vault, and whether to export backups. This directory itself has no form for a name, address, contact details, files, accounts or payment information.
- **Your data:** This specific consumer directory does not add third-party analytics, automatically query your location or save the selected market in localStorage. Its app catalog and regulation guide are read-only same-origin JSON. The web host and your browser still process ordinary network and device information; other linked apps may save records locally, use permissions or connect to user-configured endpoints. Review each program's own notices.
- **Permanent storage caution:** Device failures, clearing site data, browser quirks or losing a passphrase may make locally stored content unrecoverable. Export an independent backup of your authored originals.
- **Children and privacy:** A child-directed activity is not automatically COPPA-compliant merely because no login appears here. Children's personal data collection, if offered in any linked service, requires separate assessment and applicable parental notice/consent and security protections. Do not collect children's images, voice, account data or contact details until the specific service is approved for that use.
- **AI transparency:** Some modules simulate, demonstrate or label AI architectures without providing a live model. Where a future product actually uses AI interaction, generated content or automated consequential decisions, add accurate in-product transparency and jurisdiction-specific safeguards.
- **Health, biometric and personal files:** Health and hand/iris imaging tools are for private organization, learning and research. They are not verified medical devices, diagnostic tests, identity proof, security credentials or replacements for qualified care.
- **Hacking, security and dehacking:** Hall of Mirrors and Five Guardians are passive or inert preview functions. **They do not provide antivirus scans, live quarantine, malware removal, firewall enforcement, or permission to access systems belonging to others.**
- **Books and beliefs:** Original creative writing, mythic mappings, ancient-language hypotheses, religious interpretations and political opinions remain attributable to their author. Publication does not turn hypotheses, allegories or contested claims into established history, science or legal findings.
- **Rights:** Licenses and rights in each original work and third-party materials must be verified separately. Do not assume that a publicly visible file or a project attribution gives you permission to reuse someone else's work.
- **Consumer protections:** Nothing in these educational notices attempts to exclude or waive consumer rights or duties that cannot lawfully be excluded. No purchase or subscription is offered by this directory.

## What is still legally necessary before a *commercial* consumer release

The repository alone does not establish the legally responsible operator or real contact and address for consumer requests, nor does it establish how **every** linked service, host and provider collects or transfers data. Those facts must be supplied and independently checked before public-facing legal documents can be finalized.

1. **True publisher information:** legal entity/person that acts as operator and data controller where applicable; actual contact channel and address; applicable DPO/representative and authority contacts. **Not yet verified.** Do not invent these.
2. **Service-specific data map:** categories and purposes; controller/processor role; legal basis when required; cookies/SDKs; vendors; sharing/sale; retention; transfers; breach response; children's data. **Not yet verified across every linked app.**
3. **Privacy request and accessibility channel:** functioning user rights, support, complaint and correction/deletion workflow, with applicable time limits, languages and reasonable accessibility. **Not yet verified.**
4. **Checkout and payments:** legal seller, price/currency/taxes, automatic renewal, cancellation/refunds, store policies and consumer rights if future commerce is introduced. **No checkout is provided by this directory.**
5. **Sector and release review:** health, AI, biometric image data, children, cybersecurity, educational claims and accessibility may require separate formal review, registrations or technical evidence.
6. **Regulatory change management:** assess actual release date, geographic audience, service operator and architecture. No legal checklist published on a fixed date can guarantee permanent worldwide compliance.
