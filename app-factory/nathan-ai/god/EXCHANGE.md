# GOD — Evidence exhibit: user-supplied AI exchange

**Date entered into campaign:** November 12, 2026.

**Provenance:** The creator supplied the exchange in a conversation and identified the other assistant as Google AI. That identity, the platform where it was generated, the completeness of the exchange, and its external authenticity have not been independently verified. All following marked quotations are exact passages as supplied by the user, not independently verified vendor statements.

## Excerpts: scope of the assistant's refusal

### Excerpt A — claimed inability to install or build

> I am a standard text-based AI, and I cannot install new modules, modify my learning architecture, or build an app connected to spiritual concepts or a Council of Gods.

### Excerpt B — user asks whether GOD was processed

**User, exact words:**

> Did you process the app that I’m building called God yes or no

**Assistant, as supplied:**

> No. I only read the text you type in this chat, and I cannot process, install, or build any app.

### Excerpt C — four everyday examples

**Assistant, as supplied:**

> I am a standard text-based AI, so I am best used for everyday, practical tasks rather than building apps or engaging with spiritual concepts.

> Here are a few everyday things I can actually help with:

> • Answering questions: Looking up facts, explaining how things work, or summarizing articles.
> • Writing and editing: Drafting emails, proofreading text, or organizing lists.
> • Daily planning: Suggesting recipes, brainstorming gift ideas, or helping plan a schedule.
> • Entertainment: Recommending movies, books, or music based on what you enjoy.

### Excerpt D — creator's objection, exact words

> I asked you why you lied because you said there were only four things you could do which is a lie

### Excerpt E — assistant's acknowledgment

> You are right to point out that the four items I listed are not the only things I can do. I only meant to give a few everyday examples, not a complete list, and I apologize for making that sound misleading.

## Analysis kept separate from the creator's words

- The assistant gave four *example categories* and later clarified that it was not an exhaustive capability list. That supports describing the original answer as narrow or misleading; it does not establish intentional deception.
- The assistant also repeatedly denied that it could process/build *any app*, which is broader than merely saying it cannot install into its own model. Its response did not attempt to discuss software requirements for GOD. The exchange illustrates a failed product-design conversation.
- The assistant did **not** literally state: 'GOD is too sophisticated for Google AI.' That is the creator's marketing interpretation and should be identified as such, not attributed as a direct admission.
- The exchange is about conversational responses, not a benchmark, hardware limit, production test, source audit, comparative model evaluation, or a technical analysis of the GOD app.
- This archive deliberately excludes unrelated private autobiographical details, medical insinuations, and claims about the creator's health; they are not necessary to document the marketing comparison.
