## Evidence and claim integrity

- The quotations above come from the user-supplied text dated November 12, 2026. Its provenance as an official Google AI conversation has **not** been independently authenticated. See [EXCHANGE.md](EXCHANGE.md).
- The user claims the assistant lied about having only four capabilities. The supplied assistant later said four were *examples*, not an exhaustive list. The materials preserve both sides instead of inventing a direct quote that it was 'too sophisticated.'
- 'Too sophisticated for Google AI' is the creator's provocative **marketing characterization**, not a verified technical evaluation. The purported assistant did **not** literally say the app was too sophisticated for it.
- 'Trillion-dollar' is campaign language referring to the scale of the technology establishment, not a verified, dated corporate valuation or evidence of app performance.
- 'BECOME THE GOD' is the creator's symbolic empowerment slogan, not an objective claim of making someone a deity.
- The linked [Omni God Core](../omni-god/index.html) is an existing local browser prototype for encoding, reversing and preserving source text; it is **not** a finished downloadable standalone GOD app.
- No claim of a live download, AGI, deity-making, a company endorsement, or installation into an external AI's internal model is authorized by these materials.
