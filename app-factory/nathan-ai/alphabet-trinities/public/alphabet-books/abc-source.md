**Across ancient and modern languages, the letters A, B, and C each anchor a foundational "Trinity" of three major mythological, linguistic, or cultural figures, followed by broader sets of notable terms.**

**1. The Letter A: The Trinity of Origin, Light, & Victory**

- **Apollo (Greek & Roman Mythology).** The god of sun, light, music, prophecy, and truth. His name is traditionally linked to concepts of driving away evil or gathering together.
- **Athena (Greek Mythology).** The goddess of wisdom, strategic warfare, and crafts, born directly from the forehead of Zeus as the embodiment of rational intelligence.
- **Anubis (Ancient Egyptian Mythology).** The jackal-headed guardian of the underworld and guide of souls, presiding over the weighing of the heart against the feather of truth (*Ma'at*).

**Additional Notable *A* Figures & Roots**

- **Alpha (Greek Alphabet).** The first letter of the Greek alphabet (derived from Phoenician *Aleph*, meaning "ox" or "strength"), representing the beginning or primary source.
- **Ares (Greek Mythology).** The god of raw physical courage and the tumult of war, contrasting with Athena's strategic focus.
- **Atlas (Greek Mythology).** The Titan condemned to hold up the celestial heavens, lending his name to the anatomical top vertebra and collections of maps.
- **Avalon (Arthurian Legend).** The mythical island where King Arthur's sword Excalibur was forged and where he was taken to recover.

**2. The Letter B: The Trinity of Thunder, Wisdom, & Song**

- **Brahma (Hindu Tradition).** The creator deity in the Hindu *Trimurti* (alongside Vishnu and Shiva), associated with knowledge, the Vedas, and cosmic generation.
- **Baldr (Norse Mythology).** The shining god of light, purity, and peace, whose death sets off the chain of events leading to *Ragnarök*.
- **Bastet (Ancient Egyptian Mythology).** The lioness and cat goddess of protection, hearth, music, and fierce defense of the home.

**Additional Notable *B* Figures & Roots**

- **Beth / Bayit (Hebrew & Phoenician).** The second letter of the alphabet, literally meaning "house," "tent," or "dwelling place."
- **Brigid (Celtic Mythology).** The triple goddess of fire, poetry, smithcraft, and healing springs.
- **Boreas (Greek Mythology).** The personification of the fierce north wind and bringer of cold winter air.
- **Beowulf (Old English Epic).** The legendary Geatish hero who defeats the monster Grendel and a fire-breathing dragon.

**3. The Letter C: The Trinity of Time, Hunt, & Underworld**

- **Chronos / Cronus (Greek Mythology).** *Chronos* represents the personification of linear time itself, while *Cronus* is the leader of the Titans who ruled during the mythological Golden Age.
- **Cerberus (Greek Mythology).** The three-headed hound guarding the gates of the Underworld to prevent the dead from leaving and the living from entering without permission.
- **Circe (Greek Mythology).** The sorceress of Aeaea skilled in herbalism, transformation, and guidance, who aids Odysseus on his journey home.

**Additional Notable *C* Figures & Roots**

- **Cernunnos (Celtic Mythology).** The antlered deity associated with forests, wild animals, and natural cycles.
- **Chimera (Greek Mythology).** A fire-breathing hybrid creature composed of a lion, a goat, and a serpent, now used in genetics and biology to describe organisms with two distinct sets of DNA.
- **Camelot (Arthurian Legend).** The legendary court and castle of King Arthur, symbolizing chivalry and unified governance.
- **Cosmos (Greek *Kosmos*).** Originally meaning "order," "harmony," or "arrangement," later applied to the universe as an orderly, structured system.
