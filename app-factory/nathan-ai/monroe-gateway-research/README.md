# Monroe / Gateway Research Integration

Independent public-document research project. Preserve the original CELEBRUM AI, Voice Box, and Tabor Bank of 69 projects without merging or overwriting them.

## Research references
- Robert Monroe and the Monroe Institute: historical audio-pattern and Hemi-Sync research.
- U.S. Patent 3,884,218 (1975); U.S. Patents 5,213,562 (1993), 5,356,368 (1994): patent references requiring source verification.
- 1983 U.S. Army Gateway Process assessment in the CIA declassified archive.
- Historical CENTER LANE and STAR GATE research programs.

## Data contract
Namespace: `research.monroe-gateway`; mode: `HOLD`; source_class: `public-research`; evidence_status: `unverified-until-source-checked`.

## Integration boundaries
Voice Box: research citations and authorized acoustic measurements only. CELEBRUM AI: opt-in local audio-pattern analysis, with no claims of mind reading. Conductor: metadata-only event linkage and provenance. Tabor Bank of 69: optional research reference, never a mining profitability or trading signal.

No CIA or NSA infrastructure connections, no government server scanning, no third-party personal-data harvesting, no private audio transfer, and no claims that declassified records confer server access.

Deployment requires an explicit, authorized Cloudflare Worker binding and validation before changing live traffic.
