Geometry laboratory

The image-space measurements use relative pixel distances only. Perspective, head rotation, camera optics, eyelid occlusion and reflections can bias them. Set eye A and B in the explorer for inter-center distance and radius comparison. An iris and pupil are only approximately circular in photographs.

Ancient fractions: the Wadjet / heqat model

The six traditional unit fractions associated with the Eye of Horus are mathematical parts, not measured iris segments. The familiar mapping to particular eye parts is historically disputed.

Total: 63/64 = 0.984375. Gap to one whole: 1/64. This is ancient fractional arithmetic, not a diagnostic symmetry score.

Historical measurement notes

Egypt: Heqat unit fractions, temple symbolism, and the Wadjet (restored/protected eye) must be distinguished. Greek/Roman: theories of proportion exist, but a universal “Pythagorean eye ratio” equivalent to modern face landmark calibration is not established. Indian traditions: eyes feature in aesthetics and medical literature, but bodily symbolism is not biometric evidence. Modern: normalized iris imaging is the relevant research method for biometric texture.
