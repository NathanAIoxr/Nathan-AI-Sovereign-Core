# HAT OS and connected hardware

The playable web build implements the realm gates, locally saved catalog, suffix spinner, and the Santa cap / Solstice Crown pair. The following remain product concepts from the source brief.

- **HAT-Core** would be an operating-system kernel with modular package installation and removal, using Nathan’s 1 + 1 = 2 and 1 − 1 = 0 framing. Core milestones are boot, device I/O, signed package installation, and upgrade recovery.
- **Crown UI** would expose Work, Creator, Gaming, and an offline Ghost profile. A native linguistic engine would preserve dictated phonetic spellings and custom suffixes.
- **Hatter Rig** and **Hatette Pocket Slate** are desktop and mobile concepts, with repairable components and physical camera, microphone, and radio controls where hardware can support them.
- **Smart Crown / Brim Band** and **Spinner Deck** would add haptic, rotary, and gesture input. **Forge Studio** would support 3D modeling, layout, beat-making, and level design.
- **Zero-Gate Firewall** and **Mesh-Brim Protocol** are proposed privacy and peer-to-peer layers. Hardware filtering and encrypted mesh communications require separate engineering, threat modeling, and device validation.
- Multiplayer, AR try-on, the community word wall, creator downloads, physical merchandise, and the illustrated manuscripts are follow-on work. The site publishes a 13-volume series map.
