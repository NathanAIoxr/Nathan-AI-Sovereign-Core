# Additive Coin Classification Overlay
Applies Dewey and base-12 identifiers to **every one of the 69 bank positions**, preserves the independently authored **21 Sovereign Coin definitions**, and catalogs **26 external network research targets** from the two May 24, 2026 original emails plus BTC. No project is replaced or merged.

The 69-position archive currently has 42 previously recovered names reported, 22 unresolved identities, and five reserved slots. The exact original 42 identities must be source-reconciled before labeling. The 21 source-defined sovereign coins are stored separately in `sovereign_original_21_coins`. Do not assume they equal the 42 recovered bank identities or count them twice. The 42,000,000-unit supply proposal and 16:1 efficiency target are design claims, not minted or financially verified assets.

Dewey codes 330 (economic categories) and 332 (financial systems) are **local provisional subject tags**. Base-12 identifiers are computed for each slot; numeric positions and Dewey subject codes remain separate. No change to issuance, wallet balances, consensus, or mining state.

Implementation: `src/coin_overlay.mjs`; tests: `node --test src/coin_overlay.test.mjs`. HOLD until an authorized Worker binding and independent checks are confirmed.
