## Research trails
1. Small-scale channel erosion versus Grand Canyon river-incision timescales.
2. Ancient Near Eastern mother goddesses and Asherah's historical context.
3. Biblical maternal imagery (e.g. Isaiah 49:15; 66:13) and the distinction between imagery and a named divine person.
4. Early Christian traditions concerning Sophia, Mary, and other feminine symbolic figures, clearly separating them from each other.
5. Theological development of Trinitarian doctrine and historical evidence for disagreement.

- **SUPPORTED:** long-term river-driven canyon incision.
- **DEBATED:** particular readings of ancient inscriptions.
- **FICTIONAL:** Baby Jesus's stream, a Father's punishment and a Mother's underworld banishment.
