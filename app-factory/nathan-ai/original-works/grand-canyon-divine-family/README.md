### Children's-book cover concept
Title: **HOW BABY JESUS MADE THE GRAND CANYON**

The cover shows the infant/young Jesus from behind, standing safely on a bright heavenly cloud in a modest white wrap, his face and front unseen. A sparkling stream descends from the clouds toward a panoramic Grand Canyon, splitting into luminous branching channels and a winding river. The visual language is playful, wondrous, child-friendly and richly colored. No visible genitalia. Canyon layers, glowing sunlight and clouds are prominent.

### Two separate origin stories
1. **God the Father's Back Porch:** A gigantic divine figure sends a stream from heaven to Earth, and the flowing liquid cuts branching channels in the soil and stone.
2. **The Divine Child and the Missing Mother:** Baby Jesus escapes God the Mother's supervision; his stream makes the Grand Canyon; she disciplines him; the jealous Father banishes the Mother to an underworld.

### Evidence boundary
This is a new creator-authored mythology, **not** a literal account established by geology, Biblical text or church history. The Grand Canyon was formed by geologic uplift and river erosion over millions of years. The resemblance of stream-created small rills to canyon tributaries is a scale analogy, not proof of a divine event.
