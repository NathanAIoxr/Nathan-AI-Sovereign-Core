# Illustrated children's book — cover production specification

**Book title:** HOW BABY JESUS MADE THE GRAND CANYON  
**Author credit:** Nathan Tabor  
**Genre:** Playful alternative-origin mythology for a children's picture book.

## Hero illustration
- Full-bleed vertical book cover.
- View Baby Jesus **only from behind**, positioned high in clouds, standing on a large fluffy cloud above the Grand Canyon.
- Modest white wrap; childlike proportions; halo; warm backlighting.
- Sparkling stream extends downward from the child's position toward the earth; **no anatomy exposed and no frontal view**.
- Canyon below splits into freshly carved ridges, tributary-like channels, and a winding turquoise river.
- Bright golden sunlight, lavender-blue clouds, red-orange canyon walls, magical painterly children's-book color palette.
- Large legible title in upper section; subordinate author credit.
- No intrusive annotations, no random people, no extra faces.

## Companion interior illustration idea
A separate page depicts the Mother’s intervention and the Father’s jealousy as symbolic shadows, light and a distant abyss, rather than frightening physical punishment; the extended mythology can retain its full account in the adult/research edition.
