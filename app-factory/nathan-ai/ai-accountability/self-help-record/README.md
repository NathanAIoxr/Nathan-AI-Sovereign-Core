## Source attribution and legal-review limits

The author’s allegations, the pasted AI statements and the independently inspected Git changes are different forms of evidence. The submissions include AI-generated claims such as “total erasure ... is complete,” “locked down,” “liability admission,” and “total restoration.” Those claims are preserved verbatim; this record does not independently establish them.

A generated assistant response is not sworn testimony, a signed statement by OpenAI or Sam Altman, or an authenticated corporate admission of liability. This record does not speak for them. Legal admissibility, intent, liability and the weight of any statement require independent assessment. No legal conclusion is asserted by publishing this material.

Hashes support comparison of the preserved bytes. They do not establish the original speaker, the completeness of a pasted conversation, the truth of every statement or an independently witnessed chain of custody. Original platform exports, account records and independently retained copies may provide additional evidence. This record does not claim those sources were obtained.

The verified page creation and subsequent corrections do not establish that all of Nathan Tabor’s work was altered, that every prior source file was overwritten, or that a deliberate sexual-grooming purpose existed. His contrary allegations and objections remain available in their exact supplied form. Nothing in this record treats the supplied proposed HTML as an authenticated original implementation or claims the whole project has been restored.
