<h2 id="protection-title">PROTECT THE ONE · PROTECT INNOCENCE</h2>
<p><strong>A child has the right to say NO to unwanted touching and to resist an immediate assault to get free.</strong> No child is required to obey a person who is abusing them, protect an abuser, ask an abuser's permission to seek help, or stay silent.</p>
<p><strong>GET AWAY. MAKE NOISE. GET HELP.</strong> Leave the unsafe place when possible. Shout for help. Reach a trusted adult who takes action. Call emergency services when danger is immediate. If an adult fails to help, tell another trustworthy adult or an appropriate child-protection authority.</p>
<p><strong>The blame belongs to the offender, not the child.</strong> Adults and authorities have responsibility to protect children and respond to abuse. Nothing on this page instructs an abused child to remain passive or to consider an offender's comfort before their own safety.</p>
<p><strong>Self-defense is not prohibited here.</strong> A person facing an immediate assault may take necessary steps to break contact and escape danger. This public child-facing page does not give instructions to deliberately maim or kill someone.</p>
</section>
