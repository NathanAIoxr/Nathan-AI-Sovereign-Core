## Original-word restoration rules

- The `author/` directory contains direct author statements exactly as supplied, without replacing profanity, spelling, capitalization, or allegations.

- `editorial/` and `EDITORIAL-CORRECTIONS.md` contain separate assistant-written copyediting and admitted errors; neither modifies an original.
- Where a speaker's precise original utterance was not supplied in an independently recoverable source, no fabricated verbatim reconstruction is included.
- Reported events and names are not asserted to establish criminal guilt or corporate-directed concealment. The complainant's accusations remain fully preserved as accusations.
