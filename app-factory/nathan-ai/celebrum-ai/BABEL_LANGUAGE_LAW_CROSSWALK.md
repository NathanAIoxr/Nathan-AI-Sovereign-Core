## Bidirectional connection

- **LAW → LANGUAGE/CELEBRUM:** Rule of law limits authority; liberty preserves speech and consent; property protects original words and contracts; wrongdoing invokes evidence, victim protection, and lawful accountability.
- **LANGUAGE/CELEBRUM → LAW:** Orthography helps examine exact character changes; syntax identifies who acts and who receives consequences; prescriptive usage examines language gatekeeping; punctuation and printing demonstrate changes of clause/meaning.
- **ANCHORED:** actual sources, dates, jurisdiction, permission, citations, observed effects, reproducible tests.
- **UNANCHORED:** Babel and double-mindedness as metaphor, ant-gods fiction, alternate thought models and creative language experiments. These are labeled and never automatically promoted into established historical facts.
- **REANCHOR:** document later verified evidence by linked record, without overwriting author originals.

**Four legal roots × four linguistic families × two directions × two modes = 64 analytical pathways.** The 64 pathways are structured examination slots, not 64 demonstrated causal historical links.

## Four language families

1. Orthography — letters, spelling heuristics and exceptions.
2. Syntax — sentence roles, agreement, tense, ordering, and meaning.
3. Prescriptive usage — double negatives, split infinitives, preposition stranding, register and social judgment.
4. Punctuation/printing — negation, punctuation, capitalization, the written source record and publication authority.
