## Design and implementation mechanics
1. Browser `SpeechSynthesisVoice.localService` filtering and bounded speech chunks; only actually installed languages/dialects are supported.
2. Explicit on-device speech-recognition availability checks; no automatic online fallback.
3. User-initiated local recordings; consent controls; short WAV exports and noise amplitude gating; originals retained.
4. Native Python localhost server with limited asset allowlist, strict text/audio limits, eSpeak NG and whisper.cpp only when independently installed; no automatic downloads.
5. Acoustic environment sketches (cave, water, wind, fictional tonal sketches); not physical frequency hardware or verified historical voices.
6. Family voice audio playback only with rights confirmation; no real-person voice cloning in this implementation.
7. Living Ears (24 acoustic views), Living Eyes (48/96/192/384 geometric views), ONE Sealed Voice Box/Offloader remain **independent**.
8. The separate ONE language catalog's 7,918 indexed languages are **not** 7,918 working synthesized voices.
