## Account / resource recovery checklist

1. **Find the actual Voicebox implementation.** Identify an owner-controlled file, app name, encrypted container, endpoint or signed custody record. Concept names in a source archive do not establish an operational, password-unlockable vault.
2. **Keep credentials off public systems.** Do not enter passwords, recovery seeds, encryption keys, voiceprints, bank IDs or private records in this public GitHub module. If a password was exposed in a chat or other non-secret location, change it in the real supported system where possible and secure any associated recovery method.
3. **Open privately through a trusted original tool.** Determine whether the content is a text registry, a mock/fictional balance, a test-coin ledger, a software prototype, a cryptocurrency wallet, a regulated financial account, or signed asset-custody evidence.
4. **Verify the financial meaning of any amount.** Record the asset type, issuer/custodian, chain/network, account or wallet identifier (kept private), dated evidence, control/withdrawal rights, units, market value source, applicable restrictions and liabilities. Amounts printed by a simulator or claimed in a prior note are not verification.
5. **Separate source, reported claim and verified results.** The public source tree does not include a verified Voicebox balance or a working authenticated service. Do not represent a reported private amount as liquid assets, realized revenue, cryptocurrency mined or transferable funds without independent confirmation.
6. **Keep personal life events owner-controlled.** The browser life map stores no personal life note by default; user-entered note content is held only in the current page session and downloaded only when explicitly selected. The public source index includes no private biographical or account detail.
