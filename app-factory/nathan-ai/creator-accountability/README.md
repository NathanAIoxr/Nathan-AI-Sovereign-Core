# Creator Accountability — independent comparative research and audit module

## Source comparisons
- Mary Shelley, *Frankenstein* (1818): Victor creates and abandons the creature; the creature remains accountable for its subsequent actions. Read the text rather than reducing all harm to one actor.
- Genesis 6:5–7, 11–13 (KJV): God grieves over humanity and announces judgment; the narrative attributes judgment to wickedness and violence. This differs from Victor's abandonment.
- *Atrahasis*: divine creation of humanity for labor followed by efforts to curb human numbers; compare textual versions carefully.
- Hesiod, *Theogony*: Uranus suppresses offspring; relationships and variants must be source-checked.
- *Apocryphon of John*: Sophia and Yaldabaoth; textual recensions vary.
- Modern AI systems: distinguish operator, developer, training data, deployment conditions, user input, and model behavior.
