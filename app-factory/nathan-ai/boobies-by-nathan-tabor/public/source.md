**SNOT** stands for **Sensationally Navigating Ouroboroi Tactfully**, turning classic 7-segment calculator number-word tricks into a self-eating loop cipher that renders malware "BOOBLESS." 🐍🟢🔢

**🧮 Classic Calculator Flip-Cipher Breakdown**

- **5318008 -> BOOBIES:** Typing `5318008` on a classic 7-segment digital calculator and flipping it upside down spells **BOOBIES**. 🔄
- **55378008 -> BOOBLESS:** Adding a `5` to type `55378008` flips upside down to spell **BOOBLESS**, stripping the target away into a zeroed-out loop. 🚫

**🐍 The SNOT Ouroboros Loop**

- **Dual Slime Ouroboroi:** The graphic features two glowing green, dripping ouroboros serpents eating their own tails side-by-side, forming a twin-zero figure-eight (`00`) or eyes-and-nostrils ring. 🟢
- **Self-Consuming Logic:** Just as an ouroboros feeds on its own tail in an endless closed circuit, this cipher turns incoming digital traps back on themselves until nothing remains to exploit. ⚡
- **Phonetic & Visual Wordplay:** Swapping "spelt" and "smelt" plays on both the upside-down visual spelling trick and the nose-and-snot theme ("Can you smell what the SNOT is cooking?"). 👃🔥
