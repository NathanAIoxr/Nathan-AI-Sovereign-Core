## Edition / epistemic distinction

A manuscript quotation is source text. An earlier assistant's assertion that a document was finished is **an assertion contained in source text**, not a verification of the document's existence or exact length. Research prose drafted in this new branch is **assistant-proposed editorial content**, not retroactively assigned as a verbatim statement from the owner.

The following category names are kept separate for machine search and human reading:

1. verified_code_check — observed test pass at named commit/workflow;
2. versioned_repository_registry — content and counts visible in a specific GitHub file;
3. present_in_uploaded_excerpt — actually printed in the provided text;
4. ancient_or_religious_tradition — theological, symbolic or contemplative claim with named source;
5. independently_supported_anatomy — basic physical anatomy backed by anatomy references;
6. traditional_healing_claim_not_established — a historically described practice without a proven specific therapeutic mechanism;
7. assistant_editorial_proposal — new prose, index or suggested analogy;
8. source_claim_unverified — prior assistant claim not established by available primary file;
9. technical_specification_not_deployed — a catalog or architecture, not running devices.

## Contradictions, caveats, missing evidence

- **40 spinal entries ≠ 40 vertebrae.** The source's own description treats 33 studies plus seven repeated cervical *reflections* as editorial.
- **206 + 40 = 246** is a publishing index, not a new anatomical number.
- **Seven colors or seven diatonic notes** are editorial arrangements, not established chakra-light or gland-audio frequencies.
- The **396–963 Hz sequence** is a distinct modern spiritual numerical tone arrangement and should not be silently labeled C–B at standard tuning.
- A **human time interval** is not equivalent to a count of named dates; a **Year 0** claim depends on the selected historical calendar, whereas a zero-based array index is a software implementation convention.
- **114 = 7+21+86** and **114 = 112+2** happen to share a sum. This alone does not make their underlying terms equivalent.
- **108 technical roles**, **108 three-layer positional mappings** and **108 workable yogic centers** are independent sources and must remain distinct identities.
- Anatomical endocrine glands have actual locations/functions; the symbolic seven stations do not prove a precise one-to-one gland mapping.
