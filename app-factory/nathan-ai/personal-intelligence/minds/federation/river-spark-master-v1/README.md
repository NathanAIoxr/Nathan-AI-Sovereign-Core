1. **Original quotations remain exact within the supplied excerpt.** All newly drafted chapters are explicitly marked assistant expansion for creator review.
2. **Evidence categories cannot auto-promote**: established anatomy; traditional teaching; modern spiritual reconstruction; literary metaphor; proposed software architecture; independently verified running code; unverified other-assistant output.
3. **A common number does not create common identity**: 108 units, 108 yoga centers and 108 crosswalk rows remain three unrelated domains unless separately evidenced.

5. **No false completion**: older PDFs/Word files are mentioned in the uploaded text but were not provided as the files themselves. The present compilation cannot validate their pagination or recover unseen pages.
