# Historical Root-Source Register | 1600–2026

Compiled 2026-11-11. **101 unique web-linked documents or research pages.** Each distinct URL is counted once. Not every document is a primary source: source type is explicitly marked. Sources are grouped by subject and the historical dates they document.

**Evidence limit:** The cited records substantiate documented archive moves, losses, restrictions, misdating/re-dating, non-consensual human-remains collections, and scientific dating. They do not collectively establish a coordinated fabrication of 300 years of chronology or verify the alleged 1909 Egyptian cave excavation. This register does not falsely claim to include 101 independent documents proving that proposition.

**Independent cross-check:** Phantom Time is contradicted by physical tree-ring/radiocarbon markers at 774/775 and 993 CE, eclipse calculations, coins, and non-European chronologies. A real calendar-number adjustment (e.g., 1752) does not mean time was lost.

## Chronological foundations
