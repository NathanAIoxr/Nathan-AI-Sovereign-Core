## Unifying principle

The owner keeps the original. Quotes generate investigative questions, never automatic verdicts. The system looks at power, risk, incentives, responsibilities, evidence, counterevidence, protective action and follow-through. It can expose failures without using rejected claims or institutional status as a substitute for proof.

## Four mechanisms and their operational tests

### Perverse incentives
What choices does the reward structure actually encourage?
Signals: reward distribution, metrics and quotas, externalized work, quiet competence penalized.
Tools: reward/penalty register, incentive matrix, counterfactual test.

### Asymmetric risk
Who receives value, and who carries loss, legal exposure, labor or danger?
Signals: uncompensated labor, liability transfer, benefit/risk split, vulnerable persons bearing cost.
Tools: stakeholder cost ledger, risk allocation, safeguarding escalation.

### Leverage loss
Who controls resources, obligations, knowledge, price and exit options?
Signals: terms not recorded, unilateral concessions, reciprocity expectations, missing audit trails.
Tools: commitment and terms ledger, permissioned handoff, owner veto.

### Moral hazard
Who is shielded from consequences, and what incentives does that create?
Signals: repeated misconduct, unaccountable rescue, concealed harm, inadequate oversight.
Tools: repeat-pattern monitor, independent audit, accountability workflow.

## Interpretive and safety exceptions

Avoid turning generalizations about charity, victims, law, government, or violence into policies. Specific review flags:
- #15: Sweeping dismissal of management; do not generalize to all individuals.
- #16: Sweeping dehumanizing comparison; not an empirical rule.
- #19: Quoted religious proverb; do not weaponize as a reason to abandon people.
- #21: Blanket charity/dependency assertion is unsupported; evaluate specific programs and harms.
- #25: A rescuer does not automatically assume indefinite legal or moral responsibility.
- #28: Do not deny due process or assume allegations establish guilt.
- #30: Never apply as a reason to withhold emergency aid or victim protection.
- #37: Demeaning characterization, not a testable universal law.
- #41: Hyperbolic saying involving death; never operationalize as a confidentiality method.
- #44: Avoid blaming victims of coercion, exploitation or abuse.
- #45: Treat change in behavior as an empirical question, not biologically predetermined.
- #52: Description of coercive power, not a moral endorsement.
- #61: Violent historical saying; do not operationalize as advocacy or instructions.
- #63: Political critique, not a universally verified empirical law.
- #65: Useful privacy warning, but not literally true for every free service.
- #68: Violence-associated historical slogan; do not convert into operating policy.
- #69: Not a license to ignore law or safety rules.
- #71: Avoid recommending abandonment of vulnerable people.
- #74: Literary/philosophical expression, not an empirical finding about everyone.
- #77: Gresham’s law requires specific monetary conditions; the slogan is not generally universal.
- #79: Murphy’s Law is an engineering heuristic, not a prediction that every failure happens.
- #85: Not suitable for situations that require testimony, transparency, or reporting.
- #87: Militarized maxim; not an innocence-protection or conflict-resolution directive.
- #89: Survival aphorism, not a clinical or universal instruction.
- #98: False generalization that can blame fraud victims.
- #99: Satire of coercive management; not conduct to imitate.
- #101: Fictional narrative maxim; not a fixed trajectory for real people.

## Working pipeline

- **CERO SOURCE:** Import original narrative, four mechanisms and 101 sayings; hash, freeze and version without rewriting
- **UNO CLASSIFY:** Tag incentive mechanism, scripture cross-reference, source type, attribution and exception flags
- **DOS ANALYZE:** Build stakeholder maps, testable hypotheses, evidence/counterevidence and funding/hand-off chain
- **BUILD:** Generate source-backed lessons, case studies, report cards and issue registers with owner approval
- **CLEAR:** Remove unsupported derived allegations or duplicate work items without deleting original evidence
- **EXPORT:** Generate reviewed portable JSON packet and ZIP archive with manifest hashes
- **RESTORE:** Validate exact recovery across another browser profile/device before considering sync dependable

## Synchronization reality

The existing merged MAKE AI YOURS PWA stores original files in the local Creator Vault and derived notes separately. Owner-approved JSON handoffs, ZIP export/restore, and optional local read-only MCP are documented. Live cloud/device/provider synchronization is NOT established. This module is a new standalone schema and visual explorer; integrating it into the repository remains a separate engineering step.

## Safeguarding and ethical proof checks

Separate victims from people accused of harming them; do not use moral hazard to blame victims. Prioritize immediate lawful assistance and reporting if a person is in danger. Require evidence, contrary evidence, corroboration, time and owner approval before formal conclusions; allow rejection or revision of derived claims while preserving original testimony and files.
