Local MCP bridge for laptop AI clients

The included mcp-server.cjs is read-only and runs locally through standard input/output. Select a folder of your originals on the laptop and explicitly configure an MCP-capable client. Browser IndexedDB does not automatically synchronize to this folder; export or copy owner-selected originals first.
