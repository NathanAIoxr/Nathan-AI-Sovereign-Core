03 / The evolution

Beyond break-fix. Beyond MSP.

Generation 01

Traditional IT

Repair the issue after something breaks. Typically project-based, hourly, and dependent on manual support.

Generation 02

Managed Services

Proactive monitoring, recurring maintenance, response SLAs, managed security and support subscriptions.

Generation 03

DMSP + DIT

A unified digital operating model emphasizing verified outcomes, customer transparency, defined permissions and accountable automation.

Current release: usable public catalog, app shell and downloadable planning tools. It is not yet a staffed managed-services provider or a self-service remote management agent. Customer operations require secure identity, contracts and authorized onboarding.
