Historical record

Artane Industrial School

Ireland’s 2009 Ryan Report records a former resident’s testimony that he was struck on the hand in the 1940s for writing left-handed. Another former resident described punishments for errors in handwriting and answers in class.

Ryan Report, volume 1, §7.271–272

Sisters of Nazareth residential institutions

The Scottish Child Abuse Inquiry found that children suffered physical abuse in homes run by the Sisters of Nazareth from 1933 to 1984. The inquiry heard evidence of adults striking children and of punishments and humiliation for bedwetting.

Scottish Child Abuse Inquiry findings, 2019
