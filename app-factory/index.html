Proof of actual change

  1. Label: Who uses it, and when?
  2. Mission: Which danger or injustice was named?
  3. Mechanism: Who or what enables the harm?
  4. Action: What did the figure actually do?
  5. Scope: Whose lives changed, for how long?
  6. Evidence: Contemporary accounts, archaeology, law, archives, or a fictional text?
  7. Outcome: Rescue, reform, regime change, root eliminated, recurrence, or unresolved?
  8. Aftermath: Is innocent life safe? What happens afterward?

Reference outcome: Smallpox provides a documented example of global disease eradication through collective action. This does not prove every possible harm can be permanently eliminated.
