        Ancient Naval Warfare

        Polybius describes the Roman corvus as a boarding bridge during the First Punic War. Play Roman harbor, Trireme Tow, and Corvus Crossing. The corvus exhibit shows a separate boarding bridge. Polybius, Histories 1.22

        Castle Walls

        The Met catalogues a Japanese kagi nawa grappling hook, dated to about 1615–1868. Play Lantern Ring, Stone Wall Ascent, and Moat Swing. The Met

        Wild West Frontier

        Cowboys used a lariat in cattle work. Play Stagecoach, Saloon Chandelier, and Runaway Cattle. National Cowboy & Western Heritage Museum

        Modern Rescue

        Secure a rescue sled, cross a fictional rooftop gap, and recover a hiker at the cliff ledge.
