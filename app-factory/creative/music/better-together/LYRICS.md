# BETTER THAN ME — BETTER THAN YOU
## BETTER TOGETHER · A Song of Humble Pride

**Concept and original philosophy:** Nathan Tabor
**Format:** Original call-and-response lyrics, holiday arrangement concept
**Visual concept:** Christmas × Halloween × Thanksgiving merged into one extraordinary celebration

> These are the original draft lyrics associated with the song concept. The decorative staff on the artwork is illustrative; no verified sheet-music composition or performance recording is asserted.

### Verse 1 — I Can Do Better
**VOICE ONE:** I can do anything better than you!

**VOICE TWO:** Well, I can do better than you!

**TOGETHER:**

But that don't mean I'm better than you,  
And that don't mean you're better than me.  
You've got a gift that I haven't got,  
I've got a gift you haven't seen!

### Chorus — The Better We Become
I'll teach you everything I know,  
And you can teach me what you do.  
I'll help you rise above my best,  
And I'll keep learning from you!

You don't have to lose for me to win,  
We don't have to fight to grow.  
The greatest victory we can claim  
Is helping someone else outgrow!

### Verse 2 — No Conditions
**VOICE ONE:** What if I don't teach you what I know?

**VOICE TWO:** I'll teach you anyway!

**VOICE ONE:** What if I become better than you?

**VOICE TWO:** Then I'll celebrate that day!

**TOGETHER:**

'Cause knowledge isn't something we lose  
By giving it away.  
And when you climb beyond my reach,  
I'll cheer you all the way!

### Bridge — Love Yourself
Love yourself — there's nothing wrong with that!  
Know your worth — and let me know mine!  
You can shine without stealing my light,  
Two stars can shine at the same time!

Confidence doesn't require contempt,  
And pride doesn't have to control.  
You can love the person you are  
Without putting down another soul!

### Final Chorus — Better Together
I can do better!  
**You can do better!**

I can teach you!  
**You can teach me!**

And if you won't teach me,  
**I'll STILL teach you!**

Because the point was never  
Who's above or who's below.

**I WANT YOU TO BE BETTER THAN ME!**

**AND I WANT TO BE BETTER THAN YOU!**

**AND THEN WE'LL BOTH HAVE SOMETHING NEW TO TEACH THE WORLD!**

---

**Central message:** Strength without superiority. Compete to improve; teach without demanding something in return. Respect and consent remain unconditional.
