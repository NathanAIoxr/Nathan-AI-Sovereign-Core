The central promise

I’ll teach you how to be better than me if you’ll teach me how to be better than you — and even if you don’t teach me, I’ll still teach you.
Meaning and sovereign principles

1 + 1 = 2: two capable individuals can collaborate without losing their own identities. 1 − 1 = 0: permission can be withdrawn; friendship, human dignity and autonomy remain worthy of respect.

Humble pride: know your own worth and celebrate others. Meek pride: self-mastery and deliberate restraint. Competition: lift each other higher, not push each other down.
