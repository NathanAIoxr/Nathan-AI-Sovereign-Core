## Three connected layers

### 1. LITERAL — The Enterprise
A commercial umbrella for independent products in software, user-controlled AI, publishing, educational tools, interactive worlds, creative production, licensing, and collaborations.

Potential divisions (proposals, not registered subsidiaries):
- GODSALTER TECHNOLOGIES — apps, games, and software
- GODSALTER WORLDS — sandbox worlds, storytelling and fictional mythologies
- GODSALTER PRESS — literature and multimedia
- GODSALTER ACADEMY — research methods, creativity and media literacy
- GODSALTER LABS — prototypes, testing and developer tools

### 2. FIGURATIVE — The Transformation
The altar is a metaphor for deliberate change, not an actual claim to divinity. Examine inherited assumptions, separate evidence from interpretation, preserve consent, and build a better world through real actions.

### 3. MYTHOLOGICAL — The Universe
A fictional multiverse where players create worlds and explore gods, goddesses, pantheons, symbols, realms, stories, opposing powers, and mythic councils. Clearly label fictional lore separately from documented history.

## Platform concept
- One main enterprise directory and identity control center
- Independent databases for each product, world, and business division
- Cross-project index with linked references, not automatic merging of private records
- Evidence tags: verified, user-authored, fictional, proposed, unresolved
- Ownership, audit trails, role-based permissions, export and deletion
- Human approval before publishing, payment, data sharing, or external action
