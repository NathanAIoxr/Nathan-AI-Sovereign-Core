- **Matthew 11:19 (KJV):** “The Son of man came eating and drinking, and they say, Behold a man gluttonous, and a winebibber…” The text *reports what others say*, not a narrator's affirmation that Jesus was a drunkard. https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+11%3A19&version=KJV
- **Mark 3:21 (KJV):** “...for they said, He is beside himself.” This reports others' reaction; it is not a medical diagnosis. https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark+3%3A21&version=KJV

- **Luke 23:2 (KJV):** People accuse Jesus before Pilate of misleading the nation and opposing tribute to Caesar. This documents an accusation, but does not use the modern legal category “felon.” https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+23%3A2&version=KJV

The cited passages do not prove drug use, a psychiatric disorder, or a modern felony classification. The authorial framing must remain distinct from the sourced wording.
