# Autonomous registry audit and coin evidence

Cloudflare scheduled handler performs a bounded inventory audit and persists a receipt. Cron target is every 15 minutes once trigger is installed. No infinite loops, unauthorized trading, mining, or invented prices. Coin prices remain nullable until verified from a market source. AUDIT_TOKEN must be configured as a secret for manual audit POST. The 64-coin registry includes proposed/unissued concepts; registration does not prove issuance, supply, or value. Registered bot-agent pairs are not continuously executing processes.
