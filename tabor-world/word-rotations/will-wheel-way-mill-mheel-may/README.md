# WILL / WHEEL / WAY ↔ MILL / MHEEL / MAY

**CONTINUE BUILDING** — a bidirectional twin-triplet language, game and digital-hardware layer.

## Six independent words and three twins

| Original W | Mirrored M | Separate meaning and build branch |
|---|---|---|
| WILL | MILL | agency and intention ↔ mill mechanism, corn grinding, grits |
| WHEEL | MHEEL | wheel rotation ↔ **my heel** phonetic joke, door/heel puzzle |
| WAY | MAY | direction and method ↔ permission (“May I?”), possibility, May showers and spring flowers |

## One, twins, triplets and the six-part system

- **One:** each of WILL, WHEEL, WAY, MILL, MHEEL, MAY is its own word, module and creative identity.
- **Twins:** WILL↔MILL, WHEEL↔MHEEL, WAY↔MAY. Transformation changes the leading W to M; reverse changes M to W.
- **Triplets:** WILL/WHEEL/WAY and MILL/MHEEL/MAY. Each triplet runs independently.
- **Unified six:** cross-links connect both triplets while preserving separate sources and outputs.

## Expansion into apps, games, physical/digital hardware and stories

1. **WILL** — intention, decision, consent and user-controlled actions.
2. **WHEEL** — gears, rotation, navigation, vehicle dynamics and the Tabor Track Baller.
3. **WAY** — paths, workflows, routing and wayfinding.
4. **MILL** — millstone and grinder digital twins, corn-to-grits simulation, milling physics.
5. **MHEEL** — heel/door interaction, pressure/contact puzzle, phonetic knock-knock story.
6. **MAY** — calendar, weather and seasons, permission-language learning, “May I?” interaction.

**Knock-knock branch:** “Knock, knock.” “Who’s there?” “My heel.” “My heel who?” “My heel is stuck in the door!” This is a creative phonetic joke, not a historical etymology.

**May branch:** May showers, spring flowers, “May I?”, “I may”, and the month of May become distinct semantic nodes connected to one word.

## Infrastructure integration

- **Universal Finger Physics / Tabor Track Baller:** virtual wheel and mill mechanics.
- **Tabor Digital Hardware:** wheel, gear, door, contact, motor and mill simulation.
- **Library of Tabor:** roots, word histories and user-invented wordplay stored as different evidence classes.
