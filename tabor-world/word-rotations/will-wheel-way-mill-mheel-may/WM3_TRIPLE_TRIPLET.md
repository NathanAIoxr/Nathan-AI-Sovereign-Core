# TABOR W → M → 3 — TRIPLE TRIPLET

**CONTINUE BUILDING.** This extends, without replacing, WILL/WHEEL/WAY ↔ MILL/MHEEL/MAY.

| W family | M family | 3 family |
|---|---|---|
| WILL | MILL | **3ILL** |
| WHEEL | MHEEL | **3HEEL** |
| WAY | MAY | **3AY** |

## New independent nodes

- **3ILL:** three different types of ills, with three separately configurable child branches.
- **3HEEL:** three heels = three feet; physical and digital foot-contact, motion and door-puzzle branches.
- **3AY:** **Thug · Pirate · Spartan** — head node of **SUP**, with three independent child identities.

## Composition modes

- Nine standalone identities.
- Three horizontal triplets: W family, M family, 3 family.
- Three vertical triplets: WILL/MILL/3ILL, WHEEL/MHEEL/3HEEL, WAY/MAY/3AY.
- Three reversible symbol transformations: W→M, M→3, 3→W.
- One unified nine-node network, preserving the earlier six-node system.
- SUP remains an independent parent connected to 3AY and its Thug/Pirate/Spartan triad.

The W/M/3 relationship is a user-defined symbolic rotation and character transformation; it is not a claim that every font physically rotates W into 3.

## Build targets

Independent 3ILL, 3HEEL and 3AY apps; nine-node rotation game; digital foot-contact simulation; SUP head-node character system; Tabor World integration; tests, assets and verification receipts.
