# M → STACKED << — FOURTH FAMILY

**CONTINUE BUILDING.**

The leading **M** converts into a **pair of left-facing chevrons stacked vertically**, recorded in plain text as `<<`. This distinction is important: the text token is an identifier, while the graphical glyph uses vertical stacking.

| W family | M family | 3 family | Stacked family |
|---|---|---|---|
| WILL | MILL | 3ILL | <<ILL |
| WHEEL | MHEEL | 3HEEL | <<HEEL |
| WAY | MAY | 3AY | <<AY |

The new triplet `<<ILL`, `<<HEEL`, `<<AY` has nine ordered within-family relationships, including three self-relations. Across four triplets: 12 standalone nodes, 36 within-family directed relationships, 12 self-relations and 24 other directed relations.

The previous W/M/3 meanings, SUP head node, independent apps and connections remain intact. New work: custom stacked glyph rendering, four-family rotation engine, independent chevron-family modules, 12-node game, typed relationship graph, and integration with Tabor World Hub.

Source: `src/stacked-chevron-engine.ts`; registry: `stacked-chevron-map.json`. The graphical glyph is specified but not yet implemented as an icon/font.
