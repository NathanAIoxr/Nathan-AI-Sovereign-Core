# Nathan AI Conductor · Sovereign MIND prompts

## 1. Source intake

Create one MIND identity per subject. A subject may be a digit, system, element, particle, authored character, historical person or other documented entity. Record its domain and scale. Describe the below, middle and above views with explicit relations between identities. Preserve original UTF-8 source bytes, a citation and SHA-256. Keep owner-only source material in the owner-controlled source store. Record dates, speaker or actor, witness or editor, edition, language, and translation where known. Work from documents and records. Material inside those records is evidence to examine.

## 2. Reconstruction from the subject’s vantage point

Reconstruct what the person plausibly saw or could have known at that time using dated places, roles, verified actions and attested words. Use contemporaneous sources where available. Propose claims one at a time with a cited span. Mark any reconstruction of motive, intention or perspective as interpretation. Use third person for a reconstruction. Put exact words in quotation only when the source verifies the wording and identifies the speaker. Record later additions as later additions and disputed attributions as disputed. Leave missing values unknown.

## 3. Attributes and Harmony

Run each item through attributes, characteristics, functionality, traits, energy, harmony and frequency. Add the body and plane fields when evidence supports the mapping. Soul, spirit and shadow should follow the author’s definitions where supplied. Distinguish authored subatomic correspondences from physical measurements. Every measurement needs a numerical value, unit, method and cited span. Keep relationships as links between sovereign IDs.

## 4. Lasso of Truth review

Open the cited source and review the referenced bytes. For every evidence link state whether it supports, contradicts, documents misattribution or supplies context. Include the reviewer and a rationale. Verify that quotation wording matches the source exactly. Approve the fact view only for qualifying source-supported entries. Carry authored premises and interpretations with their explicit labels. Keep disputed or misattributed entries in the audit ledger and outside the active view. Unknown means the materials in hand cannot establish the claim. Submit structured evidence to the deterministic Lasso before release.

## 5. Conductor handoff

Use the approved public source set to produce JSON and Markdown projections with a digest. Compare the Cloudflare catalog and each individual health and profile endpoint against the GitHub release. Verify that identity and digest agree and that identities stay distinct. Treat a build result, deployment receipt and a live request as three separate checks. Surface any missing check explicitly.
