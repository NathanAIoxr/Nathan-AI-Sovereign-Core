**Timeline Before the 2008 Plea Deal**

- **August 1996 — Initial FBI Report:** Artist Maria Farmer reported Jeffrey Epstein and Ghislaine Maxwell to the FBI for sexual assault and child pornography. The FBI did not open an investigation at that time.[[1](https://www.justsecurity.org/119137/timeline-jeffrey-epstein-ghislaine-maxwell/)]
- **March 2005 — Palm Beach Police Investigation:** The Palm Beach Police Department opened a criminal investigation after the stepmother of a 14-year-old girl reported that Epstein had paid her stepdaughter for a massage and sexually abused her. Police identified dozens of underage girls with matching accounts. [[1](https://oversightdemocrats.house.gov/imo/media/doc/the_price_of_non-prosecution.pdf)]
- **May 2006 — State Grand Jury Indictment & FBI Involvement:** Palm Beach Police Chief Michael Reiter pushed for severe charges, but the Palm Beach County State Attorney's Office took the case to a state grand jury, which indicted Epstein on a single count of felony solicitation of prostitution. Dissatisfied with the leniency of the state charge, Chief Reiter referred the case to the FBI, which opened a federal investigation codenamed "Operation Leap Year". [[1](https://www.npr.org/2020/11/12/934265386/jeffrey-epsteins-former-prosecutors-used-poor-judgment-in-deal-doj-says), [2](https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/explosive-report-drags-another-president-075723262.html), [3](https://www.britannica.com/topic/The-Epstein-Files-A-Timeline), [4](https://www.justsecurity.org/119137/timeline-jeffrey-epstein-ghislaine-maxwell/)]
- **May 2007 — Draft Federal Indictment:** Assistant U.S. Attorney Marie Villafaña in the Southern District of Florida drafted a 53-page, 60-count federal indictment against Epstein, charging him with sex trafficking of minors and enticement of minors to engage in illegal sexual activity. [[1](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/2025/12/17/jeffrey-epstein-files-records-timeline/87716818007/), [2](https://www.britannica.com/topic/The-Epstein-Files-A-Timeline)]
- **Summer 2007 — Defense Lobbying & Secret Negotiations:** Epstein assembled a high-powered defense team including Kenneth Starr, Jay Lefkowitz (both of whom had worked alongside U.S. Attorney Alexander Acosta at the law firm Kirkland & Ellis and served in Republican administrations), and Alan Dershowitz. Defense attorneys bypassed line prosecutors to negotiate directly with Acosta and petitioned senior officials at the Bush Justice Department in Washington to shut down the federal prosecution. [[1](https://abcnews.com/US/key-takeaways-justice-department-review-jeffrey-epstein-sweetheart/story?id=74222922), [2](https://www.britannica.com/topic/The-Epstein-Files-A-Timeline)]
- **September 24, 2007 — Signing of the Non-Prosecution Agreement:** Acosta and Epstein’s attorneys signed the secret NPA, halting the federal indictment. Following the signing, Epstein’s lawyers continued lobbying senior DOJ officials in Washington—including the Criminal Division and the Office of the Deputy Attorney General—for nearly nine months to try to reduce the terms even further before entering a plea. [[1](https://www.wral.com/archive/22120008/), [2](https://abcnews.com/US/key-takeaways-justice-department-review-jeffrey-epstein-sweetheart/story?id=74222922), [3](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/2025/12/17/jeffrey-epstein-files-records-timeline/87716818007/)]

**Specific Terms of the 2007–2008 Deal**

- **No Federal Prosecution:** The U.S. Attorney's Office for the Southern District of Florida agreed to drop its federal investigation and file no federal sex-trafficking charges against Epstein.
- **Blanket Immunity for Co-Conspirators:** The agreement granted federal immunity from prosecution to four named female assistants (Sarah Kellen, Adriana Ross, Lesley Groff, and Nadia Marcinkova) as well as "any potential co-conspirators" who may have recruited or abused minors.
- **State-Level Guilty Plea:** On June 30, 2008, Epstein pleaded guilty in Florida state court to two state charges: one count of felony solicitation of prostitution and one count of procuring a minor for prostitution.
- **Sentence and Work Release:** Epstein was sentenced to 18 months in the Palm Beach County stockade followed by 12 months of house arrest. He served just under 13 months. Under a work-release arrangement, he was permitted to leave the jail for up to 12 hours a day, six days a week, to work at his private office in West Palm Beach, where he continued meeting with young women.
- **Sex Offender Registration & Civil Restitution:** Epstein was required to register as a sex offender and agreed not to contest liability in civil lawsuits filed by a specific list of victims identified by federal prosecutors, provided those victims sought damages only under a specific federal statute.
- **Mandatory Secrecy Clause:** At the demand of Epstein's defense team, federal prosecutors included a confidentiality clause keeping the NPA sealed from the public and concealed from the victims. Prosecutors sent letters to victims stating the case was "still under investigation" even after the deal was signed. [[1](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/jeffrey-epstein-plea-deal-jail-term-doj-files/), [2](https://www.justice.gov/opr/page/file/1336471/dl), [3](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/a-timeline-of-the-jeffrey-epstein-investigation-and-the-fight-to-make-the-governments-files-public), [4](https://www.oklahoman.com/story/news/politics/2025/08/05/epstein-plea-agreement-obama-fact-check-markwayne-mullin/85528703007/), [5](https://www.wral.com/archive/22120008/), [6](https://www.britannica.com/topic/The-Epstein-Files-A-Timeline), [7](https://oversightdemocrats.house.gov/imo/media/doc/the_price_of_non-prosecution.pdf), [8](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/2025/12/17/jeffrey-epstein-files-records-timeline/87716818007/)]

**Key Officials During the Bush Administration**

- **President:** George W. Bush
- **U.S. Attorneys General:** Alberto Gonzales (served until September 2007, when the NPA was signed) and Michael Mukasey (served from November 2007 through the June 2008 guilty plea).
- **U.S. Attorney for the Southern District of Florida:** Alexander Acosta (appointed by George W. Bush; directly approved and signed the NPA).
- **Additional Developments in Released Files:** Reporting on the 2025–2026 document releases and congressional testimony has highlighted ongoing scrutiny into the communications between Epstein's defense team and senior Bush DOJ officials in Washington during the 2007–2008 review process, as well as Acosta's later statement during his 2017 Trump transition vetting that he had been told Epstein "belonged to intelligence". Meanwhile, a coalition of 17 state attorneys general and members of Congress continue to push for the **Epstein Files Transparency Act II** to compel the release of additional internal DOJ communications and unredacted records that remain withheld. [[1](https://www.lawcommentary.com/articles/17-attorneys-general-sue-doj-epstein-files), [2](https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/explosive-report-drags-another-president-075723262.html), [3](https://www.wral.com/archive/22120008/), [4](https://www.oklahoman.com/story/news/politics/2025/08/05/epstein-plea-agreement-obama-fact-check-markwayne-mullin/85528703007/)]
