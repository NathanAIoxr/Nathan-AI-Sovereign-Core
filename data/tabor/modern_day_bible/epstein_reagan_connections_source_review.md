**The strongest documented link from the Reagan era to Epstein’s 2007 deal is Kenneth Starr.** The Iran-Contra lead runs through Adnan Khashoggi. His role in the arms sales is documented, but the sources I reviewed give no dated record tying Epstein’s work for Khashoggi to a specific arms transaction. I found no direct Reagan–Epstein or Meese–Epstein relationship in those records.
