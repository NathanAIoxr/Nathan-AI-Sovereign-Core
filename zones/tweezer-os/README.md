# TWEEZER-OS — HOLD STILL … TWEEZERS … CAN YOU GET IT OUT?

Independent **single-player iPhone arcade game**, not an actual operating system or medical device. Launch status: **HOLD**.

## Original dedication
> Dedicated to my precious daughter—and every parent, grandparent, aunt, and uncle who ever held a child down on the kitchen floor with tweezers.

## Visual blueprint
1980s/90s CRT workshop HUD; amber 04:59 countdown; CALM → WHIMPER → SQUIRM → FULL PANIC; simulated mom-proximity radar; optical magnifier; equipment dock. Virtual `/dev/*` device names map to game cards, not physical tools. `patientd`, `irq_flinch`, `sterile_guard.sys`, `er_watchdog.ko` are fictional gameplay subsystems. `/var/log/blackmail.db` is a fictional incident-log title; actual player data must remain private and protected.

## Game mechanics
Touch taps, jitter, virtual equipment, scores, simulated ER handoff, mission cartridges, sound and haptics. No physical restraint, skin incision, embedded-object removal, or medical procedures are taught. High-risk missions reward seeking professional care.

## Release
Do not publish until UI, accessibility, offline save, tests, app-store packaging, age rating, and project-specific approval are complete. Do not merge this project with TAP THAT, Pumpkin Patch, or the Conductor.
