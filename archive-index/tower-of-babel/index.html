IV. How a comparison holds up

9. American racial law and Nazi German law

Dred Scott in 1857 denied federal citizenship to people of African descent within the categories the Court described. The Thirteenth Amendment abolished slavery and involuntary servitude with an exception for punishment after conviction. The Fourteenth Amendment defined citizenship for persons born or naturalized in the United States and subject to its jurisdiction. The law used to assign status could itself be changed. Record both the original rule and the later constitutional break before describing the period that followed. Exhibit C04 · Exhibit C09 · Exhibit C10
