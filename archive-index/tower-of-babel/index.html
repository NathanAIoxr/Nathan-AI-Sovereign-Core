The difference between a word and what someone makes it say

GOD → DOG reverses the written sequence. Doing it twice returns the starting word. LIVE → EVIL, LOVE → EVOL, and CAT → TAC work on the same letter-by-letter rule. TABOR → ROBAT also works that way. ROBAT → ROBOT is the next move in Nathan’s association. Keeping both steps visible makes it possible to repeat the first and understand the second. FREE → FEE changes the spelling by deleting one letter. The manuscript uses that loss to explore what happens when a claimed freedom comes with a price.

COME / CUM share the pronunciation /kʌm/. A sound can reach the listener intact while the meaning is misread. The sentence around the word settles what the speaker intended. Nathan’s documented correction, “I picked my nose,” gives the practical consequence. The speaker’s wording controls the corrected record. A speech recognizer’s earlier spelling remains useful evidence of where the error entered. Kaylyn Tabor / Kalen DeBoer belongs with near-matches by ear. For an archive, the speaker’s audio where available, the first transcription, and the explicit correction are three records with different functions. Papacy, colonial law, and the Americas

#

C01 · May 1213, King John’s surrender charter. John transferred his kingdoms to Pope Innocent III and received them as a fief tied to annual tribute. Impact: documents a medieval relationship of papal vassalage. C02 · 1452, 1455, 1493, and 1537, papal bulls and later Vatican statements. Dum Diversas authorized the Portuguese king to conquer and subject specified peoples to perpetual slavery. In March 2023, two Vatican dicasteries repudiated the legal and political doctrine of discovery and acknowledged that the bulls failed to reflect Indigenous peoples’ equal dignity and rights. In 2026, Pope Leo XIV asked pardon for the Church’s historical role in legitimizing slavery. 1452 text · 1455 text · 1493 text · 2023 Vatican statement · 2026 encyclical

C07 · The Americas after 1492, demographic study published 2019. Koch and colleagues synthesized 119 published regional estimates and estimated a pre-contact Indigenous population of 60.5 million, with an interquartile range of 44.8–78.2 million. They estimate a decline of roughly 90% in the following century. Epidemics were the main driver in their model and warfare, enslavement, forced relocation, and hunger amplified losses. Impact: the research estimates hemisphere-wide depopulation, approximately 55 million at the central estimate. Its geography, dates, and causes cannot establish a toll of 60 million direct killings by the U.S. government. Full article and DOI, UCL
