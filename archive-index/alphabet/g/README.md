## Tabor Spiral and Counter-Spiral · two originals

The owner’s earlier Pasted markdown(20261015-012838).md names two distinct mathematical symbolic models:

- **Inward Compression** / Tabor Spiral: **r = a · exp(−bθ)**. Positive b causes radius to decrease as θ increases.
- **Outward Expansion** / Counter-Spiral: **r = a · exp(+bθ)**. Positive b causes radius to increase as θ increases.

The original text associates these paths with inward/outward propeller, DNA/RNA and worldview symbolism; that creative interpretation remains labeled as the owner’s model. The new interactive laboratory draws actual logarithmic curves and allows inward, outward or simultaneous views with rotation and growth controls.

A golden spiral is one particular logarithmic spiral, with **b = ln(φ)/(π/2) ≈ 0.30635** and **φ = (1+√5)/2**. A Nautilus-style illustrative b ≈ 0.18 is not the golden special case. Physical Nautilus shells vary; scientific research does not support describing all shells as exactly golden. See [Nautilus research, Bartlett 2019](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00004-018-0419-3), [Plus Maths](https://plus.maths.org/comment/11384), and [University of Washington interactive shell study](https://sites.math.washington.edu/~aloveles/CuriosityLab/projects/shell-growth.html). The creator name **Tabor Spiral / Nautilus** remains unchanged.

The separate **THE TÁBOR SPIRAL: NAUTILUS OF WAR** game is catalogued from the owner-held V2 manual as an alternate-history creative strategy game with **63 numbered chambers**, from Outer Gate 1 to Citadel 63. The new G app includes a noncombat 63-position number navigator, not a replacement or historical certification of the original game.

Historically, the Latin letter **G developed from C** in Roman writing. Its ancestry is different from the author’s rotated-5 or reshaped-6 visual model. [Historic Encyclopaedia Britannica letter-G entry](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/37160/pg37160-images.html).
