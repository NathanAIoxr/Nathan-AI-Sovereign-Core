## Glyph construction

- **E**: 2 original sticks; one remains whole and the other is divided into **3** crosspieces; **4 visible straight segments** in this construction.
- **3**: 2 original sticks, **fused and bent**; numeral 3 is a numeric identity, not Latin letter E.
- **M**: **4 original sticks**, joined into M.
- **W**: **4 original sticks**, joined into W; turning/reflecting an idealized M construction is a visual operation, not a statement about language history.
- Keep original sticks, parts after breaking, fused joints, bends, shape transforms, sound, Unicode code point, script, numeral meaning and source evidence as **different attributes**. Similarity does not assert etymological descent.
- Cross-script comparisons (Latin E, Greek Ε/ε, Cyrillic Е/е, Cyrillic З/з, numeral 3) are visual research routes; no silent equivalence or source rewriting.
