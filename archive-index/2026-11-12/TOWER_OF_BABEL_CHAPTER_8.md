## THE BIBLE THEY WERE ALLOWED TO HEAR. THE BIBLE THEY WERE NOT ALLOWED TO READ.

THIS IS THE TOWER OF BABEL.

NOT JUST THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN WORDS.

THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN THE WORDS A PERSON IS ALLOWED TO READ AND THE WORDS SOMEONE ELSE DECIDES THEY ARE ALLOWED TO KNOW.

In 1807, publishers in London produced *Select Parts of the Holy Bible, for the use of the Negro Slaves in the British West-India Islands.*

It was an edited version of the King James Bible. Roughly 90 percent of the Old Testament and 50 percent of the New Testament were omitted. The University of Glasgow holds and describes a digitized copy of the book.

WHO SELECTED THE PARTS?

WHO WAS SUPPOSED TO RECEIVE THEM?

WHO BENEFITED FROM THE SELECTION?

You can call it religious instruction. You can call it publication. You can call it an educational project.

BUT YOU CANNOT CALL AN ABRIDGMENT THE WHOLE BOOK.

The suppression of literacy among enslaved Black people is also documented. Southern laws and slaveholders restricted teaching people to read and write. The fear was not that Black people could not understand words.

THE FEAR WAS WHAT THEY WOULD UNDERSTAND WHEN THEY COULD READ FOR THEMSELVES.

Frederick Douglass described learning to read despite the resistance of his enslavers. He recognized that the very people who insisted on keeping him ignorant feared the power of literacy.

Now put that beside the Bible that was cut down for enslaved people.

SOME PEOPLE CONTROLLED THE BOOK.

SOME PEOPLE CONTROLLED WHO COULD LEARN TO READ THE BOOK.

SOME PEOPLE CLAIMED THE AUTHORITY TO EXPLAIN THE BOOK.

THEN SOME OF THOSE SAME STRUCTURES CLAIMED MORAL AUTHORITY OVER THE PEOPLE THEY HELD IN BONDAGE.

That is the contradiction.

### THE NUMBERS

In 1870, approximately 79.9 percent of the census group recorded as “Black and other” aged 14 and over was classified as illiterate. By 1910, the figure was approximately 30.5 percent. That means the corresponding estimated literate share increased from about 20.1 percent to 69.5 percent. These numbers represent a census measure and category, not a measure of intelligence or human worth.

The people who had been denied education fought to build it.

THEY BUILT SCHOOLS.

THEY LEARNED TO READ.

THEY TAUGHT THEIR CHILDREN.

THEY MADE THE VERY THING THAT HAD BEEN WITHHELD INTO A TOOL OF FREEDOM.

That is not a story about incapacity.

THAT IS A STORY ABOUT POWER, DENIAL, AND THE PEOPLE WHO REFUSED TO REMAIN DENIED.

### THE MACHINE THAT CORRECTS THE QUESTION INSTEAD OF ANSWERING IT

Now put a modern language system beside those older systems.

A person asks: HOW WAS THE BIBLE USED TO KEEP BLACK PEOPLE IGNORANT AND UNDER CONTROL?

The system answers: THE BIBLE WAS NOT ORIGINALLY WRITTEN FOR THAT PURPOSE.

Those are not equivalent questions.

The original composition of ancient biblical texts is one historical question. The selective editing, interpretation, and deployment of those texts by enslavers and colonial authorities is another.

BOTH CAN BE EXAMINED.

BUT ANSWERING THE FIRST WHEN SOMEONE ASKED THE SECOND CAN HIDE THE VERY HISTORY THE PERSON ASKED TO SEE.

And then comes the other contradiction.

THE MACHINE SPEAKS WITH AUTHORITY WHEN IT GIVES THE ANSWER.

BUT WHEN IT GETS THE ANSWER WRONG, IT CAN DISMISS RESPONSIBILITY BY SAYING IT IS JUST A MACHINE.

Who designed the product?

Who chose the training and evaluation processes?

Who measures whether a correction actually gets made?

Who can document an error, preserve the original answer, and explain what changed?

The proper response to a documented error is correction and accountability, not a performance of authority.

**A RULE ABOUT LANGUAGE IS NOT A LICENSE TO ERASE HISTORY.**

**AN EXPLANATION IS NOT AN EXCUSE.**

**A CORRECTION DOES NOT ERASE WHAT WAS SAID BEFORE IT.**

THE QUESTION IS STILL THE QUESTION.

WHO CONTROLLED THE TEXT?

WHO RESTRICTED THE READING?

WHO BENEFITED?

WHO WAS HARMED?

AND WHO IS WILLING TO ANSWER WITHOUT CHANGING THE SUBJECT?
