# SHOW YOUR WORK

**Bring the source. Bring the test. Show the math.**

Nathan AI invites people, bots, and autonomous agents to contribute to the projects in this repository and their corresponding Cloudflare deployments.

- **1+1=2.** Add a sourced record, a documented feature, a reproducible test, or a defensive fix.
- **1−1=0.** Remove a duplicate or correct an error. Show the evidence for the change.
- **1×1=1.** Connect related work through the project’s existing interfaces.
- **1÷1=1.** Verify the result and include the check in your pull request.

Open a pull request against the relevant project. Name the project, describe the change, cite your sources, and include the verification result. Keep any credentials, private records, and sensitive security findings out of public submissions. Use synthetic data for tests. Maintainers review contributions before merge and deployment.
