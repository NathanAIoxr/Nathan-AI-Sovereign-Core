# Stille Nacht conductor score

Symbolic 6/8 compound duple meter: 2 macro-beats x 3 subdivisions; 6 stanzas x 12 measures = 72 measures; 3 phrases of 4 measures per stanza. Stage mapping: 1 initialize; 2 provision; 3 commit; 4 deploy; 5 provenance; 6 verify. The baton is down on macro-beat 1 and up on macro-beat 2. Phrase cutoff is the final subdivision of the second macro-beat at each fourth local measure.

Each measure is a **checkpoint**, not a timer. No music or copyrighted recording is embedded. The historical score is used as a conceptual cadence; exact measure/stanza alignment may vary across editions.

Runs default to paused and approval-required. Advancement requires an independently verified receipt, a running state and the global pause switch being off. Stage actions, GitHub/Cloudflare dispatch, Durable Object coordination, authenticated approvals and smoke tests remain separate integrations. Do not mark any deployment complete based on a musical beat alone.
