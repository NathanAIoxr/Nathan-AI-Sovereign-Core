### Tabor Concordance

Keep Strong’s numbering and bring the root meanings forward.

| Language and witness | Form | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Hebrew Job 1:8 | הַשָּׂטָן, *ha-satan* | **Ha-** is the definite article. **H7854** is adversary or opponent. Strong’s links it to **H7853**, to attack, accuse, or resist. |
| Hebrew Job 1:8 | אִיּוֹב, *Iyyob*, **H347** | Job. Strong’s links the name to **H340**, to be hostile or be an enemy, and glosses the name as hated or persecuted. Brown–Driver–Briggs marks the derivation uncertain. |
| Aramaic Targum to Job 1:8 | סִטְנָא, *sitna* | The adversary. The Targum addresses the adversary and speaks of Job separately. |
| Greek Septuagint Job 1:6–9 | ὁ διάβολος, *ho diabolos* | The accuser or slanderer, **G1228** with the Greek definite article **G3588**. The Greek text calls Job Ἰώβ, **G2492**. |
| Greek Mark 4:15 | ὁ Σατανᾶς, *ho Satanas* | The adversary, **G4567** with **G3588**. This is a Greek parallel. The confirmed study title uses the Hebrew form. |

The KJV uses **H7854** for a human opponent in 1 Kings 11:14, where Hadad is called “an adversary.” In Job 1:8–9 the adversary questions Job’s motives before God. In 2:3 God describes the harm as “without cause,” and in 2:6–7 God sets the limit and the adversary strikes Job. Tabor’s **“007 AGENT”** describes his reading of that role.

In 13:3 Job asks to reason with God. In 19:6–7 he says God overthrew him and says he cries out without receiving judgment. In 31:35 he calls for the Almighty to answer. In 42:7 God says Job spoke rightly of him where his friends failed. In 42:11 the narrator attributes the calamity to the Lord, and 42:12 records the blessing.

**Tabor’s reading:** “HA SATAN is Job” identifies the adversarial role Job takes when he brings his case against God. Job 1:8 uses the Hebrew title for the figure God addresses about Job. Job’s later speeches supply the evidence for Tabor’s reading of the role reversal. Tabor’s earlier *52 Consciousness* notes emphasize “without cause,” and his JNT rendering of Job 42:7 reads “NOT today GOD, NOT ever AGAIN.” Prior chat and correspondence corroborated the earlier work.
