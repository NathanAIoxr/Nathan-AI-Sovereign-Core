## SAFEGUARDING WORKFLOW

1. **Immediate danger:** seek emergency assistance and get the child or animal to safety without confrontation.
2. **Record first-hand facts:** date, place, direct observations, witnesses, exact quotations and source provenance; separate observation, allegation, inference and verified finding.
3. **Protect victims and evidence:** never publish children's identifying details; do not obtain, download, redistribute or publicly display child sexual abuse material. Preserve only lawful, non-graphic records and report where evidence is held to authorized investigators.
4. **Report and refer:** use appropriate child protection services, law enforcement, animal welfare authorities and independent oversight channels. Escalate suspected institutional obstruction through lawful channels.
5. **Review and correct:** check records for mistakes, duplicate claims, conflicts and retaliation risks; protect presumption of innocence and witnesses' safety.
6. **Prevent recurrence:** education, independent audits, escalation rules, human review, anti-retaliation safeguards and measured follow-up.

**Do not turn a personal “list” into an assassination/target list or a public roster of unverified accusations.** If lawful case tracking is ever implemented, it must be private, access-controlled, audit-logged and designed for qualified safeguarding personnel, with retention and deletion policies.
