1. Arithmetic & base conversions: decimal vs binary vs duodecimal; note 1+1=10 in binary means decimal two.

6. Ethics/innocence: avoid harm and preserve dignity, especially for minors.
7. Creative invention: unusual analogies and original architecture puzzles, with coherent constraints.

Suggested game record:
- challenge_id, prompt, author_input, assistant_response, scoring_rule, result, evidence, provenance, uncertainty, safety_review.

- ties and equal outcomes must be possible; no programmed automatic victory.
