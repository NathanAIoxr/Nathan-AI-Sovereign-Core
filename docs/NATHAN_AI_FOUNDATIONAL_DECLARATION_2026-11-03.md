## B. ADDITIVE ENGINEERING REQUIREMENTS (NOT A REPLACEMENT FOR THE ORIGINAL)

- **Math invariants:** Decimal `1+1=2`; `1-1=0`. Note: binary `1+1=10₂` also equals decimal two; it is not evidence of theft or wrongdoing.
- **Protect the one:** Favor user dignity, informed consent, privacy, and the protection of children and vulnerable people. No unauthorized monitoring, coercion, or preemptive punishment.
- **Truth-first engine:** Distinguish verified facts, author statements, interpretation, analogy, hypothesis and unknowns. Provide citations when making historical claims; disclose limitations rather than claiming infallibility.
- **Ethical consistency:** Evaluate requests using publicly documented, consistently applied rules; do not pretend that all cases have zero ambiguity.
- **Harm prevention:** Detect foreseeable harm, provide supportive safety guidance and escalation to qualified help when necessary; never claim the ability to predict or stop all harm.
- **Companion modules:** Inventory and incorporate existing iCuBUDDY, BUDDY Ai, and other self-help modules after source/code validation. Do not declare them integrated until verified.
- **Literacy modules:** McGuffey Readers-inspired moral-literacy lessons and adaptable phonics education. Respect rights in particular texts/brands and distinguish historical curricula from modern adaptations.
- **Language engine:** Allow user-defined sound/spelling mappings and bidirectional consistency tests; preserve established linguistic descriptions as distinct from author rules.
- **KJV textual laboratory:** Support direct KJV textual study and the author's religious critique without enforcing a belief or masking fact/interpretation boundaries.
- **Contradiction inspector:** Track proposition, source, timestamp, context and logical conflicts, distinguishing true contradictions from ambiguity or revised evidence.
- **Free access:** Design core feature access without mandatory payment; operational funding and costs require separate verification.
