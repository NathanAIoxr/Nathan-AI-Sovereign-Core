### 2. Giving taxonomy — independent program ledgers
- **TOYS:** board books, reading, learning kits, sensory toys, creative arts, puzzles, group games, outdoor play, teen activities; separate inventory by age suitability and item safety.
- **CLOTHING:** socks, footwear, coats, thermal layers, jeans, shirts, schoolwear, underwear/new-only, adaptive clothing, new/gently used category subject to hygiene policies.
- **FOOD:** shelf-stable pantry, fresh groceries, family meals, holiday meals, breakfast support, school-break food, formula only under appropriate safety standards; partnering organizations handle food safety and expiration.
- **ESSENTIALS:** diapers, hygiene, toiletries, bedding, backpacks, school supplies; these are not forcibly relabeled as toys, food or clothes.
- **LOGISTICS:** storage, packing, delivery, volunteer safeguards, fuel and inventory systems.
- **WHERE NEEDED MOST:** flexible donations subject to an adopted approved allocation policy.
Every financial movement is tagged by site, campaign, source, purpose, restriction, payment verification state, refund/chargeback status and outcome batch; assets are not conflated.

### 4. Suggested packaging beyond Heifer's ordinary catalog
**Giving type:** cash contribution, merchant-purchased goods, physical goods drop-off, volunteer time, matching commitment, in-kind corporate donation. Track as distinct transactions; never convert a pledge into confirmed cash.

**Tribute options:** in honor of, in memory of, a birthday gift, classroom/school fundraiser, business team, church/community sponsor. Do not publish donor/recipient information absent affirmative consent. A gift certificate for program selection is not automatically a redeemable retail gift card.

**Recurring schedules:** one-time, monthly, annual once a true recurring merchant integration exists. Current browser planner only offers manual intentions. Renewal reminders require donor opt-in; unsubscribe and privacy deletion controls must work.

**Seasonality:** Jan–Mar winter warmth and food; Apr–Jun play, learning and pantry; Jul–Sep back-to-school and school-break nutrition; Oct–Dec Christmas distribution. All emergency or non-seasonal need remains eligible all year.

**Passing on the Gift adapted:** Reusable toy donations when checked for recall, age safety, cleanliness and integrity; reusable clothing only when appropriate; food under separate food safety controls. Optional volunteering, community drives and skill-sharing can multiply program capacity. Receiving families are not required to repay or 'pass on' donated goods.

### 5. Micro: donation-to-delivery state ledger
- \`planned\`: donor intent only.
- \`payment_started\`: link or merchant checkout opened.
- \`paid_verified\`: independent payment provider or authorized audited reconciliation confirms amount, recipient and reference.
- \`acknowledged\`: email provider confirms sent acknowledgment; tax statement requires eligibility.
- \`allocated\`: funds assigned to approved expense category.
- \`procured\`: actual order with invoice and item record.
- \`distributed\`: documented handover by secure partner without public child identifiers.
- \`reconciled\`: finance/receipt checks complete.
- Reversal states \`cancelled\`, \`failed\`, \`refund_pending\`, \`refunded\`, \`disputed\`.

**Separate physical-goods track:** pledged → accepted → inspected/safety reviewed → inventoried → distributed → reconciled. **Do not** represent in-kind donation values as cash receipts without proper appraisal/accounting.

### 6. Real provider architecture (blocked until approved)
Static GitHub Pages cannot securely run a donor email database, secret checkout keys, authentic Cash App webhook signing, refunds or a tax-receipt system. Build these in an authenticated private service. Never place donor PII, child information, cash credentials or API keys into the public repo.
1. Checkout and donor contact service accepts an explicit donor-selected purpose and email consent.
2. Merchant-hosted payment flow; no blind cashtag-link "paid" events.
3. Signed provider webhooks, event deduplication, reconciliation and refund handling.
4. Email delivery of actual acknowledgment, donor-controlled honor card and optional updates.
5. Secure partner distribution dashboard and anonymized public impact reporting.
6. Accessibility, receipt audit, retention rules, state charitable fundraising compliance and founder's explicit go-live approval.

### 7. Measurement and governance
**Trust metrics:** verified payment count/amount (not just clicks), donor opt-in and delivery rate, transaction reconciliation lag, refunds, percentage of donations with consented email, chargeback rate, unfulfilled promised gifts, cost per goods-delivered unit, inventory loss, distribution lead time, beneficiary satisfaction collected privately, physical goods safety pass rate, available cash by fund restriction.
**Non-guaranteed benchmarks:** no unverified claim of '100% directly to toys', no fabricated matches or 'double impact', no invented retail quantity, no promise of 'instant auto email', no 501(c)(3) tax deduction without legal confirmation.
**Financial controls:** charitable funds and individual revenues separated by entity/account, legal payee established, documented authorized spending, two-person disbursement approvals and transaction audit trails.
