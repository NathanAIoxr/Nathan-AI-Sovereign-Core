## The two uses of HA

- **English initials and numbers.** HA gives 81 in alphabet order and is Nathan’s shorthand for Hells Angels. AH gives 18. O’AH / o18 is an earlier extension Nathan supplied.
- **Hebrew source wording.** In הַשָּׂטָן (*ha-satan*), **ha-** is the definite article, and **satan**, Strong’s **H7854**, means adversary or opponent. Strong’s **H7853** gives the related verb senses oppose or accuse. In Job 1:8 the Lord speaks to the adversary about Job. In Job 13:3, 19:6–7, and 31:35 Job challenges God and calls for an answer. Nathan’s **“HA SATAN is Job”** connects the meaning of adversary with Job’s role in those later passages. The Hebrew meanings come from the sources and the connection is Nathan’s.
