## How to trace a claim

| Clock | Action | What to record |
|---|---|---|
| 12 · Build · 1+1=2 | Collect the wording and the record it cites. | Who said it, when, where, in what language and to whom. |
| 3 · Flip and clear · 1−1=0 | Remove certainty that outruns the evidence. | Who changed the name, what was omitted, and whether a claim of intent can be established. |
| 6 · Integrate · 1×1=1 | Put the source, the retelling and the affected community’s account on one timeline. | Translation, naming, legal power, adaptation, gatekeeping and publishing history. |
| 9 · Verify · 1÷1=1 | Repeat the check against independent evidence. | Documented, mixed, disputed, unsupported, contradicted, or explicitly fictional. Record the correction and leave a path for the next reader. |

A treaty, a testimony, a film, a community’s own name and a scientific date answer different questions. Keep the claim next to the source that actually supports it. A false result can arise from omission, error, selective framing, fiction passed off as fact, or deliberate deception. Establish the act and the evidence for intent separately.

## Cases and sources

### What footprints can date

Ice Age · White Sands, New Mexico

**Source record** — Independent dating methods support roughly 21,000–23,000-year-old human footprints at White Sands.

**How the story changes** — A textbook’s earliest known date becomes a claim about the first person who arrived.

**Check it** — Compare the initial dating and the independent pollen and sediment evidence. Mark what the footprints tell you about presence, what they leave open about routes, and why local Indigenous identities need separate evidence.

- [USGS independent age estimates](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/independent-age-estimates-resolve-controversy-ancient-human-footprints-white-sands)

### Kadesh: victory and treaty

13th century BCE · Egypt and the Hittite empire

**Source record** — Egyptian royal records present the campaign through Ramesses II’s voice. The Egyptian–Hittite treaty records a later agreement between the two powers.

**How the story changes** — A king’s victory narrative gets promoted into an independent report on every side of the battle.

**Check it** — Date the campaign and treaty separately. Mark who commissioned the inscription, who made the later agreement, and which military conclusions each record can actually support.

- [English translation of Egyptian records](https://adfonteslibrary.org/work-egypt-breasted-ancient-records)
- [United Nations treaty replica](https://www.un.org/ungifts/replica-peace-treaty-between-hattusilis-and-ramses-ii)

### Cyrus Cylinder: royal decree and later label

c. 539 BCE · Babylon

**Source record** — The Babylonian cuneiform cylinder is a royal foundation deposit associated with Cyrus’s conquest and restoration claims.

**How the story changes** — The modern phrase ‘first human rights charter’ is read as if it were the original category and legal scope of the text.

**Check it** — Separate the communities and actions named on the object from a general claim about universal individual rights. Compare the artifact label with the language of a modern rights declaration.

- [Metropolitan Museum of Art](https://www.metmuseum.org/exhibitions/listings/2013/cyrus-cylinder)
- [United Nations background](https://ask.un.org/faq/194027)

### Ashoka and the voice of Kalinga

3rd century BCE · South Asia

**Source record** — Major Rock Edict XIII records Ashoka’s account of his conquest of Kalinga and his stated remorse over the suffering.

**How the story changes** — The ruler’s own statement is used to stand in for the experience of the conquered population.

**Check it** — Annotate the account as the king’s voice. Mark the suffering acknowledged in the text and describe what kinds of evidence could supply an independent perspective.

- [Translation of the edicts](https://ibc-elibrary.thanhsiang.org/files/accesstoinsight/html/lib/authors/dhammika/wheel386.html)

### Timbuktu: manuscripts and a supposed absence

Medieval to early modern · Mali

**Source record** — Surviving manuscripts held in Malian libraries document scholarly and written traditions. Digitized examples are available through the Library of Congress.

**How the story changes** — A claim that the region lacked written scholarship mistakes a reader’s lack of access for the absence of records.

**Check it** — Pick a dated manuscript entry. Record its owner, language, subject, provenance and what it demonstrates about the community that preserved it.

- [Malian manuscript collection and provenance](https://www.loc.gov/collections/islamic-manuscripts-from-mali/about-this-collection/)

### The Time of Peace: Where Innocence Was Not a Commodity

Cross-era inquiry · Tabor Academy

**Source record** — Nathan Tabor’s framework starts with kinship, practical instruction, shared authority, and the question of when innocence becomes something institutions can trade or control.

**How the story changes** — A regional example gets recast as evidence for one universal age, or a real example of coercion is used to erase all evidence of cooperation in the same period.

**Check it** — Pick one community and one time window. Look for both mutual care and coercion. Record who set the rules, who benefited, who had a choice, and what the sources establish.

- [Tabor Academy · The Time of Peace](https://tabor-bridge.nathantaboract.workers.dev/tabor-academy/order/time-of-peace/)

### Ohkay Owingeh: who names a people’s home?

1598 → 1680 → 2005 · Tewa Pueblo, New Mexico

**Source record** — The Tewa name means ‘place of the strong people’. Oñate imposed a Spanish name in 1598. Po’pay and other Pueblo leaders organized the revolt of 1680. The Pueblo officially restored Ohkay Owingeh in 2005.

**How the story changes** — Reading only ‘San Juan Pueblo’ can make the colonial mission seem like the beginning of the place. A resemblance to English O.K. is sometimes mistaken for a historical derivation.

**Check it** — Read the state memorial with the Pueblo account. Mark the community’s name, the settler-imposed name, the distinct records of use, and who made each decision.

- [New Mexico name restoration memorial](https://www.nmlegis.gov/sessions/06%20Regular/memorials/senate/SJM002.HTML)
- [Indian Pueblo Cultural Center · Pueblo Revolt](https://indianpueblo.org/revolt/)
- [Ohkay Owingeh Tewa language program](https://ohkay.org/tewa-language-program/)

### DINE, Dinetah and the clockwise register

Ancestral homeland → 1868 treaty → present · Four Corners

**Source record** — Diné is affirmed as the people’s name by the Nation’s Judicial Branch. Dinétah names the ancestral homeland. Federal treaty wording and the people’s own names are separate historical records.

**How the story changes** — Outside labels or similar sounds are treated as complete explanations of identity. A name for a village, a people and a modern nation are treated as interchangeable.

**Check it** — Use the book’s clockwise register with the community names first. Index the distinction between a people, a village and a nation, then compare treaty language to self-descriptions.

- [Navajo Nation Judicial Branch](https://courts.navajo-nsn.gov/Harmonization/Bitsesilei.htm)
- [1868 treaty](https://courts.navajo-nsn.gov/Treaty1868.htm)
- [Diné Nihi Kéyah Project](https://dinelanduse.org/aboutus/)

### Dinetah and Eden: test the quarter-turn

Genesis narrative and present watershed · Four Corners comparison

**Source record** — Nathan Tabor’s North-as-East rule turns N→E, E→S, S→W, W→N. Genesis 2:10 has one river parting into four heads. The Animas and other drainages join the San Juan in the documented local watershed.

**How the story changes** — The revised river assignments are presented as if they followed mechanically from the first map, or the comparison itself is presented as an archaeological location.

**Check it** — Carry the first sketch through the turn: Pison E, Hiddekel S, Gihon W, Euphrates N. Compare the revised sketch: Hiddekel E, Gihon S, Pison W, Euphrates N. Check H6924 and H6926 separately and trace the real waterflow.

- [Strong’s H6924](https://biblehub.com/hebrew/6924.htm)
- [Strong’s H6926](https://biblehub.com/hebrew/strongs_6926.htm)

### We the People: promise, rule and enforcement

1776 → 1787 → 1868 · United States

**Source record** — The Declaration announces equality as a principle. The 1787 Constitution contained provisions tied to slavery. The Fourteenth Amendment later defined citizenship and required equal protection.

**How the story changes** — The promise is narrated as if it was fully implemented when it was proclaimed.

**Check it** — Read one clause from each document. Record the promise, the rule actually in force, who was excluded, and what further evidence would show about enforcement.

- [Declaration, National Archives](https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/declaration-of-independence)
- [Constitution, National Archives](https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/constitution)
- [Fourteenth Amendment, National Archives](https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/14th-amendment)

### Haiti: whose declaration counts?

1804 · Haiti

**Source record** — Haiti’s declaration of independence records a founding claim to sovereignty after revolution and emancipation.

**How the story changes** — A history of Atlantic freedom retells only European or U.S. declarations and leaves Haiti out of the reader’s picture.

**Check it** — Read a passage of the 1804 declaration. Compare the audience and claims with another declaration and record what its authors considered essential after colonial rule.

- [1804 Haitian declaration in translation](https://encyclopediavirginia.org/primary-documents/the-haitian-declaration-of-independence-january-1-1804/)

### O.K. Corral: stable, street fight and movie

1881 and 1957 · Tombstone, Arizona, and Hollywood

**Source record** — The fight occurred in a vacant lot off Fremont Street near C.S. Fly’s premises. Later writers popularized the corral label and a 1957 film brought it to more audiences.

**How the story changes** — The title is used as a literal location. A dramatized duel or a partisan account is treated as proof of who did what.

**Check it** — Put the lot, the stable and the movie on separate lines. The next-day headline was Yesterday’s Tragedy. Compare a map, accounts of the preliminary hearing and the later title.

- [U.S. Census Bureau · historic map and account](https://www.census.gov/geographies/reference-maps/1881/comm/tombstone-az.html)
- [Cochise County Historical Society](https://www.cochisecountyhistoricalsociety.org/journals/cchs-vol-11-no-01-spring-1981.pdf)
- [American Film Institute · 1957 film](https://catalog.afi.com/Catalog/moviedetails/52199)

### Ida B. Wells: check the stated justification

1892 · United States

**Source record** — In Southern Horrors, Wells challenged the accusations and justifications circulated to excuse lynching and compared them with available case records.

**How the story changes** — A repeated public accusation gets recorded as if repetition were independent corroboration.

**Check it** — Identify one public justification, the evidence Wells adduces against it, and what the record does and does not establish. Trace whether multiple reports copy the same claim.

- [Southern Horrors · University of Houston Digital History](https://www.digitalhistory.uh.edu/disp_textbook.cfm?psid=3614&smtid=3)

### Our Gang and The Little Rascals

1922–1944 → 1955 · U.S. theatrical and television media

**Source record** — Roach created the film series. MGM took over in 1938 and retained the Our Gang title. Roach-era sound shorts entered TV syndication in 1955 as The Little Rascals.

**How the story changes** — The nostalgic package becomes the only lens. Some later accounts erase the integrated cast, while others erase the racial stereotypes and working conditions.

**Check it** — Compare a scene from Pups Is Pups, where Farina helps his friends, with Julia Lee’s account of stigma and the experiences of Stymie Beard and Dickie Moore. Identify what changes between screen and offscreen.

- [Alex Film Society on the series and rights](https://alexfilmsociety.org/newsletters/Vaudeville_10_web.pdf)
- [Library of Congress · Pups Is Pups](https://www.loc.gov/static/programs/national-film-preservation-board/documents/pups.pdf)
- [Julia Lee on the series’ racial history](https://www.upress.umn.edu/9781452949789/our-gang/)

### ‘Relocation’ and Japanese American incarceration

1942 onward · United States

**Source record** — Executive Order 9066 enabled forced removal. Federal records and community archives document the confinement that followed for people of Japanese ancestry.

**How the story changes** — Administrative terms such as ‘evacuation’ and ‘relocation’ are repeated without showing whether people could choose to leave or return.

**Check it** — Compare a government label with what the order allowed and what an affected person experienced. Ask who could walk away, who could return and under what conditions.

- [Executive Order 9066, National Archives](https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/executive-order-9066)
- [Densho terminology guide](https://densho.org/terminology/)

### Partition: a line on paper and lives across it

1947 · South Asia

**Source record** — The Radcliffe boundary record shows a division on paper. The 1947 Partition Archive preserves oral histories from people who experienced the division.

**How the story changes** — The line by itself is presented as a complete account of migration, loss and survival.

**Check it** — Choose a locality shown on the boundary map. Compare one dated testimony about that place and identify what neither source can establish by itself.

- [UK National Archives · Partition records](https://cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk/documents/education/partition-of-british-india.pdf)
- [The 1947 Partition Archive](https://www.1947partitionarchive.org/archive_access/)

### Pumpkins, DINE land and Po’pay’s Tewa name

1974 onward, with a 1680 historical link · North Carolina and New Mexico

**Source record** — The Indian Pueblo Cultural Center gives Popyn as ‘ripe squash’ and describes Po’pay as a farmer. NAPI separately documents a pumpkin fundraiser contractor on Navajo Nation land near Farmington.

**How the story changes** — A plausible grower is promoted to a confirmed supplier for a particular event, or the distinct DINE and Tewa histories are merged.

**Check it** — Build a label for the pumpkin project. Include only the grower and place supported by the supplier record. Credit the Tewa source for Po’pay and the appropriate DINE source for land and farming.

- [Indian Pueblo Cultural Center · Po’pay](https://indianpueblo.org/pueblo-revolt-2/)
- [NAPI · Pumpkin Patch Fundraisers](https://napi.navajopride.com/2024/09/05/pumpkin-patch-fundraisers/)
- [Fundraiser’s history](https://www.pumpkinsusa.com/about-us/)

### Mabo: who could own land in the official record?

1992 · Mer, Torres Strait, Australia

**Source record** — The Australian High Court’s Mabo decision recognized native title and rejected the idea that prior Indigenous land rights could simply be treated as nonexistent under Australian common law.

**How the story changes** — The legal fiction behind terra nullius becomes a popular claim that the land lacked its own people, law or tenure.

**Check it** — Compare the court outcome with the community’s case. Mark which finding concerns the Meriam people and what further legal tests apply elsewhere.

- [AIATSIS · The Mabo case](https://aiatsis.gov.au/explore/mabo-case)
- [National Museum of Australia](https://www.nma.gov.au/defining-moments/resources/mabo-decision)

### Apartheid: testimony and the TRC record

1990s · South Africa

**Source record** — The Truth and Reconciliation Commission published testimony and findings through a defined mandate and official process.

**How the story changes** — A state’s earlier justification is accepted as the whole story, or one later commission is treated as if it heard every witness and resolved every case.

**Check it** — Read the commission’s mandate beside a case. Record whose testimony survives, which conclusions the commission reached, and what would require another source.

- [South African Department of Justice · TRC reports](https://justice.gov.za/trc/report/index.htm)

### DINE and the confident wrong turn

Contemporary · search and generative AI

**Source record** — In the supplied exchange, a query about DINE was answered as a restaurant request despite the existing subject. The example can be checked against the conversation sequence.

**How the story changes** — The confident answer or a later explanation is accepted without checking what had already been said. A context error is automatically classified as deliberate deception.

**Check it** — Record the claim, the earlier context and the observable error. Preserve the original transcript privately and give the source for any new factual claim. Run the same context test against any AI, including Nathan AI.

- [NIST Generative AI Profile · confabulation](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf)
