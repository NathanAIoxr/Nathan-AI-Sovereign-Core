## Four axes, both directions

The center is **0 · ONE**. Direction buttons change navigation lenses; they do not rewrite, collapse or reverse original source identities.

- **UP**: Subject pyramid, using broad Dewey 000–900 labels and provisional app-store-to-subject crosswalks. These *are not* certified call numbers.
- **DOWN**: Functional pyramid, showing more actual source context as the visitor increases integer depth: name/path → capabilities → rationale → evidence.
- **LEFT**: Historical indexing practices and alternate sort strategies from ancient source colophons through medieval inventories, print bibliographies, modern classification, and faceted data.
- **RIGHT**: Apple categories, Google Play categories and game genres. Category selections filter the existing source-backed app directory, including unclassified entries.
- **SPECTRUM BOTH WAYS**: User-adjustable signed whole-number coordinate, −9..0..+9, with forward/reverse traversal and independent 0 anchor. The visible above sequence is +9,+8,+7,+6,+5,+4,+3,+2,+1; below listed from far south toward zero is −9,−8,−7,−6,−5,−4,−3,−2,−1. No fractions are created.

The formula remains exact and user-authored:

No software consciousness assertion, mirror claim, opposition or word-anagram inference is added.

## Category / classification strategy

This is a **multi-index**, not one master renaming. Each app remains referenced by its exact current path. The page reads the repository's existing, provenance-labeled capability atlas inventory and provisional store-discovery manifest; it does not duplicate or overwrite either one.

- **Apple**: up to one primary and one secondary category; game subcategories where relevant, selected for actual app behavior.
- **Google Play**: one app or game category plus up to five approved relevant tags, with independent game genre labels.
- **Dewey Decimal Classification**: the ten verified broad classes, 000 general/computer/information, 100 philosophy/psychology, 200 religion, 300 social sciences, 400 language, 500 science, 600 technology, 700 arts/recreation, 800 literature, 900 history/geography. Broad grouping only.
- **DEO decimal**: *exact owner-supplied name*. An independent, intentionally unassigned set of whole-number positions 0–9. No meaning, acronym expansion, provenance or code has been invented; no equivalence to Dewey or base-12 duodecimal is assumed. The DEO-digit control demonstrates reserved slots while explicitly indicating that the assignments are not defined.
- **Historical lenses**: selected major ideas from colophon/provenance, author/title inventories, printed indexes, Dewey, Library of Congress Classification, Ranganathan's faceted system, machine-readable catalog metadata and contemporary category tags.

History cards are historical *method comparisons*. They do not claim a contemporary app existed or was historically classified in an ancient library.

## Preservation and limitations

1. The original prior root file is copied unmodified to landing/original-chat-landing.html.
2. The existing HOP IN chatbox is accessible at its original route and through a full-page interactive overlay from the new landing.
3. Existing ONE, TABOR FACTORY, app, game, book, and original source modules remain at their existing routes. The new UI only adds a public navigation/catalog layer.
4. Store classifications remain provisional source-labeled descriptions; they do not imply native app packaging, eligibility, approval or actual publication.
5. The Dewey crosswalk is an internal browsing convenience. It does not assign official library call numbers or replace language, author and provenance metadata.
6. Every existing app entrypoint from the snapshot is retained, including items awaiting review; metadata and counts are based on the source-backed repository inventory and may lag concurrent commits until refreshed.
7. No actual original manuscript content, API credentials, or private records are copied into the landing.
8. Animated arrows are reduced when the operating system prefers reduced motion; controls have keyboard operation, visible focus and labeled results.
