The Last Weighing

A Nathan Tabor concept. This finale is a new adaptation.

The first weight Anubis lifted that morning was a dry water jar. A woman had carried it to the hall because the gate below her village had been shut. The clerk had recorded a full delivery. She set the empty jar on the scale.

Karma opened the book to that day. It held a second village, farther down the channel, where the miller had paid the clerk to turn the gate. If the water went back the other way all at once, the workers below would lose the grain they were grinding.

Anubis called the clerk. He set the signed record beside the jar. The clerk reached for the page and the woman stopped his hand. She asked him to write down what he had done where she could see it. He wrote. She waited while he read it aloud.

Karma walked with the gatekeeper to the fork. They cleared the blocked branch and opened the two channels in turn. The mill workers finished the grain that was already in the stones. In the upper village the woman filled her jar. She brought it back and weighed it herself.

The clerk copied the gauge readings into the book, beside his first entry. Anubis weighed the record again. Karma followed both channels to the last house. When they returned to the hall, the truth of the entry and the effect of the correction agreed. The clerk marked the repair with a one.

Outside, the closed gates had left the stepping stones dry. Three neighbors crossed carrying boards for the porch. The first pushed a loose block back into line. The second set a pin. The third hung the swing. When the woman came home, they made room for her. Behind them, the water kept running.
