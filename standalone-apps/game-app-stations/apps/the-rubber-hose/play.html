THE RUBBER HOSE

By Collyn Tabor and Nathan Tabor

WHAT'S BETTER THAN ONE TABOR? TWO!!!

Choose your settings. Press START ROUND, then hold the hose until somebody lets go.

Desktop: hold SPACE to grip, release the key to let go. On mobile: press and hold the blue button, lift your finger to release. Click LET GO for an immediate release. Both sides must start gripping before the round begins.

FULL BOARD. FULL PARTICIPATION. PURE INTENT.

Nathan and Collyn have always been best friends. The 1987 hose incident was one of many adventures, with Collyn laughing through it. This is a fictional, cartoon-physics simulation, not an activity to imitate with real stretched tubing. The virtual contest ends only when someone releases first; waiting increases the possible score and the danger of the computer releasing first.
