The rules

  1. Every player is equal. Nobody permanently holds the storyteller role.
  2. On each turn the storyteller draws one of thirteen system cards and frames a dilemma from the world codex.
  3. The responding hero describes their choice; the storyteller narrates challenges, but never overrides an agreed resolution.
  4. The table selects success, partial success, or setback. Choices permanently change the shared world counters.
  5. The storyteller role passes clockwise after the outcome. If a player passes without a draw, the next storyteller takes over.
  6. A castle can be captured, liberated, or reformed. Dragons and unicorns are equals; nobody is forced into servitude.

Prototype rules and encounters are original design proposals. Source catalog names are preserved; historical interpretations may require verification. The 13 existing system labels are provisional, and do not overwrite your canonical 13.
