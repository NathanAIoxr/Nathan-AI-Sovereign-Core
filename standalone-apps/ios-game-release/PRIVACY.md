# Privacy policy for Tabor iPhone games

The offline iPhone editions of Scrambler, Tabor Yard Arcade, Nathan AI Sudoku Master, and Tabor Retro Lab run their game resources on your device. These builds do not contain accounts, tracking, advertising, or analytics. The apps do not send gameplay, drafts, or device identifiers to a Tabor server.

The apps can store preferences, the last selected activity, game progress, and drafts you choose to save on your device. Scrambler's routes are simulations and the app requests no location access. Sharing an export opens the iOS share sheet and lets you choose where the file goes. App storage can be removed by deleting the application. Copies you exported remain where you sent them.

For privacy questions, use the [Tabor iPhone games support page](./SUPPORT.md).
