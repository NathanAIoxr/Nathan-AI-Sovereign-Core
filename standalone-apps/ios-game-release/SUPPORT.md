# Tabor iPhone games support

Use **Library** to switch activities and **Continue** to reopen the last one. The iPhone editions work with bundled game files. If a game fails to open, choose **Reload game**. Game progress and preferences are local to the device.

For help with Scrambler, Tabor Yard Arcade, Nathan AI Sudoku Master, or Tabor Retro Lab, [open a support issue](https://github.com/NathanAIoxr/Nathan-AI-Sovereign-Core/issues/new). Include the app name, the version, the iPhone model, iOS version, and the steps to see the problem. Keep personal details and saved route files out of public issues.
