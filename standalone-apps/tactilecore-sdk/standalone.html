How this experimental controller works

Touch contacts are tracked using stable pointer IDs. A bottommost contact is estimated as the anchor when multiple touches are present. Lifting the anchor opens a short 15–45 ms release window; a 14 ms guard excludes noisy lift-off position spikes. Touch movement sets arcade-game power, aim, and spin. With permission, recent device gyroscope readings can modify touch results or drive the new virtual air mode. Air motion is armed only while holding an explicit control; never let go of your device. Air patterns use projected angular movement, not true XYZ position.

Finger identities, actual fingertip pressure, guaranteed latency, professional dart aerodynamics, and real-world collision physics are not established by this demo. Calibration and hardware trials are necessary.
