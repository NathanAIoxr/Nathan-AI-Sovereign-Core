## Shared controls to reconcile

1. Physical mm mapping **must use calibrated scale** where hardware PPI is unavailable; `devicePixelRatio` is not proof of physical PPI.
2. 50 ms touch history; pre-lift 14–16 ms anti-twitch release lock; multi-touch index/middle/thumb staged release.
3. Velocity-aware 1-Euro smoothing; local adaptive grip and reset.
4. Outer 5% bezel safe cancel; no throw counted.
5. Portrait and landscape rendering.
6. Seeded wind/glare/bird environmental conditions, at least 1.5s visual telegraph, post-throw telemetry with playful excuses.
7. On-device motion sensors only with permission; no physical release of handheld phone.
8. Gesture shapes: triangle, circle, wave, figure eight, swish/flick, arches, W/M/R/4 and 2/3/4 conducting patterns. These are original generic mechanics, not copies of licensed theme-park audiovisual assets.
