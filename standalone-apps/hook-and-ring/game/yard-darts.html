YARD DARTS — THE GAME

HOW TO PLAY

Welcome to YARD DARTS — THE GAME. This is a virtual arcade competition: throw bright digital lawn darts into a backyard ring.

  • TAP anywhere on the field for a quick throw, or DRAG AND RELEASE to fine-tune your aim.
  • Use ← ↑ → ↓ to move the crosshair and SPACE or the THROW! button to shoot.
  • 5 points for a bullseye, 3 for the inner zone, 1 for the outer ring.
  • WIND nudges the dart sideways, so aim a little against the arrow.
  • SOLO: 5 rounds × 3 darts. Chase your personal best.
  • VS COMPUTER or 2 PLAYERS: 3 rounds, 3 darts each per round. Highest total wins.

Everything happens on screen. No physical projectiles are involved. DON’T PLAY THIS FOR REAL: real metal-tipped yard darts can cause serious or fatal injuries.
