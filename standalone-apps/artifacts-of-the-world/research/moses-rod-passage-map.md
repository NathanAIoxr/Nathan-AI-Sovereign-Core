# Staff of Moses — passage and tradition separation

Status: research dossier; artifact identity and custody **unverified**. The author's exact words remain in [original.txt](../original.txt) without modification.

## KJV textual chronology (references, not archaeological verification)

| Passage | What the passage supports | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Exodus 4:2–5 | Moses holds a rod, casts it down, and it becomes a serpent | No Eden provenance or surviving-object custody |
| Exodus 4:17, 4:20 | Rod used for signs and called the rod of God | No physical inventory after antiquity |
| Exodus 7:8–12 | Aaron's rod becomes a serpent before Pharaoh | Does not identify Aaron's rod as Moses's rod |
| Exodus 14:16 | Moses told to stretch his hand and rod over the sea | No Eden chain of custody |
| Exodus 17:5–6 | Moses directed to take the rod and strike the rock | No surviving museum object |
| Numbers 20:7–11 | Moses takes the rod from before the LORD and strikes the rock | The narrative does not prove it is the same physical rod at every event |
| Hebrews 9:4 | Aaron's rod that budded is described with the ark | Does not establish a present-day artifact location |

Primary textual source: [King James Version, Exodus 4](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Exodus%204&version=KJV), [Exodus 7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Exodus%207&version=KJV), [Exodus 14](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Exodus%2014&version=KJV), [Exodus 17](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Exodus%2017&version=KJV), [Numbers 20](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Numbers%2020&version=KJV), [Hebrews 9](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Hebrews%209&version=KJV). These support *textual references only*.

## Eden connection — later interpretive tradition

The statement that the staff came from Eden is **not made in these KJV passages**. It is associated with later Jewish interpretive traditions, including *Pirkei de-Rabbi Eliezer*, chapter 40 (the staff's transmission). This is a research lead, **not a verified citation of a specific manuscript or edition**; exact wording, edition, date and chain of transmission remain open pending primary-source examination.

## Unresolved

The staff's identity, material, present location, chain of custody, any equivalence between Moses's and Aaron's rods, and the earliest dated manuscript witness to the Eden narrative remain unverified. No archaeological authenticity or ownership claim is made.
