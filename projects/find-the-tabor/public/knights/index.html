  1. Tradition

    1846 · Antecedent reported by Order histories

    Museum historians report that accounts of the Order connect it to an antislavery Order of Twelve formed in 1846. Treat the claimed organizational continuity as a separate question and seek contemporary records.

    • Scottish Rite Masonic Museum & Library · International Order of Twelve
  2. Documented

    1872 · Moses Dickson and the International Order of Twelve

    The African American fraternal organization was founded at Independence, Missouri. Moses Dickson’s 1891 Manual provides the Order’s own account, rules and vocabulary.

    • Scottish Rite Masonic Museum & Library · International Order of Twelve
    • Moses Dickson · 1891 Manual, public-domain scan
    • Moses Dickson · 1891 Manual, searchable record
  3. Documented

    12 August 1872 · the Order’s official date

    In the 1891 Manual, Dickson distinguishes the modern beneficial order from earlier organizations and identifies three Tabernacles and two Temples at the organization meeting in Independence. The 1872 date is explicitly adopted for official papers and observance.

    • Moses Dickson · 1891 Manual, searchable scan
    • Scottish Rite Masonic Museum & Library · International Order of Twelve
  4. Documented

    Temples · Uniform Rank · Palatiums and Tents

    The Manual identifies Temple and Uniform Rank terminology alongside shared or other divisions, such as Palatiums and Tents. Use the constitution and rank sections when assigning titles. The motto In Solo Deo Salus appears in the Manual.

    • Moses Dickson · 1891 Manual, public-domain scan
    • Moses Dickson · 1891 Manual, searchable record
  5. Documented

    1942–1982 · Taborian Hospital

    Mississippi's historical inventory records that the Knights and Daughters of Tabor operated the hospital in Mound Bayou. It opened in February 1942 and closed in 1982.

    • Mississippi Department of Archives & History · Taborian Hospital
  6. Open

    Membership, overseas chapters and badge variants

    Audit claimed membership totals, numbers of states, charters abroad, colors, and the interpretation of 333 and 777 against a dated emblem or order document. Keep each claim tied to a specific edition, year or jurisdiction.
