  1. Documented

    1420 · Tábor founded in Bohemia

    Radical Hussites established Tábor and named the settlement after biblical Mount Tabor. The official city account records its Hussite origin and later church dedicated to the Transfiguration.

  2. Documented

    Scripture, worship and community

    Sources describe radical Hussite reforms, a strong scriptural basis, changes to worship and an early communal experiment. Track individual articles and factions before generalizing the reforms to every resident or to the full history of Tábor.

  3. Documented

    1434 · Battle of Lipany

    A coalition including Catholic and moderate Utraquist forces defeated the radical forces. Treat Lipany as a dated military and political event within a longer institutional history.

    • New Catholic Encyclopedia · Taborites
  4. Documented

    1452 · George of Poděbrady takes Tábor

    Tábor remained a center after Lipany. George of Poděbrady took the town in 1452. The military defeat and later loss of autonomy belong on separate timeline entries.
