  3. Documented

    1891 · Daughters’ emblems and the Grand Queen Mother

    The Manual illustrates crescent and other emblems bearing 333 before the Tabernacle installation ceremony. It also directly invokes Mount Tabor in the instructions to the Grand Queen Mother. This supports a biblical allusion in the order’s own rituals.
