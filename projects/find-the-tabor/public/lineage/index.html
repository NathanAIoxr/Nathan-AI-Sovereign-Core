    Tabor · Tabour · Tabur · Taber · Thabour

    The English name has a documented occupational or nickname explanation from the small drum. Middle English and Old French dictionaries record several drum spellings. This is one origin of the English surname. Search spelling and family relationship independently.

  4. Documented

    Tábor · Tabor · Taborite

    Tábor is a documented Czech place-based surname origin. The Czech town took its name from biblical Mount Tabor. The surname dictionary also lists a Bohemian Jewish habitational origin and South Slavic and Polish camp or fortification explanations.

    • City of Tábor · Official history
