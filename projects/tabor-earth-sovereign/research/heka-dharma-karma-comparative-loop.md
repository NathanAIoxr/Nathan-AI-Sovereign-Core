# Comparative Framework: Heka / Akeh / Akh, Dharma, Karma, Ma'at, Hózhǫ́
Status: authored comparative research, not an assertion of shared etymology.

## Source-preserving structure
- HEKA (Egyptian ḥkꜣ): historical Egyptian concept of effective/magical power; compare Sia and Hu as distinct Egyptian concepts.
- AKEH: exact reversal of Latin-letter HEKA; user-proposed structural mirror.
- AKH (Egyptian ꜣḫ): attested concept of an effective/transfigured spirit. AKEH is not a verified transliteration of ꜣḫ.
- DHARMA: Sanskrit dhṛ ('hold, support'), with varied meanings including duty, teaching, law and sustaining order.
- KARMA: Sanskrit kṛ ('do, make'); action and its consequences across traditions, not a promise of immediate measurable returns.
- MA'AT: Egyptian concept of truth, justice and order.
- HÓZHǪ́: Diné concept often associated with harmony, beauty and right relations; community-specific meanings must not be collapsed into a generic synonym for dharma.
