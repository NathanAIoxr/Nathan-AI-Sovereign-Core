# Tabor Earth: DINE / ENID Connected Evidence Graph
Date: 2026-11-19
Status: source-backed research synthesis, not deployed Cloudflare data

## Observed connection
DINE reversed is ENID (exact four-letter reversal). Diné is a Navajo self-designation meaning "the People"; diacritics and linguistic identity must be retained.

## Evidence graph
- DINE -> ENID: exact spelling reversal, independently noted in The Oklahoma News, February 3, 1939. https://gateway.okhistory.org/ark:/67531/metadc2014718/m1/1/
- Enid (given name) -> Middle Welsh eneit / Welsh enaid (soul); breathing-related PIE etymology: https://www.etymonline.com/word/Enid and https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/enaid
- Enid (literary) -> Geraint and Enid / Arthurian literary tradition -> Tennyson's Idylls of the King.
- Tennyson Enid -> Enid Station: leading railway naming explanation, circa 1889: https://www.okhistory.org/publications/enc/entry.php?entry=EN006
- DINE reversal -> Enid Oklahoma: documented as alternative naming tradition; historical documentation of the legend does not establish that the legend caused the name: https://visitenid.org/media/
- Enid Oklahoma -> Cherokee Outlet -> Cherokee treaty rights, Indigenous nations and the 1893 land run: https://www.okhistory.org/shpo/docs/ILSofEnidDT.pdf
- Diné -> Southern Athabaskan language and ancestral history; NOT established as a genetic or linguistic connection to Welsh by reversal alone.
