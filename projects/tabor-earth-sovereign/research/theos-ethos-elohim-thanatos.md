# THEOS / ETHOS / ELOHIM / THANATOS — Tabor Concordance

## KJV textual bridge
John 10:34 (KJV): "Jesus answered them, Is it not written in your law, I said, Ye are gods?"
Psalm 82:6 (KJV): "I have said, Ye are gods; and all of you are children of the most High."
John 10:34 directly cites Psalm 82:6.

## Separate lexical records
- Greek θεοί (theoi): nominative plural of θεός (theos), Strong's G2316, used in John 10:34.
- Hebrew אֱלֹהִים (elohim), Strong's H430, in Psalm 82:6; interpretations of its referents are debated.
- Greek θεός (theos): god/deity.
- Greek ἦθος (ēthos): character, custom; the source of English ethics. Not the same Greek root as theos.
- THEOS ↔ ETHOS: exact anagram in five-letter Latin transliteration (E,H,O,S,T).
- Greek θάνατος (thanatos): death.
- Thanos: modern Greek short form of Athanasios, whose name means immortal; not identical to the lexical noun thanatos.
- Ethos Life: contemporary brand name; no historical Greek root connection beyond its use of the word ethos.
