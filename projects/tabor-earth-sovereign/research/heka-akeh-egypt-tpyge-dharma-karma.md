# Heka ↔ Akeh, Dharma, Karma and EGYPT ↔ TPYGE
Research and software activation specification.

## Exact reversals
EGYPT -> TPYGE; HEKA -> AKEH. These are character reversals, not automatically historical etymologies.
TPYGE begins with T-P-Y; it does not, by itself, establish an etymological connection to Lakota thípi/tipi. The Greek word πυγή (pygē, buttocks/rump) is a separate lexical record, not a validated derivation of TPYGE. EGYPT's history through Greek Aigyptos and Egyptian ḥwt-kꜣ-ptḥ requires its own sources.

## Independent conceptual modules
- Heka: ancient Egyptian concept of efficacious magical power, requiring source-specific interpretations.
- Akeh: reversal/search node, not a verified ancient term.
- Dharma: distinct South Asian philosophical and religious concept; interpretations vary by tradition.
- Karma: distinct concept of action and consequence; never a license for software to declare guilt, innocence, or moral worth.
- Egypt: separate history, geography, language, and place-name corpus.
- Tipi: distinct Siouan language and cultural housing research, requiring Indigenous-language source review.
