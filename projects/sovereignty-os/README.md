# Sovereignty OS — THEOS / THE OS Design Framework

## Author's proposed transformation
"THEOS = the OS … the OS that runs the matrix’s, so take that and change out that OS to Soverignty - and deliver the world that has always been promised, but never delivered"

Preserve this as user-authored conceptual framing. THEOS -> THE OS is a modern segmentation/metaphor, not the historical etymology of Greek θεός.

## Purpose
Build consent-based, independently deployable applications that protect innocence, respect personal agency, and keep accountable records of actual operations. No claim of controlling a literal global matrix or guaranteeing a transformed world.

## Immutable design principles
1. Protection of innocence and vulnerable people.
2. Personal sovereignty: identity, privacy, portability, deletion and informed consent.
3. Least privilege; no master account with unlimited access.
4. Exact source wording and provenance, separate from interpretation.
5. Evidence-backed verification and transparent uncertainty.
6. No autonomous surveillance, harassment, or unauthorized access.
7. Reversible changes, auditable execution, human approval for consequential actions.
8. Independent applications and data boundaries; share only permissioned references.

## Four-phase workflow
WORD: specify and preserve source and intent.
ORDER: validate authority, consent, safety and schema.
ACTION: execute authorized, scoped operations.
VERIFY: log outcomes, inspect failures and roll back when necessary.

## Sovereign modules
- Concordance: language, roots, reversals, textual comparisons.
- Earth Atlas: public archaeological, historical and geographic sources.
- Diné Research: community-sensitive cultural and language research.
- Find Your Name: public etymology and user-controlled searches.
- Find Your Real Ancestry: private-by-default genealogy.
- Innocence Guardian: safeguarding, minimal data, reporting resources.
- Sovereignty Core: shared auth contracts, audit schema, permission checks, deployment manifests.
