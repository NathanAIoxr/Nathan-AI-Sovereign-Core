Pass means the current invariant was observed. Ready means the feature exists but has not been exercised in this session. These live checks are distinct from the automated regression suite.

The supplied reverse endpoints are Z: Zero Clearing and A: Absolute Arrival. The other 24 reverse names are explicitly marked implementation-defined verification labels.

Standard Match-3 process

Read input → validate neighbors → swap → find runs → rollback or commit → create powers → activate chains → clear → score → gravity → refill → cascade → check outcome → recover a dead board.

Only adjacent orthogonal swaps are legal. A normal swap must make a match of at least three. Invalid swaps restore the exact board and do not spend a move. Powers can be activated by swapping them with any neighbor.

Reach 1,200 points in 30 accepted moves. Each cleared tile earns 10 × cascade depth; each activated power adds 25 points. Four-runs earn line powers, intersections earn blasts, and five-runs earn spectra. One power is created per connected matching component, protected during its creation wave. Existing powers trigger when cleared and can trigger other powers.

Power+power swaps combine their effects. Spectrum+power converts every tile of the other gem’s symbol into that power. Newly earned powers are free; there are no purchases or consumable paid hints.

Cascades are bounded to 32 resolution waves. The final board is stabilized and recovered without awarding unperformed clears. Recovery preserves score, move count and tile identities; it shuffles first, and can regenerate colors if repeated shuffles cannot produce a stable playable board.

Click/tap two gems, drag or swipe toward a neighbor, or focus a gem and use Space/Enter. Arrow keys move focus; Home/End jumps to the first/last gem. Escape or selecting the same gem again clears the selection. Input is locked during animation. Reduced-motion users get the same committed result without animated delays. Sound is off until explicitly enabled.

The inspector reads current state only. Switching to reverse Z–A never undoes play or clears saved data. Detailed source and executable tests are included in the private v025 package.
