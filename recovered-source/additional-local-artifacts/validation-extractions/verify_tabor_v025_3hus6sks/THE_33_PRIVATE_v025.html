    Campaign rules, victory and defeat

    Win: complete the stage’s board objective and reduce guardian health to zero. Completing only one keeps the campaign running. Mastery contracts are optional extra achievements. They count opening clears unless they name a board-state objective.

    Lose: your health reaches zero, the campaign player-turn limit expires, or a tile enters the spiral’s entropy void. Other stages have no real-time limit.

    Contested board: the guardian announces an opening from the visible board. Ancient guardians commit; Industrial guardians adapt at 70% attack strength if disrupted. Grandmaster guardians and Future bosses in their last phase hunt a fresh visible match. Guardians can read current concealed memory symbols, just as you can with free Study; they never read future refills. Guardian turns advance shared-board mechanisms; Concordia uses echo strikes to preserve alternating human turns.

    Objective ownership: your value/target points belong to you. Rooms, balanced sectors and seals are board-state objectives: a guardian clearing those also advances that shared objective. Your score and focus are never increased by a guardian move.

    Damage: a productive turn deals 6 + floor(√score gained), capped at 24 before stance, forecast, gambit and boss shield. Precision: ×1.3 for an opening run of 4+, otherwise ×0.9. Cascade: ×1.25 when gained score exceeds the opening clear’s baseline (10 per tile in stages 6–33; 100 per structural group in stages 1–5), otherwise ×0.8. Fortify: ×0.75 damage and +5 shield. Productive turns gain 1 focus, plus 1 for an opening run of 4+ (cap 6).

    Temperaments: Collectors take large clears. Saboteurs target your objectives and contracts. Architects favor long runs and simultaneous matches. Echo guardians chase your last cleared symbol: use a sacrifice to bait their preference.

    Guardian attack: 4 + min(12, opening tiles ÷ 2), scaled by difficulty, adaptation and Gambit, then rounded. Shield absorbs damage first. Explorer guardians act every second player turn; other difficulties act each turn. A guardian without a valid match regroups.

    Bosses: stages 11, 22 and 33 have extra health and three phases. A visible shield halves damage when the opening clear’s dominant symbol matches it. The shield changes with the boss phase.
