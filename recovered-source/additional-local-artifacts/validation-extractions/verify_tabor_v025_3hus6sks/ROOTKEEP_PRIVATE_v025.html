Field guide · lane combat, elements, mana and progression

Read the five lanes.

The board has seven rows and five columns. Swap orthogonal neighbors to match three or more. Invalid swaps spend nothing. Tiles use the attack of the living hero in their column, then apply their own elemental counter. A fallen hero’s lane deals no tile damage.

Ember → Grove → Tide → Ember. Strong counters deal 2× damage; reverse counters deal ½×. Aether and Umbra deal 2× to each other. Other pairings deal normal damage.

A lane without a direct living enemy ghosts: its shot reroutes to the nearest living enemy, with ties going left. A tile matching its lane hero’s element gives one mana charge; a ghost gives two. Other colors still attack but give no mana. Charges are stored in half-units so 6.5/13.5 thresholds remain exact.

Turn power into a plan.

Mana speed thresholds are Very Fast 6.5, Fast 8, Average 10, Slow 12, Very Slow 13.5 charges. A full hero can use a skill once per board move. Select an enemy to target a skill; tile attacks follow their lanes. One item is allowed between accepted moves.

Enemies announce their next action. Guards reduce damage by 40%; a guard break increases it by 35% for three moves. Cascades increase tile damage by 20% per wave, capped at 2×; no more than 24 cascade waves resolve. Dead boards recover freely. Clearing a wave prevents its defeated enemies from attacking and does not give fresh enemies a free strike.

Buildings upgrade immediately. Rootkeep caps building levels; the Storehouse caps food and iron. Production accumulates up to 12 hours, with fractional units carried forward. Collect before upgrading. First clears earn larger rewards and relics; replays still earn food, iron and insight. Storage overflow goes into the 48-hour inbox and is shown in the victory receipt. Production overflow is retained there too.
