- A spacetime-shape equation is not a constructed faster-than-light craft. Ideal parallel-plate vacuum energy cannot simply supply the stress-energy of a gravitational drive.
- A static plasma pinch or cutoff does not create a real portable blade or all-purpose protective shield.
- Frame-dragging and force accounting do not provide antigravity or a controllable hovering mechanism.
- A bit-erasure bound, tunneling model or synthetic membrane response is not a neural upload system, skill injection mechanism or approved biomedical interface.
- A synthetic correlation and periodic signal model is not telepathy, mind reading or human teleportation.
- Several inverse gates have more than one possible solution. The engine explicitly chooses the upper z-score branch, positive amplitudes, positive Gaussian arm or low-probability-half entropy as relevant.

This software never controls electrodes on people, high-current plasma, dangerous coils, disruptive electromagnetic pulses or deployable weapons.
