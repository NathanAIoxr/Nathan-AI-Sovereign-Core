# THE MIDDLE C — AXIS OF INFINITY
**Creator/concept:** Nathan Tabor. **Parent:** TABOR 1-2-3 FACTORY. **Status:** original functional static browser word game with deterministic levels and local progress.

## Play
[Launch the playable game](./index.html). No payment, account, or live AI is needed.

The player alternates three challenge families: (1) **literal reversible mirror**: spell words backwards without adding or moving letters; (2) **conventional meaning**: choose dictionary-style meanings instead of unverified cryptic claims; and (3) **ancient-number connections**: select the historically or mathematically relevant reference while inspecting attribution and limitations. Every correctly answered puzzle scores 100 plus a streak bonus, and every attempt enables the next level. The generator repeats a curated word/reference pool across deterministic level indexes, creating **up to 1 billion indexed levels**, not a claim of literally infinite original content. The level data grows as source records are approved.
