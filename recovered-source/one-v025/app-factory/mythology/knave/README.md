## Preserve distinctions

- Cultural references retain nation, language, region and original qualifications. Yaya is explicitly generally not classed as female; Rainbow Serpent gender differs by community. Awilix, Chasca and Jaci retain classification uncertainty.
- Names, maternal titles, collective beings and linguistic elements are not all interchangeable goddesses. Shared meanings do not establish cognacy.
- `fictional_character` / `original_fiction` labels identify Zeusara through Abaddona as original KNAVE identities; the supplied inspiration/function mapping is not a historical counterpart claim.
- `cultural_reference` and `linguistic_term` carry `owner_supplied_compilation` and verification-pending status. No independent historical fact-checking has been performed.
- `earliestKnownSource: null` means no earliest attestation has been established. Original-source links are retained; image-proxy URLs are not downloaded, licensed art or independently verified citations.
- Australian source restrictions and living Indigenous contexts remain visible in full-section view. Do not invent or expand ceremonial details.
