Creator / curator: Nathan Tabor. [Open archive](./index.html) · [Full supplied source](./source.md) · [Structured data](./atlas.json) · [Greek atlas](../greek/index.html)

The entire October 14 attachment is preserved byte-for-byte in `source.md`, including narrative, qualifications, comparison maps, equipment, original spellings and source suggestions. Its SHA-256 digest is recorded in `atlas.json`. The searchable browser module indexes 293 source table rows and 35 complete sections. Rows include beings, vocabulary, places, titles, historical stages, comparison concepts and source lists; these are not 293 unique gods.

Eight Indigenous tradition groups remain separately identified: Lakota/Dakota, Anishinaabe/Ojibwe, Diné, Haudenosaunee, Hopi, Mexica/Nahua, K’iche’ Maya and Andean traditions. Zoroastrian ensembles and the twelve supplied examples from the 101-name devotional collection retain their original context. Modern Satanist movements remain distinct from biblical adversarial figures.

## Data contract

Schema: `tabor.ancient-roots.archive.v1`. Stable source-order IDs `ADR-0001` onward; independent section IDs `ARS-001` onward. Each row preserves original column labels/values, family, source-section context and exact-source locator. `earliestKnownSource: null` means not researched; original-script spellings, etymologies and roles are retained only where supplied. Do not invent missing Strong’s identifiers, ancient equivalents or citations. All rows carry `owner_supplied_compilation` evidence status and independent-verification-pending labels. Domain favicons and copied citation artifacts are part of the original, not verified claim-level citations.

Historical claims were imported, not independently fact-checked. Shared attributes do not prove shared origins or identical divinities. Narrative qualifications remain accessible through complete-section view. Names remain source-specific, with no forced same-as merging.
