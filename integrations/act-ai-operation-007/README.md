# ACT AI — Operation 007: Protect Innocence

Purpose: document publicly available infrastructure information and credible allegations of child sexual abuse, preserve original sources, and support appropriate reporting. No unauthorized access, disruption, credential collection, malware, or private-data publication.

## Status
- Vatican infrastructure compromise: **not established**

## Process
1. Record an exact public source URL, retrieval date, and quoted excerpt within fair-use limits.
2. Classify each assertion as documented fact, attributed allegation, disputed, or unknown.
3. Preserve original material and its cryptographic digest; store sensitive evidence only through authorized, access-controlled channels.
4. Record public DNS information only from lawful sources, without scanning private systems or attempting access.
5. Escalate suspected child sexual exploitation to appropriate law enforcement or child-protection reporting channels. Do not upload or redistribute abusive imagery.
6. Correct unsupported claims while retaining original evidence and attribution.

## Protection
Do not publish victim identities, private addresses, credentials, or unsupported accusations. Never conflate public DNS metadata with proof of abuse. This repository is not an attack tool.
