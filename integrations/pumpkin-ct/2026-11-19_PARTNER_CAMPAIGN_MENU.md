# Pumpkin Patch Fundraisers | Year-round partner menu for Collyn

**Partner conversation.** Since we already deliver your pumpkins, which other fundraisers would you like to offer this year? Would your organization sell Christmas trees and wreaths, spring flowers, summer produce, or take-home gift wrap for more than one holiday?

Pumpkin Patch Fundraisers started in 1974 with one church and reports working with more than 1,000 organizations, including churches, schools and civic groups. The company describes its pumpkin arrangement as a proceeds-sharing model in which it covers growing, harvesting and shipping and takes on spoilage and theft risk. Existing contacts and fall renewals give the company a place to ask what partners want. Terms for trees, plants, food and other product lines are campaign decisions. [Pumpkin Patch Fundraisers company history](https://www.pumpkinsusa.com/about-us/)

## Fulfillment and launch mechanics

- **Print on demand.** Offer an 8–12 page kitchen-table catalog with item numbers, QR links and a tear-off order form. Give each participating organization a mobile storefront and optional supporter links. Route eligible files to a print vendor once an order is confirmed. Compare the full landed cost of home shipping and consolidated pickup.
- **Demand-triggered bulk.** Open a preorder window by location. Set the minimum volume, order deadline, delivery window, vendor commitment and refund or rain-check terms. At cutoff, release a purchase against confirmed demand when supplier and route capacity both clear. Record what happens if the minimum is missed, goods arrive damaged, or weather stops unloading.
- **Pricing and tracking.** Compare sales less landed goods, handling, delivery, payment fees and expected refunds before setting the partner share. Record paper and online orders under the same campaign. Track organization proceeds, sponsor contributions, credits and refunds individually. Keep optional family spending caps, volunteer hours and campaign rollover available for review.
- **Delivery.** Collyn’s driver concept covers parked or hands-free assistance, dispatch contact, pickup and drop-off notes, counts, weights, discrepancies, signatures and invoicing. Track capacity and unloading for each route. See the [reuse-first build queue](REUSE_FIRST_BUILD_QUEUE.md) for the independent Pumpkin Driver, Pumpkin Volunteer and Pumpkin Network Console work.
- **Church print and digital.** Pair a printed weekly bulletin with a hosted site update, RSVP, potluck signup and campaign progress. CT Inc. Church OS can publish the approved campaign to bulletin, site and livestream once the integration is available.
- **Books, games and giving.** Record CPT as Chestnuts, Pumpkin and Potatoes, with chicken or turkey variants. Keep Diné with Pumpkins, Three Sisters seed and story kits, and cultural attribution in the editorial workstream. Offer Scramble, soft-target Yard Darts alternatives, Cornhole, Sorting Hats and Huffettes, and Thanksgiving hospitality as optional campaign companions. Handling Life, The Daily Cup of Hope, The Daily Plate of Love and ChristmasToyDrive.com are proposed recipients or collaborators. Identify the designated recipient and approved terms before offering a contribution.

A pilot can enroll one existing partner in one bulk drop and one catalog campaign. Compare participating partners, order conversion, landed cost, contribution per order, on-time fulfillment, refunds, leftover units and coordinator time with the partner’s previous fall campaign. Collyn can choose or revise the campaign, partner and pilot dates.
