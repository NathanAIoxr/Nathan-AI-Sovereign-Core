## Separate independent products, shared opt-in links
1. **Diné with Pumpkins**: independent cookbook/website/app, recipes and garden stories, pumpkin/squash/corn/beans, historical and contemporary foodways. Do not claim recipes are traditional Diné without provenance and permission.
2. **Three Sisters learning**: corn/beans/squash planting, companion cultivation, climate and ecology, food science, gardens, seeds and hands-on kits. Explain that Three Sisters methods occur across multiple Indigenous traditions; do not assign exclusive origin.
3. **101 Lost Pumpkin Recipes**: independent historical recipe research and cookbook; label origin, time, source, adaptation, and verified vs speculative.
4. **CPT**: Chestnuts, Pumpkin and Potatoes, independent recipe series; optional chicken/turkey variants from prior brainstorm.
5. **Diné history & education**: Diné identity, Dinétah, place names, Navajo Nation governance and contemporary life, land and agriculture, forced relocation/Long Walk (1864), Bosque Redondo imprisonment, 1868 treaty and return, boarding-school history, language resilience, environmental stewardship, modern artists/farmers/educators. Require reviewed primary sources and Diné scholars' input. No claim of unanimous experiences.
6. **Diné language and story**: Diné Bizaad pronunciation, approved vocabulary and audio, living language and literature. Respect community restrictions on ceremonial/sacred stories and images.
7. **Farm-to-patch learning**: map the New Mexico farm-to-church logistics route, grower interviews with consent, harvest cycle, water and soil, seed care, fair procurement and verified farm attribution. Do not reveal private grower locations or proprietary supply contracts.
8. **Children's books & activity packs**: age-appropriate agriculture, food, history, reading, coloring, garden journaling and QR lessons; Indigenous authors/illustrators compensated and credited. Youth privacy and guardian consent.
9. **Museums / oral histories / timelines**: evidence-linked archival material, family oral histories by permission, source metadata, correction process, and opt-out/removal rights.
10. **Games and community events**: seasonal planting and cooking challenges, harvest festivals, recipe exchanges, safe lawn games, sorting teams, and optional educational scavenger hunts. Keep independently branded games separate.
11. **Church and nonprofit fundraisers**: opt-in books, classroom packs, seed kits, recipe bundles, printed take-home catalogs, digital QR links, direct shipments or threshold bulk drops. Disclose where proceeds go and pay Indigenous contributors under agreed terms.
12. **Extend beyond pumpkins**: adaptable templates for other crops, Indigenous communities and regional food histories ONLY through locally governed partnerships, verified sourcing and cultural permissions.

## Required data model
Each cultural item should have: content_id, product_id (optional), title, category, community attribution, contributor name or anonymous designation, provenance/source URL or archive reference, verified date, review status, permission scope, license, compensation terms, restrictions, language, intended age group, sensitive/sacred flag, correction/contact route, withdrawal status, and tenant visibility.
Each recipe: ingredients, quantities, method, allergens, cultural attribution and source, adaptations and safety notes.
Each fundraising item: SKU, fulfillment mode, vendor, landed cost, approved tier, church payout/credit, Indigenous partner payment, refund terms and availability.

## Implementation gates
- Obtain written permission before using tribal seals, marks, copyrighted art, photos, audio, oral histories, sacred narratives or names implying endorsement.
- Establish consultation/paid editorial review with Diné historians, educators, growers and artists; document approval and corrections.
- Distinguish user-reported New Mexico/Navajo Nation sourcing from independently verified supplier contracts and exact origin.
- No automatic sale or publication of contributed cultural material.
- Protect minors' art, identities, and personal data.
- Keep source text immutable; normalized descriptions and implementation layers separate.
- Use tenant-scoped permissions and editorial audit logs; content defaults to unpublished.
- Do not treat these plans as a live Cloudflare deployment or a completed catalog.
